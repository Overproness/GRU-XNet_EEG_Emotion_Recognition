# Cover letter for author review — Journal of Medical and Biological Engineering

12 October 2026. **Draft; not sent.** Complete corresponding-author details and truthful final declarations before submission.

Dear Editors,

We submit for consideration as an Original Article our manuscript, “Context Controls and EEG Pairing in Retrospective Valence Prediction: An Exploratory FACED Study,” by Muhammad Muntazar and Muhammad Wasif Shakeel.

The study evaluates whether tested EEG prediction recipes improve individual retrospective valence prediction beyond available elicitation metadata. On FACED, development-validation people are separate from source people, and each rotation contains both familiar and catalogue-title-held materials. A source baseline audit identifies an information mismatch when withholding the only title representing a category. Category-supported selection, a fixed-alpha matched context comparison and constrained EEG pairing diagnostics make the information conditions explicit. Every acquisition failure remains in the denominator through declared fallback.

The category prior is competitive with the tested EEG models. Spectral/context prediction shows a small, inconsistent average improvement; descriptive unfamiliar-title intervals include zero. EEGNet results are less consistent and source-learning flags limit negative interpretation. All fifteen historical conditions, adverse familiar results and all pairing panels are preserved in the supplement. The paper reports a reproducible empirical evaluation, not a novel architecture, a first stimulus-confound analysis, an equivalence result or absence of useful EEG information. It clearly states its single-corpus, adaptive-development and limited-material boundaries.

We believe the biomedical signal-evaluation question and reproducible acquisition-aware controls may be relevant to the journal's readership. The source, anonymous development predictions and audit records are public; original EEG/rating archives are not redistributed. No independent confirmation is claimed, and reserved outcomes remain sealed. The manuscript uses the Original Article structure and checked abstract, word and display limits. We seek the subscription publication route.

Before sending, the authors will supply and verify author consent, originality/concurrent-submission information, funding, competing interests, contributions, relevant institutional requirements and the AI-assistance disclosure. This draft does not certify those facts. The corresponding author is to be designated by the authors.
