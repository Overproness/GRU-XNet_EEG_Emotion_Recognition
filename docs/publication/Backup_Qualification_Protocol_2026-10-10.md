## Gates before any fitting

1. Authenticate release and file lineage, exact header calibration/reference/channel order, raw-versus-derivative processing and sample-time alignment. Verify numbered rating semantics and explicit material/execution joins through author documentation. A curator's inferred array order is a candidate until corroborated.
2. Audit stimulus families across development/confirmation materials and any source corpora. Audit actual pretraining membership: models trained on FACED, including emotional pretraining that uses FACED, cannot establish an untouched FACED confirmation claim. Use scratch or demonstrably compatible pretraining for that claim; preserve unknown membership as unresolved.
3. Complete and push a material reservation, exclusion rules, target and observation interval. Keep participant self-report and assigned elicitation targets distinct. Numerical ratings may be opened only after these gates and the separate development-access declaration. Confirmation remains sealed.
4. Inspect development support only under the declared target, without changing confirmation allocation, thresholds or window definitions. Evaluate whether a feasible question offers a substantive distinction from prior work. Present the resulting question and experiment budget to the author before adopting it. Archive the current manuscript immediately before an approved change.

## Prepared evaluation design, conditional on those gates

The target is an explicit pending decision: continuous self-reported valence, predeclared binary self-report, or assigned emotion category. No threshold is selected here. Continuous outcomes retain their original scale; categories and subjective reports are separate estimands. The eventual primary target and primary metric must be frozen before opening development ratings.

Use participant- and material-separated development validation with familiar-material and unseen-material arms. Source preparation first selects permitted identities, then reads only permitted labels. It must be invariant to deletion/poisoning of excluded rows and labels. Source-fitted scaling, imputation, channel rejection and feature reduction cannot use validation or confirmation. No overlapping windows cross trial boundaries; one original trial has one evaluation weight. Avoid fitting ICA across protected trials, or explicitly restrict any outcome-blind recording adaptation to a declared deployment setting with matched controls.

Begin with a uniform/constant source target baseline, a source-learned material or elicitation prior where the material is legitimately familiar, a regularized spectral model and a compact neural reference. A familiar video-ID prior has no learned identity lookup for an unseen video; use a separately declared source-only fallback or permissible stimulus descriptors. Scratch and permitted pretrained representations receive matched source selection and resource budgets. A source-learning/capacity diagnostic is required before interpreting a weak neural result. No new large encoder grid is queued.

Select hyperparameters/checkpoints using source-only inner validation. Compare EEG-only, context-only and EEG-plus-context on exactly matched trial identities. If context is available only retrospectively, state that limitation. For individual self-report, assess whether EEG improves prediction beyond context and whether within-material pairing matters; this is a candidate contrast, not a causal emotion claim. Cross-corpus negative transfer would separately require matched target-only versus joint training on compatible targets.

Use trial predictions, participant-equal summaries and participant/material dependence in uncertainty. Report familiar and unseen arms separately with class support. BA is undefined for absent classes; retain missing/unsupported cells explicitly. For continuous targets report MAE and a declared secondary squared-error metric; for classification report balanced probabilistic loss plus BA, rather than choosing the best accuracy. Define one primary contrast, multiplicity handling, a minimum meaningful effect and failure criteria before confirmation; none is selected from unseen data here.

Final confirmation uses the reserved participants and material families once, after choices are sealed. Retraining on permitted development participants/materials must preserve exclusion boundaries. Zero/negative results are retained. Another split or initialization on inspected cohorts is development sensitivity. Unknown pretraining overlap, partial author responses, repaired metadata or software-test success do not clear all gates automatically.
