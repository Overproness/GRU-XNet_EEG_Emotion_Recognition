Disable both module dropout and internal multi-head attention dropout; use no weight decay or label smoothing and no scheduler, with gradient clipping at1. Retain every failed result; no early stopping, outcome-dependent duration or revised threshold. All **16 trajectories and 48 states** are checked, including permuted-label controls. Memorizing random labels demonstrates available fitting capacity, not meaningful emotion learning.
