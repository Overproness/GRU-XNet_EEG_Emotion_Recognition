# CBraMod source-only representation assessment

Declared 9 October 2026 before feature extraction or task fitting. The manuscript and main research question remain unchanged. This is a small development diagnostic, not a conference contribution or a reproduction of the authors' published scores.

## Why this representation

[CBraMod was accepted at ICLR 2025](https://openreview.net/forum?id=NPNUHgHF2w). Its [primary paper, version 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.07236v3) describes pretraining on the clinical TUEG corpus and downstream emotion experiments on FACED and SEED-V. These tasks and evaluation protocols differ from our DEAP/SEED-IV panels. The [official repository](https://github.com/wjq-learning/CBraMod) supplies an approximately 4.9-million-parameter encoder and links the released checkpoint. This is an existing baseline with a more narrowly documented pretraining corpus than the partially disclosed REVE corpus; neither external checkpoint's actual full training membership has been independently certified here.

The locally reviewed author implementation is pinned to Git revision `b9e961003214326972c567eff390e75b0287e32a`; [released weights](https://huggingface.co/weighting666/CBraMod/tree/500543c7e30bda1b22bfd51a49301b238dee21fd) are pinned to HF revision `500543c7e30bda1b22bfd51a49301b238dee21fd`, SHA256 `0792cb808c14e6b7a2bb2ce1dff379bc47bc54c49a779825bdfeb33bf8157178`. The HF card is minimal and labels weights Apache-2.0; the source license is MIT. Code and weights remain local; public evidence records download hashes and review rather than redistributing them.

## Input contract and adaptations

The [author FACED loader](https://github.com/wjq-learning/CBraMod/blob/b9e961003214326972c567eff390e75b0287e32a/datasets/faced_dataset.py) divides numerical EEG by 100, and its [preprocessor](https://github.com/wjq-learning/CBraMod/blob/b9e961003214326972c567eff390e75b0287e32a/preprocessing/preprocessing_faced.py) uses Fourier resampling and ten-second windows. The [SEED-V preprocessor](https://github.com/wjq-learning/CBraMod/blob/b9e961003214326972c567eff390e75b0287e32a/preprocessing/preprocessing_SEEDV.py) operates on continuous CNT recordings, resamples to 200 Hz and applies a 0.3–75 Hz MNE filter; its loader also divides by 100. No per-trial z-scoring is introduced in this probe.

We preserve all native EEG channels in their recorded order: 32 for DEAP and 62 for SEED-IV. DEAP's three-second measured baseline is excluded. Its entire available sixty-second stimulus is resampled from 128 to 200 Hz before taking the first forty seconds. The released, already preprocessed signal's bandwidth is retained; interpolation cannot restore discarded frequencies. SEED-IV's available 200 Hz trials are individually filtered with MNE 1.10.0 default zero-phase FIR at 0.3–75 Hz before taking forty seconds. This matches the numerical filtering family but differs from the author's continuous-file boundary treatment and SEED-V task. Offline preprocessing can depend on later samples of the same trial; this is not streaming inference.

The microvolt interpretation of the numerical release is an explicit assumption. The local SEED-IV ReadMe does not certify physical calibration, and first-party DEAP signal authentication remains outstanding. Report RMS, maximum magnitude and the proportion exceeding numerical amplitude 100 for every retained source trial. Do not rescale or discard subjects after inspecting outcomes. The TUEG pretraining-only 60 Hz notch and amplitude rejection are not transferred indiscriminately to these downstream datasets.

## Fixed small comparison

Reuse the verified preprocessing diagnostic's two participant/video groupings, session 1, rotation 0, fold 0, unexposed arm. Each fit accesses only that grouping's training trials and its two participant-disjoint source validation panels. No outer-test predictions are computed. The preparation cache is the union of allowed source trials across groups; an observation excluded from one grouping may belong to another grouping's source panel. Preparation opens original participant files containing other recordings, but only designated source observations are transformed and no excluded trial is fitted or evaluated.

Use the unchanged official encoder at the released checkpoint and at initialization 42. Strict tensor-only checkpoint loading precedes removing the reconstruction projection, as in the author classifiers. Both encoders remain frozen in evaluation mode. Each observation has four disjoint ten-second windows, represented as channel × ten one-second patches × 200 samples. FP32 inference, no AMP or model adaptation.

Retain two [author-supported classifier readouts](https://github.com/wjq-learning/CBraMod/blob/b9e961003214326972c567eff390e75b0287e32a/models/model_for_faced.py): average all channel/patch tokens to 200 features, or flatten native channel/patch/token coordinates to 64,000/124,000 features. Average the four window representations before classification. The flatten readout preserves within-window temporal positions but averages corresponding positions over the four windows. It is a frozen linear probe, not the author full fine-tuning or multilayer classifier.

Add absolute and relative four-band Welch features extracted from precisely the same native 200 Hz waveforms, before amplitude division: ten four-second windows, Hann 400, overlap 200, FFT 400, half-open bands 4–8/8–14/14–31/31–40 Hz, density integration at 0.5 Hz, natural logarithms with floor 1e-12. All six models are retained in all four source panels.

Each head uses training-only StandardScaler and balanced lbfgs logistic regression with C = 0.01/0.1/1/10, max iterations 4,000, tolerance 1e-6. Choose within each model/panel by equal-weight familiar/unseen validation class-balanced log loss, then mean balanced accuracy and stable first tie. Preserve all training/validation candidates. This gives 24 selected heads and 96 task fits, plus 96 independent refits. No outcome-dependent extension, global winning encoder selection, context fusion or joint-training claim.

## Verification and decision

Hash-bind all implementation dependencies, prior source panels, author assets, checkpoint and synthetic resource pilot before fitting. Check trial, participant and material exclusions. Independently refit every candidate, replay its scaler and coefficients, calculate the probability link and weighted metrics, and recheck selection. Reprocess the first and last two source-union observations per dataset from raw files; replay both encoders with batch one instead of four for those eight observations.

Eight implementation tests pass, including binary and three-class fit/CSV/refit integration. The synthetic pilot's highest allocated CUDA tensor memory was 78,934,016 bytes; this excludes driver/context memory and does not measure full fine-tuning. An initial unactivated interpreter launch could not resolve the already-installed CUDA runtime DLL. Activating the user's `pytorch` environment fixed it; no package or author code was changed. The preflight is preserved.

Report every readout and grouping, including training behavior and source validation. These panels are small and reused; no population intervals, new-cohort replication, architecture-only conclusion, generic absence-of-EEG statement or novel algorithm follows. A consistent pretrained loss advantage over both random and spectral controls can justify a separately declared source-only fine-tuning diagnostic. Failure of a frozen readout cannot rule out full fine-tuning. Before adopting a new research question, show the evidence and concrete proposal, obtain the author's explicit approval and archive the then-current paper.
