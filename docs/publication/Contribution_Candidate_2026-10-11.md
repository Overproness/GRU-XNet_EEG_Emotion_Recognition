# Candidate individual-valence evaluation question

11 October 2026. **Reviewable candidate only. Adoption, fitting and confirmation access are held.** The author permits exploratory work but requires evidence and explicit approval before changing the paper question, with a fresh manuscript archive immediately before adoption. The [decision report](GRU-XNet_Contribution_Target_Decision_2026-10-11.md) gives the accumulated evidence and closest-work comparison.

## Exact question and possible contribution

For new participants, does EEG improve prediction of individual retrospective valence beyond source-learned elicitation/context information, and does that improvement survive transfer to different source-film families?

The possible contribution is a reproducible finding about incremental usefulness under defined information and population/material shifts. Neither the question, split, established regression tools nor a failed model search is claimed as a new method. The current evidence does not establish the proposed contribution.

## Target and information available at inference

Retain FACED item-10 individual continuous valence on 0-7, with every observed value eligible. Retain the existing non-neutral elicitation population and global exclusion of clip 22. Predict one retrospective whole-clip report from the existing late-viewing EEG interval. Do not describe it as instantaneous affect, real-time tracking, all-valence coverage or a shared native target with SEED-IV.

The existing context model receives assigned elicitation category, nominal catalogue duration, presentation ordinal and acquisition group. These are legitimate **conditional benchmark inputs** only when supplied at inference. Assigned category is stimulus-design information; it is not an observed test participant's self-report. Do not call this a generic deployment setting or a direct raw-video semantic model. Source film-rating medians apply only to familiar material. Unfamiliar films cannot receive their own evaluated-person consensus or outcomes from source people watching reserved films.

A category-unavailable track would ask a different operational question and needs its own fixed controls. It must not be introduced after seeing a weak conditional result. No footage encoder is currently authenticated or evaluated; physical content similarity and pretraining membership remain separate limitations.

## Endpoint, precision and outcome interpretation

Use paired context-MAE minus joint-MAE on the original scale, with equal participant and source-title-family weighting. Average clips within a family first. Report familiar-material error, MSE, coverage, acquisition groups and leave-family sensitivity secondarily; do not promote a secondary winner after a primary failure.

The existing 0.15 MAE rule is an engineering screen, not a psychometrically established minimum or a novelty criterion. A future plan must state why an increment matters and show sensitivity to alternative margins before confirmation. An interval spanning zero and useful gains is inconclusive. A bounded null requires its upper bound to exclude the chosen useful increment for the tested recipes; it cannot imply that all EEG methods lack information. Descriptive intervals from reused development outcomes do not meet this requirement.

The fixed reserve contains 33 people, nine clips and eight exact-title families: at most 297 unfamiliar-film trials. Confirmation labels/quality remain unknown. Assess precision from development-only evidence under explicit assumptions, with participant/material dependence and selection uncertainty acknowledged. Do not reallocate the reserve, count windows as independent samples or claim equivalence because a significance test failed. Additional acquisition or material diversity may be necessary if the claim cannot be estimated with this reserve.

## Gates and stopping criteria

1. Establish a specific finding not already covered by the closest FACED individual-label/confounding methods, familiar-video priors, shared/individual regression and honest benchmarking. Full FACED methods remain inaccessible; search coverage is bounded.
2. Qualify catalogue/material version grouping and marker endpoint scope, and document every context field's inference availability. Without physical media authentication, restrict the transfer claim to catalogue families.
3. Produce a fixed selection/baseline/precision plan using existing development evidence only. Resolve the EmoEEG-MC codebook and overlap before promising external replication; do not wait indefinitely to define a fallback.
4. Present the findings and concrete plan to the author. Adoption requires their explicit approval and a fresh archive; approval alone does not open confirmation or cure methodological gaps.
5. Only then declare an exact bounded development execution. Expand a method only for a reproducible, meaningful held-out lead against strong controls. Use independent confirmation only after locking the claimed result and analysis. Preserve negative and inconclusive outcomes.

If the gap or data-scope gate fails, do not adopt this question. If a bounded approved study has no defensible effect or sufficiently precise bounded null, stop that direction and reconsider acquisition/target suitability. Do not continue adding heads until a favorable story appears.
