# Contribution options after the fixed EEGNet diagnostic

11 October 2026. Reviewable decision options, not an adopted paper question. The author specifies **public datasets only** and requires findings plus explicit approval before a research-question change. The [decision report](GRU-XNet_Post_Diagnostic_Contribution_Decision_2026-10-11.md) gives the evidence and source limitations.

| Option | Current decision | Evidence needed before fitting/adoption |
|---|---|---|
| Submit the original joint CNN/BiGRU/attention story | Not supported for a strong conference | A distinctive contribution and matched generalization effect; pooled historical accuracy cannot supply these |
| Add another encoder/head or rating dimension on current panels | Stop automatic expansion | A separate scientifically justified intervention with a documented prior-work difference, not a favorable score search |
| Publish a broad "EEG adds nothing" or negative-transfer paper | Not supported | Adequately precise recipe-specific bounds or matched cross-dataset mitigation evidence; convergence flags/reused panels prevent those conclusions here |
| Keep individual valence and qualify a separate public corpus | Recommended metadata-only action | Original release completeness, self-report semantics, endpoints/units, material overlap and identity-based reservations before outcome access; then a concrete contribution proposal |
| Preserve the current work and choose another research direction | Honest fallback if no qualified question emerges | Present the alternative question and feasible public data to the author; obtain approval and archive before changing the manuscript |

FACED remains the best qualified local candidate: continuous retrospective item-10 valence, 0–7, late marker-relative EEG, supplied study metadata, catalogue-title transfer and the existing non-neutral population. Keep all 33 reserved participants/eight protected families sealed. No new labels, exclusions or fitting are selected.

DENS is an additional directly public source to assess before further fitting. Latest checked snapshot 1.0.6 provides 34 possible EEG/behaviour participant joins; these are filename counts rather than verified trial/rating support. Methods describe whole-clip valence on 1–9, distinct from emotion-click categories; stored coding, timing and protocol discrepancies remain unresolved. The metadata review does not establish larger material diversity, better performance or a new scientific gap. EmoEEG-MC remains conditional on the existing author codebook dependency, with its current reservations intact; DREAMER/AMIGOS remain original-access-dependent alternatives. [Public-source review](../../results/development/post_diagnostic_decision_2026-10-11/source_review.json).

The unchanged operational candidate asks whether EEG predicts individual retrospective valence beyond source-learned elicitation/context for new people and different catalogue-title families. **Adoption remains held:** existing context/confounding and individual-affect studies substantially overlap, and the completed methods have no reproducible meaningful increment or precise bounded null. A separate corpus or new split alone would not create novelty.

Next deliverable: a bounded DENS metadata/header and annotation-codebook qualification, with no rating-value access, full-corpus download or fitting. If it fails, preserve the failure and reassess the public-data options. If it passes, prepare the exact hypothesis and analysis for author review; do not automatically launch a model programme.
