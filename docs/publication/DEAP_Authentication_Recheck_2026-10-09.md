# First-party DEAP recording authentication recheck

Checked 9 October 2026. First-party recording-content authentication remains incomplete. This is independent of the completed rating recovery and mirror/cache consistency checks; those corrected labels remain in use.

The network-enabled public probe, with verified TLS and the previously audited CA bundle, received **HTTP 503** from the official dataset home, download, README, metadata archive and preprocessed Python archive routes. The access-request host timed out. The non-www download route redirected to Queen Mary's general department page; its HTTP 200 was not a DEAP download. Direct EPFL access returned HTTP 403, although the web-readable institutional research page still points to Queen Mary rather than supplying an alternate recording archive. [Official download route](https://www.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/mmv/datasets/deap/download.html), [EPFL project page](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/mmspg/research/page-58317-en-html/bci-2/bci_datasets/emotion_dataset/).

Targeted searches for a relocated official release or author-issued recording checksum did not identify an independently verified first-party copy. Third-party software and Zenodo research records inspected in this search point back to Queen Mary or the same Kaggle mirror already audited; repeating that lineage would not establish independent signal provenance. This is a bounded search finding, not proof that no legitimate copy exists elsewhere.

The final response-bounded [route record](../../results/development/deap_authentication_recheck_2026-10-09/route_checks.json) records URLs, statuses, redirects, timestamps and body hashes. The earlier sandbox-only attempt failed to connect to its local proxy; that was an environment limitation and was not interpreted as official server downtime. A first network-enabled probe and its responses were also preserved locally before tightening the decompressed response bound; the final probe enforces at most 128 KiB per response and records its own source hash. No credentials, agreement acceptance, access-form submission or messages to authors occurred.

The necessary next input is restored official access or a legitimately obtained, provenance-documented copy. The existing `compare-deap` command can then compare every decoded signal sample, participant/trial order, channels, baseline/peripheral signals and ratings, allowing serialization differences. HTTP service checks, matching third-party files and corrected spreadsheets cannot replace that comparison. Until it is possible, retain the limitation and avoid a central scientific claim that depends on independently authenticated DEAP recordings.
