## Recommendation

An established subscription journal is the strongest practical way to pursue a good specialist publication without an author APC or international travel. Open access is optional in the verified hybrid journals below; the expensive optional APC is not the price of the subscription route. No country waiver or institutional funding is needed for that route.

**Journal of Medical and Biological Engineering (JMBE)** is the most plausible zero-APC candidate checked for this project's scope. It would still need a clear original empirical finding and the planned scientific strengthening; neither its fee policy nor its impact factor establishes acceptance prospects. **Medical & Biological Engineering & Computing (MBEC)** is a conditional higher-bar option, with a particularly relevant editorial warning described below. We should complete the bounded development diagnosis before deciding which version of the study can meet either journal's standards.

The author has now fixed the budget: **free first, at most PKR 10,000 in mandatory publication/registration fees**, with travel counted separately. **INMIC** and **ICoDT2** have listed student prices within that ceiling, but their checked submission deadlines are already past. Monitor a future edition; do not assume its price will be unchanged. **ICECT 2027 is excluded because its current PKR 18,000 nonmember student price exceeds the ceiling.** There is no verified currently open conference author-publication route within the ceiling in this bounded search.
