13 October 2026, Asia/Karachi. This is a scientific and arithmetic review of a proposed future evaluation, not protected activation. The completed source comparison, historical failed quality gate, saved papers and inactive proposals remain preserved. This reviewer has opened no original recordings, private arrays, original ratings, validation outcome rows or protected numerical inputs. ## Claim that the evidence can support

The defensible prospective purpose is to estimate the predictive utility of one frozen distributed spectral Ridge recipe, relative to its frozen availability-matched context control, for retrospective individual whole-clip valence on the exact reserved FACED films. This stays within the approved question. Its value would be an independently measured, reproducible benchmark of these particular inputs and recipes, including an unfavorable or inconclusive result. It is not an architecture novelty claim.

The actual source evidence does not supply an incremental EEG lead. On the same 840 material/person-held source pairs at alpha 100, distributed joint MAE is 1.445543, matched context MAE is 1.366504 and category-median MAE is 1.303970. The corresponding distributed gains are −0.079039 and −0.141573. Distributed minus late coverage yields a small favorable MAE contrast of 0.019074, but the direction reverses at the other declared alphas. An algorithmic nomination between EEG recipes cannot be described as a scientifically useful EEG improvement.

The comparison also has a concrete reference limitation: the common-alpha selection optimizes a pooled objective across all three model families, and does not independently optimize context. The context model's source MAE at alpha 0.1/10 is lower than at the selected alpha 100. Therefore a future gain over the matched alpha-100 reference would concern that matched recipe, not an optimized contextual baseline. The category median must remain a mandatory practical comparator, with every method's absolute error visible irrespective of the primary result. Adding or optimizing another comparator after reserved outcomes would invalidate the proposed fixed comparison.

The matched context regressor contains context columns, but its routing consumes the all-three-region EEG availability flag. It is consequently an acquisition-matched control. The category median is the acquisition-free practical baseline. Both EEG recipes require the same early/middle/late acquisitions for eligibility; their difference concerns feature representation, not acquisition burden or latency. The shared fallback correctly gives all matched branches the same category prediction on an unavailable input, retaining that case in every denominator. The known failed source trial is a training-only Inspiration title, so zero real held-source missing cases mean that source predictive results do not empirically validate inference fallback.

Neither an adverse source result nor a future zero-crossing interval establishes that EEG lacks useful emotion information. The current spectral recipes, target timing, convenience sample, engineering artifact rules and previously unresolved neural convergence constrain that conclusion. No equivalence, practical negligible-effect margin, universal negative finding, arbitrary-film generalization or stronger-venue acceptance guarantee is justified.

## Prospective estimand and uncertainty scope

The inactive proposal correctly fixes one primary difference: context absolute error minus nominated-joint absolute error. Absolute errors are formed for each clip first; clips 7 and 8 share one catalogue family; losses are averaged within participant/family, then across eight equally weighted families and thirty-three equally weighted participants. The primary arm has nine clips, 297 outcomes and 264 participant/family cells. Treating the 297 outcomes as independent observations, weighting the two-clip family twice, or averaging predictions before errors would change the estimand.

The paired participant t interval with 32 degrees of freedom describes uncertainty conditional on the exact films, fixed source states and source selection. Its formula is `mean(g_i) ± t(0.975,32) * sample_SD(g_i) / sqrt(33)`. Approximate participant independence and a suitable sampling distribution are assumptions; convenience recruitment does not establish representativeness, and bounded or skewed gains may affect small-sample coverage. It does not include retraining or selection uncertainty and does not estimate generalization to arbitrary films. The shared training state and stimulus catalogue are conditioned on rather than treated as independent training/material draws.

Family-specific and leave-one-family contrasts are required diagnostics. The proposed 4,000 crossed participant/family resamples are descriptive material sensitivity, not a substitute primary interval, new hypothesis family or empirical proof of arbitrary-film precision. The familiar secondary arm remains thirty-three by fourteen development clips, or 462 outcomes. All four methods on both arms require 3,036 complete method/trial predictions to be locked and committed before any reserved target conversion.

## What source-informed precision can establish

The completed source CV provides seventy participants and twelve held titles/families from six paired categories. Fear and Inspiration are training-only source categories; they have no comparable source material-held gains. The future primary contains eight categories/families and uses different fixed full-source states. The source folds also share training data, and selection has reused source development evidence. All real held source inputs were available, so their loss differences do not estimate how future shared fallback will affect gain variation. These differences prevent the seventy source participant gains from certifying a future empirical interval, power or the distribution of the thirty-three reserved participant gains.

Source gain variation can nevertheless inform a transparently labeled plug-in calculation and sensitivity table. The correct planned half-width multiplier is `t(0.975,32)/sqrt(33)`, applied to an assumed future participant-gain standard deviation. An observed source SD is one planning scenario, not a measured future SD. Sensitivity must vary that assumption and preserve unfavorable source means. A source contrast projected to thirty-three people is not an independent confidence interval for the original adaptive CV estimate. Removing difficult source families, inventing a superiority/equivalence margin, extrapolating independent per-clip outcomes or treating selection-dependent fold gains as i.i.d. would overstate precision.

The historical 0.15 engineering continuation screen is not a perceptual importance or equivalence margin and remains unmet. A planning table may explain what precision thirty-three participants could support, but must not silently turn this screen or the old empirical precision gate true. No scientific precision pass follows automatically from a successful arithmetic audit.

## Continuation decision

Preparing and qualifying the exact source-state/backend/export pipeline is justified without consuming the reservation. Protected activation should remain on hold until the source-informed assessment is complete, the authors accept the limited fixed-catalogue estimation purpose despite the adverse source lead, and the frozen state/export and original-file backend are independently qualified. There is only one reserved roster; spending it cannot itself create novelty or provide a reusable model-selection panel.

If independent evaluation is chosen, a result should be reported under the frozen primary and mandatory practical comparator regardless of sign, with no subsequent architecture search on reserved outcomes. A new research question would require showing the evidence and obtaining the user's approval. This review does not change the current question, any manuscript claim or any access gate.
