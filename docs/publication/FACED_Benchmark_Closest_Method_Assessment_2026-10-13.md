# FACED independent benchmark: closest methods and claim boundary

13 October 2026, Asia/Karachi. This review uses public aggregate findings, preserved method audits and primary online sources checked today. It opens no original recording, rating, private feature/state array or trial prediction array, and performs no fitting. It changes no saved paper or research question and activates no protected input.

## Assessment

**A one-time estimate of the frozen recipes on reserved participants and reserved catalogue titles is a defensible benchmark question. It is not an established novel discovery or a strong-conference contribution.** Its useful output would be the incremental predictive utility of the specified spectral EEG representation beyond matched contextual inputs, with a category-median practical comparator, full-denominator acquisition handling and explicitly limited precision. Positive, adverse and uncertain findings must all remain reportable.

The [completed source findings](FACED_Coverage_Missing_EEG_Findings_2026-10-13.md) report category-median MAE 1.3040, matched context 1.3665, late joint 1.4646 and distributed joint 1.4455 on the same 840 held source pairs at common alpha 100. The distributed recipe improves over late by approximately 0.0191 MAE, but is worse than matched context by approximately 0.0790 and category medians by 0.1416. Source-only selection is valuable for leakage control; its use after repeated development remains adaptive. The source score supplies no incremental EEG lead and no independent interval.

An unfavorable source result does not invalidate an independent estimate, provided the purpose is reviewed before consuming the reservation. It does rule out describing confirmation as verification of a demonstrated superior EEG model. The primary question can remain within the approved context-versus-EEG framing: **on this fixed reserved FACED catalogue, what MAE change does the source-selected distributed spectral joint Ridge produce relative to the matching contextual Ridge, for previously unused participants?** Category medians must remain visible, because outperforming one contextual regressor would not ensure practical usefulness.

## Category-context ancestry

The original [FACED Methods](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-023-02650-w) support intended elicitation context: categories informed selection and valence informed blocking. I infer that organizers knew this context before each trial; it is not an individual's response. An independent pilot, complete annotation ancestry and absence of later main-cohort recoding remain unauthenticated. Condition the benchmark on supplied metadata. Do not claim independently validated categories, entirely leakage-free upstream labels or generalization to unlabeled new stimuli. This caveat requires no local ratings.

## What can become a contribution

The potential contribution is a reusable, audited **evaluation instance**: individual retrospective valence, fixed source-only fitting and selection, both people and catalogue families reserved, matched contextual and practical contextual comparators, temporal coverage controlled at equal feature dimension, and unchanged acquisition-failure rules. Its novelty lies, if established, in the resulting narrowly specified empirical benchmark and inspectable protocol, rather than any individual component. It requires actual independent findings; a dormant proposal contributes no measured evidence.

The independent estimate would be conditional on thirty-three reserved people, eight deliberately chosen title families represented by nine clips, these fitted source states and supplied elicitation/acquisition metadata. The 297 clip outcomes are not 297 independent participants. The interval must use participant-level gains after family weighting, with the family-specific and crossed-resampling summaries described as material sensitivity. Eight nonrandom families cannot certify arbitrary-film generalization. Catalogue titles do not authenticate physical footage independence, prior media exposure or exact playback alignment.

For unavailable EEG, the matching context and both joint branches use the same source-category fallback. This keeps paired differences at zero on those cases and retains the denominator. The matching context control consumes the common EEG-quality routing flag; it is not an acquisition-free predictor. The category prior is the practical predictor that requires no EEG acquisition. Source donor-only imputation and inference fallback are separate operations that require exported-state qualification.

## Reporting boundaries and decision

| Possible independent result | Defensible statement | Unsupported extension |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Positive matched gain with suitable interval | The specified frozen joint recipe improved over its matched contextual Ridge on the fixed reserved catalogue. | Emotion-specific brain information, best EEG model, or superiority to category medians without that separate contrast. |
| Adverse matched gain | This fixed recipe performed worse than its matching control on the reserved catalogue. | All spectral features, all EEG or all deep learning are ineffective. |
| Zero-crossing interval | The incremental utility estimate is uncertain at this cohort's precision. | Equivalence, negligible benefit or absence of EEG information. |
| Distributed improves over late | This equal-dimensional representation contrast favored distributed coverage under the common acquisition policy. | Whole-film decoding, independent temporal evidence, lower acquisition burden or peak-end causality. |

**Go:** finish the outcome-free claim/precision decision and state/backend/export qualification for a bounded independent benchmark. Preserve the existing no-lead findings and reserve adverse/uncertain conclusions equally.

**No-go:** activate a superior-EEG, architectural, equivalence or broad absence-of-information confirmation claim. The completed neural work still has unresolved convergence and authenticated stronger/pretrained regression baseline gaps. A Ridge-only confirmation cannot settle optimized deep learning. Those gaps must be disclosed; additional baseline fitting is not automatically authorized by this literature review.

My publication judgment is that a clearly limited independent benchmark could strengthen an affordable applied methods or replication-oriented submission beyond the current exploratory paper. A good conference would usually need greater empirical breadth or a stronger distinct scientific result, such as qualified external-dataset replication and robust stronger baselines. This is a reasoned assessment, not an acceptance guarantee or a venue-tier measurement. Novelty remains limited even if the independent interval is precise. The root claim/precision decision should decide whether the finite reservation answers a worthwhile limited question before any protected input is accessed.
