## Scientific and author decisions before targeting

Reused validation, overlapping participants/materials, model selection and incomplete neural convergence remain relevant at every venue. The current evidence can support a transparently scoped exploratory report; an untouched, frozen confirmation would strengthen a claim of generalizable EEG benefit or its absence. Do not open reserves merely to improve a score. If confirmation is chosen, approve a single method/comparator and selection/analysis plan first.

The contribution rests on the target, context comparator, acquisition coverage and complete empirical comparison. Generic stimulus confounding, participant-independent evaluation and EEGNet are established prior work. The manuscript should explain its particular operational distinction and retain the closest references.

Finish corresponding-author/current-affiliation details, all-author review, actual contributions, funding/conflicts, applicable secondary-data ethics/consent and accurate assistance disclosure. Then choose one route, adapt its template and artifact/anonymization requirements, and review its final PDF. Do not put the same work under concurrent journal/conference review.

DENS is a separate public-dataset candidate. Its [authorized inquiry](../../results/development/dens_author_contact_2026-10-12/README.md) has been submitted, but stimulus/phase mapping, interval joins, units, target coding/calibration and release terms remain unresolved. DENS contributes no manuscript result and is not a dependency for the present FACED-only submission. Original GRU-XNet score provenance and first-party DEAP authentication also remain outside this paper's evidence.
