# Independent review of the FACED claim and dormant confirmation proposal

12 October 2026, Asia/Karachi. **Scientific review of completed development evidence and an outcome-free future plan. No protected recording or rating, private array, new fit or new development analysis was accessed.** The current manuscripts and research question remain unchanged. Preparing a frozen protocol does not activate it.

## Decision

The current evidence supports a narrowly framed exploratory evaluation. It does **not** support EEG superiority, equivalence to contextual prediction, general absence of useful EEG information, or a new architecture contribution. A dormant, executable confirmation proposal can make the remaining decision concrete. The evidence does not currently justify consuming the protected panel: the preceding incremental-utility screen remains unmet, neural convergence remains unresolved, and eight deliberately chosen material families offer limited material precision.

The supported present claim is: **in the reused FACED development panels, a source-selected category-median predictor is competitive with the two tested late-interval EEG predictors, and their incremental gains and EEG-pairing effects are inconsistent or uncertain.** This is an adaptive case-study result, not an independently confirmed population finding.

The [completed findings](FACED_Target_Context_Pairing_Findings_2026-10-12.md) report unfamiliar-film mean gains of approximately 0.0446 MAE for spectral joint Ridge and 0.0052 for EEGNet residual prediction over category medians; all six descriptive gain intervals include zero. The means combine dependent rotations. Neither those means nor repeated seeds constitute independent replication. All 24 reported pairing intervals include zero. The adverse familiar-film neural result must remain reported.

## A necessary distinction in the future question

Spectral joint Ridge receives the 13-column contextual representation plus 150 spectral features. Category medians receive assigned category only. Their difference therefore measures the utility of **the whole EEG-containing recipe relative to a category predictor**. It cannot isolate EEG's information because duration, presentation ordinal, acquisition fields and predictor form also differ.

There are two defensible prospective formulations:

- Keep category median as the single primary reference and ask whether the specified whole recipe improves prediction over it. Describe the effect as recipe utility. Freeze a matched context-only Ridge contrast as secondary if making any statement about EEG contribution.
- Make joint versus matched context-only Ridge the single primary contrast for a narrower EEG-specific comparison, and retain category medians as the practical contextual benchmark. The matched model must use the same source rows, 13 contextual columns, source scaling, intercept/target convention and fixed alpha as the joint model. This does not automatically establish usefulness relative to the stronger category benchmark.

The dormant proposal chooses the first formulation: **one primary whole-recipe gain over source category medians, with a mandatory matched context-only Ridge secondary**. This choice is for the author to review; it does not adopt a research question or authorize protected access. Confirmation results cannot select a different favorable framing. Fixed alpha 100 is defensible as a declared historical recipe because all three spectral-joint development selections used it, but that agreement does not establish optimality.

For a matched contrast, acquisition-failure handling also matters. If only the joint model falls back to category medians while the context-only model continues its Ridge prediction, any difference on unavailable EEG trials comes from different fallback functions. The dormant proposal therefore uses the same source-category fallback in both matched branches on these fixed flags. The paired matched difference is zero on unavailable EEG outcomes, and every outcome remains in the denominator. Protected targets cannot decide availability or the fallback. This is a prospective recipe distinct from the old anchors, whose original full-context fallback remains unchanged in the completed development packets.

## Source and material scope

A proposed final source fit on the original 70 clean source participants and all 14 permitted development titles has 980 trials. The 20 development-validation participants can inform historical recipe selection without entering this source fit. Using that rectangle avoids silently converting their 15 unavailable EEG trials into source training. All learned scalers, priors, intercepts, contextual coefficients and spectral coefficients must use only those 980 identities; stored final-source state must be bound before protected prediction.

Category support remains a limitation. In each completed eight-title rotation there was exactly one source title per category. The first crossed source-title folds removed categories as well as titles; the later participant-only qualification retained category support but assessed familiar source films. Neither procedure by itself established category-preserving unseen-title selection. An independent metadata-only check of the original participant reservation, development plan and codebook confirms 70/20/33 people, the 14 permitted clip IDs, and 14 distinct catalogue family hashes in the development source. Fear (clip 9) and Inspiration (clip 20) each have one source title; the other six assigned categories each have two. Thus the final source supports all eight categories but cannot establish category-preserving material-transfer cross-validation for all eight. Do not add an unsupported claim that a source-fold score represents generalization to arbitrary new films.

The proposed primary protected rectangle is metadata-defined: 33 people, eight title families and nine clips, or 297 clip outcomes. Clips 7 and 8 share a family. Compute absolute error on each clip, average clip losses within person/family, then average the eight family losses and 33 person losses. Averaging predictions before errors changes the estimand and is not interchangeable. The familiar-title secondary rectangle has 462 clip outcomes.

Those counts are not access authorization. The category is supplied elicitation metadata, not an individual's reported emotion. Catalogue title separation has not authenticated distinct physical footage or content exposure. The target is one retrospective whole-clip valence rating on 0–7. The predictor uses a fixed offline late interval; it is not a full-clip or momentary-affect decoder. The experiment conditions on category and acquisition information being available at deployment.

## Precision and interpretation

Freeze one primary paired gain, positive when the declared reference has greater MAE than the declared joint recipe. The same family/person weighting must govern the estimate, denominator and every resample. A 4,000-draw crossed participant/family bootstrap is a useful **descriptive sensitivity analysis**. Eight deliberately balanced, nonrandomly chosen title families do not make its percentile bounds calibrated confidence limits for arbitrary future stimuli. State its scope and assumptions rather than calling the interval a general film-population result.

If the intended primary inferential claim is conditional on this fixed roster, prescribe a participant-level interval under an explicit participant-sampling assumption before activation, and keep crossed-family resampling descriptive. Do not choose between those methods by which produces a favorable sign. In either formulation, an interval including zero is inconclusive about superiority. It cannot establish equivalence or absence of neural information. The old 0.15 engineering threshold is neither a perceptual threshold nor an equivalence margin.

An outcome-free precision simulation can evaluate the planned 33-by-eight structure using **explicit assumed** person, family and interaction variance components. It must not estimate them from protected values, tune assumptions to obtain an attractive power figure, or present simulated precision as observed protected variability. Show sensitivity over materially different component allocations, including family-dominated cases. Increasing participant count cannot compensate indefinitely for few independent materials. Simulation quantiles and Monte Carlo error should be reproducible from fixed synthetic seeds.

## What could make this worth publishing

A useful paper could document an authenticated, reproducible evaluation of individual retrospective valence with explicit category information, participant/material separation, acquisition failures and person/film pairing controls. The source-category support problem is a concrete reader-facing lesson: comparator selection changes when the fold removes a category that is available at the intended inference setting. Careful reporting of these operational distinctions is more useful than another unqualified architecture score.

That value remains an empirical case study. The related-work audit already records close work on FACED stimulus confounding and contextual priors, with some complete methods unauthenticated; [closest-method qualification](../../results/development/faced_scope_precision_2026-10-11/closest_method_review.md) remains a submission risk. Crossed splits, assigned versus individual labels and negative model searches cannot be advertised as novel by themselves. A modest specialist submission needs a clear question and reproducible evidence. A stronger methodological claim would need a distinct method or independently supported general finding, credible temporal/model baselines, and more authenticated materials or a suitably qualified second public dataset.

Unresolved EEGNet convergence prevents a conclusion about an adequately optimized neural baseline. Excluding neural confirmation can keep the future question bounded to the spectral recipe; it does not resolve the broader baseline limitation or erase unfavorable neural development evidence. Adding a longer neural schedule or broader temporal coverage requires its own bounded source/development declaration rather than silently broadening this protocol.

## Mandatory stop gates before any confirmation access

1. The author reviews a concrete claim and explicitly approves adopting it. A fresh paper archive precedes any manuscript change. The existing approval to perform diagnostics does not itself adopt this claim.
2. The dormant protocol records that the prior reproducible-lead gate is unmet. It stays inactive unless a separately documented decision justifies and authorizes testing a bounded question despite that limitation. An uncertain-result framing is not a way to bypass the old gate silently.
3. The protected identities, title-family joins, endpoints, category/access contract, late interval, source rows, model recipes, unavailable-EEG policy, metric weighting, interval method and budgets are frozen and numerically preflighted without protected access.
4. Source-only feature/QC and identity replay pass; final source transformations/states and predictions are hash-bound. No protected waveform-derived normalization, calibration or source substitution is allowed.
5. Protected predictions are generated and committed to a receipt before protected rating access. Access requires a separate explicit activation; the current plan must refuse fitting and decoding even if invoked accidentally.
6. Any source integrity, annotation, resource, model or endpoint failure stops the declared attempt and preserves its failure ledger. No automatic retuning, role reassignment, outcome exclusion, interval change or favorable-subset selection follows a protected result.
7. All declared outcomes, acquisition coverage, source fallback predictions, adverse effects and uncertainty are reported. Secondary pairing and comparators remain descriptive and cannot select the primary result.

Preparing a metadata-only freeze and synthetic precision study passes the authorization boundary and makes the choice reviewable. It is not a new scientific finding, a successful final source fit, or independent confirmation.

## Read-only implementation preflight

The reviewer subsequently inspected `gruxnet/faced_confirmation_contract.py`, `scripts/faced_confirmation_precision_2026_10_12.py`, their synthetic tests and the precision-design document without invoking any data backend or simulation producer.

The contract enforces the exact source/protected rectangles, distinct roles, catalogue-family grouping and source identity digest. Protected identity/access checks and a complete-denominator check precede conversion of numerical outcomes. Activation requires separate question/access approvals and byte-bound precision, complete-source cache/QC, original continuation and final-state receipts. Protected signal and rating access are distinct phases; rating reads additionally require all three complete prediction exports to be locked. A source fixture replay alone cannot replace complete source feature-cache qualification. No fresh all-980 waveform replay is claimed.

The prediction helper gives category predictions to both joint and matched-context branches on unavailable EEG and calls neither backend there; it rejects source-fit calls before invoking a callback. The scoring helper verifies a prediction-only digest, excluding targets, against the lock before target conversion. It calculates clip absolute errors before family means, uses all declared trials, shares crossed draws between contrasts and reports both the primary whole-recipe difference and descriptive matched-context secondary. The future interval remains descriptive. The implementation is a **guarded inactive contract and pure analysis helpers**, not a completed final-source fit or protected-data extraction/inference runner. A future backend must qualify actual export bytes against the locked projection and authenticate actual source model/scaler/prior states, including equality of shared contextual scaling. The present metadata assertions are not those state qualifications.

The precision script accepts only explicit public reservation/design metadata files, checks their hashes and a separate simulation-only activation before drawing, and never opens empirical targets, prediction files or signals. Multinomial axis counts implement the same Cartesian person/title resampling as repeated index draws. Translating the same zero-gain matrices by fixed gains correctly preserves interval width/coverage while changing endpoint signs. The model is a family-level working approximation; it does not generate clip ratings or authenticate a physiological model.

Its 256 synthetic matrices per scenario/dimension and 512 bootstrap draws per matrix are bounded planning calculations, distinct from the prospective 4,000 empirical descriptive resamples. Four assumed variance allocations and hypothetical 66-person/16-family designs test sensitivity. The supplied gain of 0.045 was informed by published development summaries, and the design discloses that it is not blinded to those aggregates. No empirical variance is used. A passed reproducibility check cannot turn `precision_gate_passed: false` into a scientifically adequate precision gate.

This preflight finds no scientific boundary violation in those code paths. An initial independent fixture-only run passed 35 tests. After the target-phase and prediction-lock safeguards were added, the final independent run passed **41 tests in 1.33 seconds**, with pytest caching disabled. Those tests manufacture every numerical outcome; their real-file reads are limited to the declared public metadata. They check inactive access, each required gate, source/role/family identities before numerical conversion, complete denominators, immutable prediction projections before target conversion, common fallback, clip-loss/family weighting and crossed-bootstrap math. The synthetic execution receipt and independent synthetic-output recount remain to be inspected after the producer completes. The preceding no-lead hold remains binding regardless of that result.
