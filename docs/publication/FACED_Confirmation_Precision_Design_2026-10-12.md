# FACED outcome-free confirmation precision sensitivity

12 October 2026, Asia/Karachi. **Declared synthetic study; execution requires a separately frozen activation. Protected outcomes remain sealed.** This study evaluates how a small title roster can limit the precision of a paired EEG-versus-context comparison. It estimates neither FACED variances nor empirical statistical power and does not adopt a manuscript question.

Only the public participant reservation and catalogue-family proposal are inputs. Their hashes and metadata projection are frozen by `precision_plan.json`. The protected rectangle has 33 people, eight catalogue title families and nine clips (297 nominal trials, 264 person/title cells). Clips 7/8 belong to one title; paired losses must be averaged within that family before equal family averaging. The existing source-only 70-person/14-development-family rectangle has 980 trials. No additional people or titles are actually available for confirmation under this calculation.

The synthetic paired absolute-loss differences are `D_ij = delta + A_i + B_j + E_ij`, with independent zero-mean Gaussian subject, title and interaction effects. Positive differences represent lower EEG-model loss. These are idealized loss differences in rating-point units, not generated self-reports, predictions or physiological measurements. No empirical variance estimate enters the study. Gaussian differences have unbounded support outside the actual loss-difference range `[-7,7]`; that approximation and unmodeled training/selection uncertainty limit interpretation.

The following **analyst-chosen standard deviations**, fixed before simulation, span different variance allocations. They are sensitivity assumptions rather than observed values:

| Scenario | Subject SD | Title SD | Interaction SD |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Low | 0.10 | 0.10 | 0.20 |
| Participant dominant | 0.30 | 0.05 | 0.20 |
| Title dominant | 0.05 | 0.30 | 0.20 |
| Balanced | 0.20 | 0.20 | 0.40 |

Each scenario uses dimensions 33×8, 66×8, 33×16 and 66×16 and fixed true gains 0, 0.045 and 0.15. The latter three designs are hypothetical comparisons; they neither reopen development identities nor authorize new protected assignments. The root supplied 0.045 after published development summaries: this sensitivity point is not a fitted truth, and the scenarios are not blinded to public aggregate findings. The 0.15 value is an engineering screen, not a validated clinical or perceptual threshold. No empirical variance calibration is used.

For each of 16 scenario/dimension combinations, generate 256 synthetic matrices. For each matrix, draw 512 paired crossed-bootstrap replicates by independently sampling whole person and title axes with replacement and taking the family-equal matrix mean. Use linear 2.5%/97.5% percentile quantiles. The same zero-gain matrix and bootstrap samples are translated by each gain: this preserves the exact coverage and width calculation while allowing zero-exclusion counts to change. Totals are 4,096 matrices, 2,097,152 bootstrap means and 48 reporting rows. NumPy `default_rng` streams use `SeedSequence([2026101209, scenario_index, dimension_index]).spawn(2)`, separating matrix and bootstrap generation. The fixed execution budget is five CPU minutes and five wall minutes, with no new empirical model fits.

Report interval coverage counts/rates, mean/median widths and the descriptive fraction with a positive lower endpoint, negative upper endpoint or either endpoint excluding zero. Report binomial Monte Carlo standard errors so readers can judge simulation granularity. Analytic mean variance is `subject_SD²/N + title_SD²/J + interaction_SD²/(N×J)`; the title term leaves an `title_SD/sqrt(J)` standard-error floor as people increase indefinitely. A Gaussian oracle width supplies a reference, not an empirical interval.

The simulation treats titles as random exchangeable units. FACED's eight protected titles are an assigned, category-stratified roster, so simulated crossed coverage cannot validate inference over arbitrary movies. The proposed primary empirical estimate should stay conditional on its fixed catalogue roster; crossed title-resampling can be reported descriptively. A narrower synthetic interval does not establish a meaningful EEG effect, exclude neural emotion information or resolve source-media/timing authentication. Missingness, acquisition heterogeneity, non-Gaussian dependence, recipe selection and final training variability are omitted. The existing empirical lead gate remains blocking; this sensitivity study always records `precision_gate_passed: false` and does not satisfy that gate.

The standalone script supports metadata-only `--declare`, separately gated `--run` and a `--verify` aggregate recount from its own synthetic records. The root activation must bind the plan SHA256, explicitly permit the precision simulation and retain `confirmation_access: false` and `research_question_changed: false`. Script, test and design hashes plus both metadata hashes are checked before the first synthetic draw. Existing empirical caches and original EEG/ratings paths are never opened. Existing packets and manuscripts are unchanged. The outputs' seeded counts are independently reproducible; synthetic tests verify crossed weights, translation, denominator guards, variance floors and activation failure before any draw.

No empirical target/prediction file, private array, protected outcome or original recording/rating file was opened by this study.
