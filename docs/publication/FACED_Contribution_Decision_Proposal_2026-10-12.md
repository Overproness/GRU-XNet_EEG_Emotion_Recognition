# Contribution and confirmation decision for author review

12 October 2026. **Proposal only: no research question adopted, no manuscript changed and no confirmation activated.** The existing source/PDF archives and both current FACED paper packages remain intact. This document follows the completed, independently audited target/context/pairing diagnosis and category-supported baseline qualification.

## Evidence-backed framing

Suggested working title: **Context controls and EEG pairing in retrospective FACED valence prediction: an exploratory evaluation**.

Candidate question: **For the specified late-interval predictors, does EEG improve individual retrospective valence prediction beyond a source-qualified stimulus-category control, and how sensitive are predictions to matching EEG to the person and film?**

The supported present finding is bounded to reused development panels: source-selected category medians are competitive; spectral gains average 0.0446 and EEGNet gains 0.0052 MAE on unfamiliar films, with all six descriptive gain intervals including zero. Category support changes comparator selection. Spectral person matching has a consistent direction on unfamiliar panels, but its intervals include zero; film and neural matching are mixed.

This would be an empirical evaluation/case-study contribution. It would not claim a new architecture, general absence of EEG information, a causal emotional mechanism, equivalence to context, momentary emotion recognition, or independently confirmed superiority. The known evaluation principles themselves are not a new method. A scientifically useful negative or uncertain result still needs a justified task, credible baselines, honest precision and a clear reader benefit.

## Recommended decision now

**Keep protected confirmation sealed and stop broad model-grid expansion.** There is no qualifying incremental-utility lead from the completed conditions, and low precision prevents a confident absence-of-benefit claim. Preserving the panel permits a stronger, fully specified future question to be tested once.

There are two viable routes for the author to choose:

1. **Develop a modest exploratory evaluation paper from the current evidence.** Add the new comparator qualification and pairing results to an archived copy, retain their adaptive-development status, and explain why one source title per category changes source selection. A specialist venue might find the reproducible evaluation useful, but current evidence does not establish the originality/generalizability expected by stronger venues. Do not make independent confirmation a claim in this version.
2. **Prioritize a stronger public-data study before a submission pivot.** Continue authenticated DENS timing/annotation qualification once the authors provide the execution/export codebook, or qualify another public dataset with better material/target support. Establish participant/material separation and reserve outcomes before model fitting. A different dataset is useful only if its annotations and independent stimuli address the present limitation; additional participants alone do not resolve category/title confounding.

The first route fits the author's request for a paper from present progress. The second has a better chance of producing a stronger scientific contribution, but depends on suitable authenticated public data. No private collection or EEG-lab access is proposed.

## Confirmation plan if the author later chooses that route

A complete executable plan must be frozen before any protected waveform or rating is decoded. The existing metadata reservation contains 33 people and eight unfamiliar title families, nine clips because two clips share a title. Its primary rectangle would contain 297 unfamiliar clip-level trials; average clip-level absolute errors within each person/title group before averaging titles and people. Do not average predictions before computing error. Familiar-title secondary scope would contain 462 clip-level trials across 14 development titles. These counts are metadata only and do not authorize access. Title grouping/separation comes from the catalogue; footage independence has not been authenticated. A confirmation claim must retain that scope unless original stimulus files and their overlap are separately verified.

The confirmation claim must remain specific to a chosen target, interval, information contract and evaluated predictor recipe. The current candidate primary comparator is source category median. A practical candidate EEG recipe is the qualified spectral-joint Ridge; all three old development selections used alpha 100. That historical agreement is development evidence, not proof of optimality. A final recipe still needs a source-only fit/identity preflight before deployment.

For a bounded final fit, retaining the original 70 clean source people and all 14 permitted development titles would provide 980 previously qualified source trials. The 20 development validation people would inform development decisions but would not become source fit rows. This avoids silently promoting their 15 unavailable EEG trials into training. Source scaling, priors, model state and any final normalization must use exactly the declared source identities. No final fit has been executed here.

Before activating confirmation, the plan must resolve and bind:

- One primary paired estimand: category-context MAE minus the chosen EEG predictor MAE, averaged equally over people and title families, with every declared outcome retained and a frozen unavailable-EEG fallback.
- Fixed model/readout/alpha/interval/preprocessing and source identities; numerical replay and source-only QC gates; exact protected join/endpoint and artifact policy before rating access.
- A small-material uncertainty plan and an explicit interpretation rule. Report paired effect and descriptive crossed-person/title interval; use one primary comparison and retain all adverse outcomes. With eight families, an interval including zero cannot prove equivalence. The old 0.15 engineering threshold is not a clinical/perceptual equivalence margin.
- Secondary checks, including pairing or a neural comparator, selected in advance with honest convergence and multiplicity limits. Do not reselect a successful rotation, seed, title subset or interval after confirmation outcomes.
- Fit, extraction and inference budgets; abort/no-retry rules; preservation of failures; public anonymous predictions and reproducible receipts; no adaptive reopening of confirmation after an unfavorable result.

The observed 0.045 spectral mean gain is much smaller than development uncertainty. There is no evidence that the reserved panel will be precise enough to confirm that effect. An outcome-free precision simulation using the declared grouping structure would be needed before consuming it, with assumptions disclosed and no guaranteed power. A larger trial count does not replace more independent title families.

## Remaining baseline/target decision

Existing EEGNet source-window integrity is qualified, but earlier curves raised convergence questions. A longer fixed schedule or broader temporal coverage may be worth a separately bounded source/development control if the author wants a broader claim about EEG utility. Neither is activated by this proposal. Conclusions can instead remain bounded to the present recipes and late 32-second representation. This choice affects scientific scope, not only computational effort.

## Approval boundary

The author explicitly requested findings before any research-question change and continuation only after approval. The proposed framing therefore remains unadopted. Approval of a direction would authorize preparation of a new manuscript/protocol version after archiving; it would not silently authorize protected confirmation, submission or payment. The scientific findings and all authorized code/artifact publication are complete independently of that future decision.
