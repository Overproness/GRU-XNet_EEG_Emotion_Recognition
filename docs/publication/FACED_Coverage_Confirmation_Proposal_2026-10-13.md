# A fixed-catalogue confirmation proposal after the temporal-coverage diagnostic

13 October 2026, Asia/Karachi. **Inactive preparation for the approved FACED retrospective-valence question.** This document authorizes no protected recording or rating access, final-source fit, model selection, manuscript revision or external message. The saved papers and the [earlier inactive confirmation contract](../../results/development/faced_confirmation_freeze_2026-10-12/contract.json) remain unchanged. The new source-only coverage diagnostic must finish and be independently audited before a finalist can be nominated.

## Purpose and the proposed primary claim

The scientific question remains whether an EEG representation adds predictive utility beyond declared context for individual retrospective whole-clip valence. The proposed independent evaluation asks a narrower, answerable question: **for new FACED participants under the exact reserved catalogue roster, how much does one frozen spectral-plus-context Ridge recipe improve absolute error over its matched context-only Ridge control?**

This is a comparison of supplied predictor inputs within a fixed prediction procedure. It does not identify cerebral rather than peripheral information, an emotion-specific mechanism, causal effects of EEG, general superiority of EEG models, or performance on an arbitrary population of movies. Source-only temporal selection is adaptive development. Only a later locked, one-time reserved evaluation would be independent of that selection.

The old contract made category median versus joint Ridge the primary whole-recipe comparison. This proposal instead makes **matched context versus joint Ridge** the single primary comparison. Category medians remain a mandatory practical benchmark, because a gain over a weaker matched model need not improve on a competitive category prior. That primary choice is fixed now for review, before any protected outcome. It must not be exchanged for whichever reference later gives the favorable result.

## Nomination remains a dependency

The ongoing source-only experiment compares the historical late 150-dimensional spectral vector with the equally weighted mean of early, middle and late 150-dimensional vectors, using the same context representation and bounded Ridge selection. Distributed sampling is not exhaustive whole-clip measurement: regions can overlap and long clips remain partly unobserved. Its source selection supports only the six categories with two development titles; Fear and Inspiration each have one development title and remain source training only in material-transfer selection.

The [declared source grid](../../results/development/faced_coverage_selection_preflight_2026-10-13/plan.json) has 90 CV fits: context-only, late joint and distributed joint Ridge, each at alpha 0.1, 10 and 100, in ten source person/material cells. Each cell fits 56 source participants by eight titles and holds fourteen participants by six other titles, with all assigned categories supported in training. The fixed-alpha-100 matched gains and late/distributed ablation are the primary development diagnostic over all 840 distinct held source pairs. Secondary nomination selects one **common alpha** by the smallest equally weighted mean MAE across the three model families on those same 840 pairs; ties use mean MSE, then the declared alpha order. At that common alpha, the lower-MAE joint representation is the nominee; ties use MSE and then prefer late. This is shared-alpha adaptive source selection, not independent confirmation or separate alpha optimization for each predictor.

Only if every one of the 2,940 source regions passes strict QC may targets be joined or any grid/final fit execute. If that gate passes, three final source fits on all 980 trials plus a category-prior construction are part of the declared development experiment. Those fit/state outputs would still need independent qualification before any protected use. There is no access to the twenty validation participants in this experiment.

No finalist, alpha or improved-accuracy finding is adopted in this preparation document. The exact pre-frozen selection rule, outcome, resource use, complete-source QC disposition and independent audit will be bound in the accompanying metadata proposal once available. If strict source preparation fails, the result is a **stopped source-qualification attempt**, not a negative accuracy result. No historical late recipe may be promoted as a favorable replacement after that stop. Any corrected source preparation requires a separate declaration preserving the failed attempt.

Successful source-only selection would nominate a recipe, not establish a reproducible improvement, adequate precision or novelty. Existing neural convergence limitations and adverse neural results remain reported; a spectral confirmation cannot certify adequately optimized neural affect decoding.

## Fixed signal scope, pending backend qualification

The proposed signal definition preserves the source preflight's exact integer range rule. At native sample rate `r`, let `A = ceil(marker_start × r)`, `B = floor(marker_end × r)` and `N = 32 × r`. Early starts at `A`, middle at `floor((A+B−N)/2)` and late at `B−N`; each range ends at its start plus `N`, with an exclusive stop. The existing operator uses local seconds 2–30, thirty common scalp channels, 128 Hz and seven four-second windows per region. The late vector is the historical 150-dimensional spectral representation; the distributed vector is the equal mean of all three such regional vectors, preserving column order and dimension.

The bound calibration, filtering, channel repair and availability implementation must remain unchanged and pass the actual backend preflight. Existing artifact limits include pre-CAR core standard deviation above 100 microvolts, peak-to-peak above 1,000 microvolts and at most three interpolated channels. The availability policy remains `faced-quality-context-fallback-v1`; these are engineering rules, not validated artifact labels. An endpoint/range mismatch or incomplete input does not authorize a different window.

All 759 proposed protected primary/familiar trials require three ranges, or 2,277 region preparations. This is prospective scope metadata; no protected region is opened here. Exact permitted recording-byte/count and wall/process-CPU caps must be frozen in the separately qualified executable activation before access, using authenticated header geometry. Source sample-rate/QC qualification does not by itself qualify protected files.

## Matching the actual prediction procedures

After a valid nominee is fixed, four prospective predictors are retained: category median, matched context-only Ridge, late joint Ridge and distributed joint Ridge. The three Ridge models must use the same nominated common alpha, exact source rows, 13 contextual columns, per-column source scaling, intercept convention, SVD solver and clipping to [0,7]. Here "fixed alpha" means the source-selected common value, locked before protected access; it does not automatically mean the diagnostic's alpha 100 or the historical recipe's alpha 100. The context portion of each joint scaler must numerically equal the context-only scaler. Each joint adds its declared 150 spectral columns in their fixed order. No predictor's alpha is independently reselected after nomination.

The practical comparator is the median of the same exact source ratings in each supplied assigned category, with a source global median fallback. Protected eligibility is fixed for all three Ridge branches: **all three early/middle/late regions must pass the same frozen QC policy**. If any fails, matched context and both joint models use that same category prediction. Keep every trial in all denominators, and call no EEG model on unavailable input. Both matched EEG gains and the late/distributed difference are therefore zero on those trials.

The all-three-region requirement is a conservative prospective resource/availability policy, including for the late predictor and context-only control. It deliberately changes the historical late-only operational recipe and historical full-context fallback, which remain unchanged in completed evidence. A trial with usable late EEG but a failed earlier region still receives the common category prediction. This isolates the declared coverage contrast from different failure handling; it is not a claim that earlier-region failure scientifically invalidates late EEG.

Late and distributed candidates must not receive different source rows or outcome exclusions. Failure of an early or middle region is not repaired by substituting late-only features. Neither primary nor secondary protected results may change the fixed quality thresholds, channel repair limit, feature operator, availability logic or source-fit membership.

## One primary estimate and an explicitly conditional interval

For each protected clip, calculate the paired difference

`d = |rating − matched-context prediction| − |rating − joint prediction|`.

Average those **clip losses** within each participant/catalogue family, then weight all eight families equally within each participant. Average the resulting 33 participant gains equally. Positive gain favors the joint recipe. Do not average predictions before calculating losses; the two operations define different estimates.

The proposed primary interval is fixed in advance: **mean participant gain ± t(0.975,32) × sample standard deviation of participant gains / sqrt(33)**. It conditions on the exact films, final source states and selection procedure. Its interpretation depends on the participant gains behaving as independent observations from the intended new-participant population and on the usual approximate sampling-distribution assumptions. FACED convenience recruitment does not establish that population's representativeness. Thirty-three people, bounded/skewed losses and shared source training can limit approximation quality; the interval does not include uncertainty in retraining or adaptive model choice. No interval is promised to have exact coverage.

There is one primary matched gain, with its full estimate, interval and denominator reported regardless of sign. No primary hypothesis test or equivalence margin is introduced by this proposal. An interval crossing zero is inconclusive about superiority; it cannot establish no useful EEG information or negligible gain. A positive interval is conditional evidence for this fixed recipe/roster under the stated assumptions, not general-film or brain-causality evidence.

Mandatory secondary reporting includes the fixed-alpha late-versus-distributed gain, defined as late absolute error minus distributed absolute error, with the same clip/family/person weighting. Both representations are scored regardless of which source nominee wins. This independently describes their predictive difference under the common availability policy; it does not prove a temporal brain mechanism or exhaustive whole-clip superiority. This contrast never replaces the primary nominated-joint versus matched-context comparison.

Other mandatory secondary results include category-median MAE and gains, each of the four methods' absolute MAE, coverage/fallback counts, the eight family-specific gains, leave-one-family mean gains and the familiar-material results. Paired crossed participant/family resampling with **4,000 draws, seed 425401**, using the same family weighting and shared draws for all contrasts, remains a **descriptive material-sensitivity analysis**. It cannot replace the fixed primary interval after seeing the result. Secondary comparisons do not select the primary conclusion.

## Why the old inactive gates cannot be silently cleared

The old 0.15-unit engineering continuation screen remains unmet and is not a validated perceptual, clinical or equivalence margin. Its synthetic precision exercise used analyst-assumed variance components and did not certify empirical precision. The earlier contract therefore stays frozen and inactive, with its original false gates intact.

A separate scientific decision is needed before activating this new proposal: is a limited-roster independent estimate worth consuming the remaining reservation, given the source-only diagnostic, unresolved baseline limitations and likely uncertainty? A source gain is not logically required to estimate an uncertain effect, but an uncertain-effect purpose is not an automatic bypass of the old screen. The rationale, precision limitations and decision must be recorded explicitly before access. No practical negligible-gain claim can be added without a scientifically justified margin and an appropriate new prospective design.
