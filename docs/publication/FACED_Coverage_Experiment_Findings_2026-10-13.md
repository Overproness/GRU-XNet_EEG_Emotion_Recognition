## Coverage interpretation

The [metadata-only geometry report](../../results/development/faced_coverage_selection_2026-10-13/source_geometry_diagnostics.json) finds overlapping retained cores in 770 of 980 trials, including three-way overlap in 210. Unique nominal retained coverage averages 66.1006 seconds (range 30.108–84), compared with a 28-second late core. The average additional unique coverage is 38.1006 seconds, but clip 23 adds only about 2.17 seconds. Thus three processed 28-second cores are not necessarily 84 distinct seconds or independent evidence. The core geometry is descriptive metadata; it did not alter selection or inspect outcomes.

No claim about coverage benefit can be made without the actual matched comparison.
