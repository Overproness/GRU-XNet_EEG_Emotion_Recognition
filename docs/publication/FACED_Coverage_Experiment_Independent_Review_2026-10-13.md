## Scientific scope

The useful question is whether a fixed spectral representation formed from early, middle and late observations performs better than its existing late-only counterpart when predicting the same whole-clip retrospective item-10 valence report. Both representations have 150 features and use the same contextual metadata, Ridge family, training rows and preprocessing. The distributed vector is the arithmetic mean of the three regional log-power vectors. It is not the logarithm of mean power, complete-video coverage, three independently labelled examples or a new pooling architecture.

The signal-quality pilot established only eight-trial feasibility. Whole-source preparation must qualify all 980 source trials and all 2,940 ranges before a new supervised fit. The old late interval must reproduce the previous exact count, processed-window and QC identities for each of the 980 trials. Every early/middle result must independently pass the unchanged policy. Under this initial declaration, any unavailable source region holds target conversion and fitting. Missing regions cannot be dropped, replaced by late-only data or repaired by choosing a more favourable interval after access. Technical or resource failure preserves the partial ledger and does not authorize an automatic retry.

## Selection design and fairness

The source rectangle is 70 participants by 14 development clips. Cross five existing disjoint 14-person groups with two deterministic material folds. Each material fold holds one title from each of the six paired categories; training retains the other six titles plus singleton Fear and Inspiration. Each of the ten cells therefore fits 56 people by eight titles (448 rows) and predicts 14 people by six titles (84 rows). Fitted and held people and titles are disjoint within each cell, while every evaluated category remains supported in training.

Across the ten cells each participant is evaluated once for each of the twelve paired-category titles, giving 840 distinct held predictions per candidate. Fear and Inspiration remain training-only categories in this diagnostic. Their absence from held outcomes is a limitation, not evidence of successful transfer. Material allocation must use deterministic metadata before new feature or target values, and both clips and catalogue-family identities must be checked for training/held overlap.

Three representations (context only, context plus late EEG, context plus distributed EEG) crossed with the three declared alphas and ten cells yield exactly 90 CV Ridge fits. Category medians add ten prior constructions rather than Ridge fits. Source transforms and priors are estimated only from each cell's training rows; held participants, held titles and their targets cannot enter a scaler or category median.

The coordinating agent adopted the following rules before execution. The primary coverage diagnostic is the late-versus-distributed contrast at fixed alpha 100, the previously qualified spectral Ridge setting. A secondary nomination chooses a single common alpha by the equal-weight mean of the three representation-specific MAEs over the same 840 held identities. Ties use the corresponding mean MSE, then the declared ascending alpha order (0.1, 10, 100). Compare late and distributed coverage at that common alpha, and nominate the coverage finalist by its held MAE, then MSE, then a late-only tie preference. This symmetric rule does not choose a separate alpha for whichever coverage happens to look better. All three recipes receive each of the same three alpha settings; the standalone context control remains constrained to the common alpha in each matched contrast. This is a matched readout comparison, not a claim that every comparator has received independently optimal tuning. Preserve every alpha/representation score and the category-median reference so this restriction is visible. The fixed-alpha diagnostic and adaptively nominated coverage must remain separate in reporting.

The three models must use exactly the same fitted rows, finite target values, intercept, solver, clipping and alpha. The first 13 joint columns must have the identical means/scales and transformed values as the standalone context scaler; concatenating EEG cannot alter context normalization. Require an explicit numerical or exact-byte check, not merely reliance on the columnwise behavior of `StandardScaler`. A full-source fit of the three frozen selected-alpha candidates is at most three additional fits. Any such fits are development qualification, not held-out scores, and require the fixed cap and model-state receipts.
