# Independent audit of FACED development target, context and pairing diagnosis

12 October 2026, Asia/Karachi. **The completed public numerical audit passes.** The experiment remains exploratory development evidence. Its crossed source context-selection recipe has an important category-coverage limitation; it does not establish a strongest category-informed baseline or an independently confirmed EEG advantage.

## Category coverage limits the first context-selection recipe

Each rotation's eight source titles contains **exactly one title per assigned emotion category**. Across all 60 person-by-title held cells, both held categories are absent from that cell's training categories. Consequently:

- Category-median held predictions fall back to the training global median; their CV scores are identical.
- A category-indicator linear model cannot identify a coefficient for a category absent from its training cells.
- The coarse-valence median wins this declared source-selection recipe in all three rotations, but that result does **not** mean it is the strongest category-informed comparator for held films whose assigned categories are represented in the final source fit.

This is a limitation of the selection contract rather than leakage or a numerical failure. The original experiment and its outputs must be retained. A separately declared, category-preserving source-person qualification can address this mismatch. Its source score will still involve source films seen during fitting and must not be described as independent new-title performance.

## Boundaries and next dependency

The audit reproduces scores, selections, median priors, target decomposition, donor identities, neural cached-correction swaps and full-panel interval calculations. **It does not independently refit Ridge, read its private states/features, recompute spectral swap values for changed inputs, or forward-replay validation EEG.** Those spectral values are supported by inspected immutable runner code and the original prediction reconstruction, with their public metrics independently checked. The neural validation diagnostic is a transformation of authenticated cached raw predictions; only source fixtures have independent state-to-window replay.

Full source feature-cache authentication is distinct from a fresh all-980 source waveform/QC replay. No whole-trial coverage experiment or longer neural convergence experiment occurred here. The prior manuscripts, their PDFs, original report, independent confirmation reservation and official research question remain unchanged.

The immediate scientific dependency is category-supported source context qualification, followed by interpretation of all retained context and pairing controls. These results alone do not justify opening protected outcomes, replacing the paper's research question, or expanding the encoder grid.
