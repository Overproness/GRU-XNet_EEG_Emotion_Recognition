# FACED baseline strengthening: code and metadata feasibility

12 October 2026. Source code, public metadata and existing public aggregate receipts were inspected. No private array, original signal, original rating or protected outcome was opened for this assessment. The current papers and every frozen experiment remain unchanged. This note recommends a diagnostic under the approved retrospective-valence evaluation question; it does not activate supervised fitting or confirmation.

## Most useful small next step

Test **distributed temporal coverage before adding another encoder**. The target is a whole-clip retrospective valence report, but both existing EEG anchors use only end−30 to end−2 seconds. This limits interpretation of an uncertain advantage: the selected late observation may omit relevant experience earlier in the clip. A coverage control improves baseline adequacy without inventing a new architecture or changing the target. It cannot guarantee an EEG gain or architectural novelty.

Keep the exact qualified 32-second/30-channel processor. Prepare three equally long observations at the beginning, midpoint and end of each permitted source video span, each producing seven four-second windows after the two-second boundary trims. Compare the existing 150-dimensional late spectral vector against the arithmetic mean of the three regional 150-dimensional vectors. The distributed representation retains the same feature dimension and readout family; it does not triple the number of independently labelled trials. Region overlap and source-film support must be reported.

This is **distributed coverage**, not full-video EEG. For the shortest permitted clip, the three observations overlap almost entirely. Longer clips still contain unsampled intervals. Current source adapters and QC routines explicitly enforce `(30, 32 * rate)` and `(7, 30, 512)`; passing a variable-length whole recording through them would invalidate the qualification. Genuine complete-video processing needs a separately qualified variable-length filtering, boundary, interpolation, aggregation and availability policy. There is no reason to begin with that larger implementation.

## Prepared metadata preflight and source pilot

The [inactive plan](../../results/development/faced_temporal_coverage_preflight_2026-10-12/plan.json) contains only the **980 source pairs** (70 people × 14 development titles). It checks 2,940 native sample ranges and requires all 980 late ranges to equal the exact prior source ranges. Source marker spans are 34.108–129.417 seconds. The 420 source trials at 250 Hz and 560 at 1,000 Hz are preserved; the 20 validation and 33 protected participants are denied by the guard. These are metadata facts, not numerical signal qualification.

Ranges are fixed before any signal access:

- Early begins at `ceil(marker_start * native_rate)`.
- Middle begins at `floor((ceil(marker_start * native_rate) + floor(marker_end * native_rate) - 32 * native_rate) / 2)`.
- Late begins at `floor(marker_end * native_rate) - 32 * native_rate`.
- Every endpoint is beginning plus `32 * native_rate`; every range stays inside its own marker span and recording geometry.

A deterministic metadata-only pilot selects eight source trials, one per elicitation category, covering all three acquisition groups. Its frozen 24 segments contain 16,560,000 native sample values across 30 scalp channels. Retaining all 24 processed float32 tensors would require only 10,321,920 bytes (9.84 MiB). The pilot covers seven distinct source participants. Native intervals, original-file/header hashes, selected channels and prior late count/window/QC hashes are included for the execution backend. No target or previously favourable signal quality chooses pilot identities.

The pure [guard module](../../gruxnet/faced_temporal_coverage.py) rejects validation/protected people, other clips, other source trials, changed ranges and changed rates before a signal-reader callback. Twenty-six focused synthetic tests pass. [Preparation](../../scripts/prepare_faced_temporal_coverage_2026_10_12.py) opened no raw, private or outcome file. Its declaration remains `prepared_source_only_not_activated`; the root agent prepares a separately frozen actual source-QC worker.

The actual pilot should authenticate originals and calibration; decode only the declared 30-channel ranges; replay late count/window/QC bytes; apply the existing processor and engineering rules separately to early/middle observations; report every failure and interpolation decision. No source rating, validation signal or fit is needed. Its first decision is whether this broader observation is technically supportable under the fixed policy. If it fails, preserve the evidence and resolve the acquisition/QC issue before fitting. Do not pick a different trial or threshold from favourable outcomes.

## Source-only selection must preserve category support

The fourteen development titles provide two titles each for Anger, Disgust, Sadness, Amusement, Joy and Tenderness, and one each for Fear and Inspiration. Earlier eight-title rotation fits have only one source title per category. Holding titles within those eight-title sets removes their categories and disadvantages the category comparator; that historical design is already qualified in the revised paper.

A feasible next diagnostic can use the **70-person, fourteen-title source pool** for selection. Make two deterministic material folds: each holds one title from each of the six paired categories and retains the other six plus the two singleton-category titles. Cross these with the existing five disjoint person folds. Each of the ten cells fits 56 people × eight titles (448 trials) and evaluates 14 people × six titles (84 trials), without sharing fitted people or fitted titles with that cell's held rectangle. Every evaluated category is present in training. Fear and Inspiration cannot supply unseen-material selection outcomes under this source roster and must be labelled train-only categories for this diagnostic.

Keep selection small: late and distributed representations, three fixed Ridge alphas (for example 0.1, 10 and 100), plus the same three alphas for the 13-column context control. This is at most **90 source-CV Ridge fits**, followed by at most three full-source fits. Retain all source-cell scores and identities; choose using equal person/title MAE and deterministic ties. Category medians are mandatory comparators, not validation-selected replacements. In every matched EEG contrast, source rows, context columns, scaling, intercept, alpha and unavailable-EEG fallback must agree. A separate primary category comparator measures whole-recipe utility rather than EEG-specific information.

This design is a proposal, not implemented or activated by the preflight. Its folds and caps must be frozen before scores are computed. Existing source ratings were previously used, so these source folds are **development selection**, not fresh confirmation. Training a finalist on all fourteen source titles makes subsequent evaluation on the twenty development people a familiar-material/held-person diagnostic. It must not be called unseen-material testing. Existing three rotations can remain historical sensitivity panels; they do not become independent repetitions. Only the untouched reservation can provide new independently held people and title families.

## Neural optimization: one bounded remedy, conditional on need

The existing 20-fit EEGNet run completed and was audited, but five of six selected panels still improved appreciably in source MSE from epochs 60 to 120. That flag establishes a training-budget limitation, not that later epochs improve held data. The established regression adaptation has 1,841 parameters, fixed dropout, seven-window trial averaging, source-only channel scaling and AdamW. Its scalar adaptation is local; it is not a reproduction of published classification scores.

If the coverage/source-selection diagnostic remains inconclusive and a stronger neural baseline is required, qualify **one longer residual-EEGNet schedule selected on source-held people/materials**. For example, retain one documented learning rate, two seeds and checkpoints 120/240/360; use two prespecified source cells, then two full-source fits after selection (six neural fits total). Keep loss and trial count fixed, qualify source OOF offsets for the reduced training roster, and report every checkpoint's source and held-cell learning. The current runner hardcodes five OOF groups and fixed readout schedules; this experiment therefore needs a new qualified runner rather than edits to the old sealed engine. No new pretrained encoder grid or silent schedule extension is justified now.

Twenty 120-epoch historical fits used 1.275 hours of charged fit/checkpoint time and 160 MiB peak PyTorch reservation on the RTX 3050 laptop. A rough workload calculation for the proposed four 448-trial plus two 980-trial 360-epoch fits is `(4 * 448 + 2 * 980) * 360 / (20 * 560 * 120) = 1.005`, approximately 1.28 GPU-hours using historical fit time. This is an order-of-magnitude planning estimate, not a benchmark: preparation, fold offsets, validation overhead and future nondeterminism are additional. A four-hour fit cap and 5.5-billion-byte reserved-memory cap would be reasonable to qualify, not promise. The final schedule must be justified and frozen before fitting.

## Resource and interpretation boundaries

All three processed source regions together require 1,264,435,200 float32 bytes (1.18 GiB), within the old two-billion-byte window cap. Spectral-only extraction can process one region at a time and retain only the 150-dimensional feature vectors; it need not retain this entire waveform tensor. A subsequent available-validation stage would add preparation cost and must keep the fifteen previously unavailable trials through a fixed matched fallback. Do not fetch those EEG inputs merely because a new observation might make them usable. The pilot opens none of the validation inputs.

Acquisition group, category, duration and presentation order remain supplied inference metadata. Reusing existing signal-quality measures as nuisance predictors could be a later bounded robustness comparison, but amplitude or line-power features are not authenticated artifact labels. Their performance would not prove that EEG benefit is non-cerebral. Auxiliary EOG channels were not included in the qualified 30-channel decoder; using them requires separate role/unit/channel/source qualification. Avoid expanding the immediate pilot into an unbounded artifact/representation search.

The current limitation is addressed in two distinct ways: the small baseline control makes the exploratory finding better tested; a later locked, adequately reviewed untouched evaluation addresses development reuse. Neither alone creates a novel architecture or proves lack of emotion information. A stronger contribution would be a reproducible incremental finding with correctly specified comparators and scope, or a sufficiently precise bounded result under strong baselines. Protected outcomes remain sealed until a concrete finalist, interpretation and confirmation decision are approved.
