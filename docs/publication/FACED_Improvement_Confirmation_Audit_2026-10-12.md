## The two weaknesses require different remedies

Independent outcomes can address **development reuse** if the complete prediction recipe, target, access policy, estimand and reporting rule are fixed before those outcomes are accessed. More seeds, rotations, windows, alternative heads or additional analyses of already opened validation ratings cannot supply that independence. A new participant panel on known titles supports person transfer; new participants on held catalogue titles support that combined separation for this roster.

Independent evaluation alone does **not** supply a new architectural or methodological contribution. It can strengthen an empirical evaluation if the fixed question is useful, closest methods are qualified and the result is sufficiently informative. A positive gain for the same established Ridge recipe would be an independently supported dataset-specific utility result. An uncertain gain would still be useful honest evidence, but would leave a stronger-venue claim limited. A negative result cannot be transformed into evidence that EEG carries no useful information.

The most defensible immediate improvement is a clearly delimited, reproducible comparison of **adding EEG to the same contextual prediction recipe**, with preserved acquisition failures and properly matched information inputs. Whether this is original enough requires the separate closest-work assessment and preferably independently qualified evidence on another public corpus. The present single-corpus audit does not establish that novelty.

## Estimand and precision should match the actual material roster

The existing plan's crossed person/title percentile intervals are explicitly descriptive. Crossed resampling has established theoretical motivation, but an eight-title, deliberately chosen roster does not thereby acquire calibrated population coverage. [Owen's pigeonhole bootstrap](https://arxiv.org/abs/0712.1111) motivates separate axis resampling; [Owen and Eckles](https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.2125) give variance properties under stated conditions. Neither source guarantees exact finite-roster percentile confidence for this FACED design.

A proposed new protocol can distinguish two targets:

1. **New participants on this fixed catalogue.** First compute paired absolute-loss differences for every clip, average clips within each person/title family, then average the eight families equally within each participant. The primary estimate is the mean of the resulting thirty-three participant differences. Participant-level uncertainty, with a predeclared procedure and explicit participant-sampling assumptions, conditions on these exact films and trained states. Crossed person/title resampling and leave-one-family effects can remain descriptive material-sensitivity analyses. This narrower estimand avoids treating the films as an authenticated random sample. It does not resolve participant representativeness or model-training uncertainty.
2. **New participants and arbitrary new films.** The same reservation cannot support a broad material-population claim without stronger sampling and much more material support. Eight families and unauthenticated original-footage overlap remain limits. Extra seeds or trials within a film do not create new film families. A qualified independent public corpus with suitable individual ratings and material identities would be the more useful extension.

Changing the primary uncertainty procedure would require the new version before outcome access; it must not be substituted into the already frozen proposal silently. A participant-conditional interval is not a narrower estimate of the identical random-title estimand.

The completed synthetic calculation used assumed, uncalibrated variance components. At the actual reserved dimensions, an assumed gain of 0.045 gave positive lower bounds in only **8.2–19.5%** of simulations across the four scenarios; interval widths depended on whether variability was assigned to people or titles. Those figures are not empirical FACED power, and they do not clear the existing precision gate. They explain why “one more holdout will fix the paper” is an unjustified promise.

Before a new evaluation is activated, specify an information goal such as the range of gains that would change a practical conclusion, and conduct an outcome-free precision review for that goal. A value chosen only to rescue a favorable development result is not a justified margin. The previous 0.15 engineering screen is not a validated perceptual or clinical threshold. If reasonable uncertainty remains too broad, report the limited-roster estimate honestly or qualify more independent materials. Do not claim equivalence from a zero-crossing interval; equivalence would require a justified margin and an appropriate, prospectively fixed inferential design.

## Concrete preparation needed before any protected access

The present contract implements guards and pure numerical helpers. It does not implement a complete final-source fitting, recording extraction or inference backend. The following bounded preparation can be completed before consuming the reservation:

1. **Create a versioned decision and execution plan.** Bind the saved paper hashes, exact source/validation/confirmation roles, target item, catalogue grouping, signal interval, inference information and QC policy. Preserve the inactive v1 packet. State which of the two purposes above is intended and what evidence would make the result informative. Do not claim that manuscript approval alone activates it.
2. **Qualify an actual source-only backend.** Authenticate all 980 source cache identities and QC records; reject protected identities before invoking any reader. Validate feature ordering, units, finite values, context columns and source-only transforms. Independently replay declared source waveform/feature fixtures and QC/fallback boundary cases. Complete-cache identity qualification plus disclosed partial waveform replay is a practical scope; a fresh all-980 waveform replay must not be claimed unless actually performed.
3. **Fit and qualify the exact final source states only under the appropriate new declaration.** For the old candidate this means two Ridge fits and one median construction, not a grid. Bind source rows, scalers, coefficients, feature names and prior values. Verify that the context-only scaler matches the context portion of the joint scaler and that both use alpha 100, their declared intercept, clipping and solver conventions. Source predictions must replay from the saved states. Validation or protected ratings may not enter fitting, selection or priors.
4. **Exercise the real export pipeline using source or manufactured fixtures.** Qualify exact export bytes against prediction-only value projections. Test duplicate/missing/wrong-role trials, changed predictions, nonfinite values, incorrect title grouping, altered model states and early rating conversion. Test that unavailable EEG calls no EEG backend and gives the same frozen fallback to joint and matched context. These checks should cover actual file readers and serializers, not only pure helper arrays.
5. **Obtain an independent pre-access review and a separately recorded activation.** Bind the scientific purpose, precision review, source qualification and final-state/export receipts. Old outstanding criteria must either be genuinely satisfied or explicitly replaced by the reviewed new protocol; historical false gates remain historical false gates.
6. **Separate protected signal and target phases.** After permitted signal preparation, retain every declared identity, including acquisition failures. Lock all complete unfamiliar and familiar prediction exports for all declared methods, with state hashes, file hashes and prediction-only digests, before the first protected rating conversion. Commit the prediction lock, then score once under the declared analysis. Invalid identities, targets or caps stop the attempt with the partial ledger preserved. No favorable-outcome retry, retuning or role reassignment follows.

The historical unavailable-input fallback was full-context prediction. The dormant final proposal instead shares the source category prior between joint and matched context. These are distinct recipes. Whatever the new purpose chooses must be declared and kept common for the matched comparison; all failed EEG trials remain in its denominator and have zero matched EEG gain under that common fallback.

Fear and Inspiration each have only one source title even in the 14-title source rectangle. A material-holdout source fold cannot retain their category-specific source support. This restricts unseen-material selection and should be visible in the protocol rather than hidden by person-only CV. Any proposed alternative source selection must disclose its supported categories and leave singleton-category transfer unqualified. A source-only improved utility lead is necessary if the unchanged old continuation screen is retained; it is not logically necessary for a separately justified independent evaluation of uncertain performance. More open-ended encoder expansion is unnecessary to make that protocol decision.
