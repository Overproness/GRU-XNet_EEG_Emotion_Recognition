# Improving the FACED paper: contribution hypotheses and closest work

Checked 12 October 2026, Asia/Karachi. This is a literature and design review for improving the approved exploratory paper. It does not modify a saved manuscript, adopt another target, run a model, access raw/private EEG or new ratings, open confirmation, download a corpus, submit a paper or send an external message. Public datasets remain the only acquisition route.

## What can actually be improved

The two weaknesses have different remedies. Reused development outcomes can be addressed by a genuinely untouched evaluation after freezing the procedure, or by a separately qualified public corpus reserved before outcomes are examined. More rotations, random seeds, windows, retrospective nested cross-validation or a new split of already examined people do not erase outcome-aware exploration. Such analyses can qualify a recipe but remain development evidence.

Originality requires a useful, distinct, supported scientific finding. An independently confirmed result about how much EEG adds under a clearly stated information and target contract can strengthen this case; a new network name or replacing a recurrent layer with a Transformer cannot establish it. An adverse or inconclusive confirmation must also be reported. Independent confirmation improves credibility and may leave originality modest.

The current paper is already clearer than the historical GRU-XNet draft. Its [reviewed evidence](../../paper/exploratory_evaluation_2026-10-12/README.md) includes source-supported category priors, matched contextual predictors and constrained EEG pairing. Spectral gain over the category prior averages 0.0446 MAE, the neural gain 0.0052; all six descriptive unfamiliar-title intervals include zero. The new retrospective matched-alpha sensitivity also has intervals including zero. This does not establish equivalence, negligible information or a robust increment. All analyses still use the development panels.

The [inactive confirmation design](FACED_Confirmation_Freeze_Findings_2026-10-12.md) reserves 33 participants and eight catalogue-title families. It has no activated final fitting backend and its variance simulations are assumptions, not empirical power. The historical 0.15 engineering margin is not a validated perceptual or publication threshold. Confirmation preparation therefore remains necessary even if no further model is proposed.

## Three bounded contribution hypotheses

These are questions to qualify, not adopted claims. Only one should become the main question; the remainder are controls or later replication. A pilot using reused development outcomes cannot decide its own independent evidence.

### 1. Coverage qualification followed by a fixed incremental prediction test

**Question:** does late-window sampling limit useful prediction of an individual whole-clip valence report, and does a fixed broader-coverage EEG recipe provide an increment beyond the strongest admissible contextual predictor on untouched people/materials?

Use a small source/development-only comparison between the existing late interval and a marker-qualified whole-trial aggregate. Keep spectral definitions, channels, preprocessing, targets, source-only scaling, regression family and selection rules matched. Include the existing late representation as the historical anchor, a full-context EEG-free predictor and the source-category prior. Do not search for a favorable “emotional” second or tune windows against reserved ratings. Treat pooling extrema or temporal bins as established features, with no emotional-peak interpretation.

If healthy source learning and source/development held-person/material evidence support a fixed recipe, freeze it before independent confirmation. A possible new finding is whether this specific sampling assumption changes EEG's incremental utility for FACED retrospective valence. The representation, pooling and residual learning are established methods. A null coverage result is useful qualification but leaves little basis for expanding another architecture grid.

**Gates:** original marker-relative onset/end and valid interval access; source-first feature/QC preparation; fixed all-trial scoring and unavailable-input fallback; source-only category support; finite predefined feature/fit budget; no implicit change from retrospective valence to momentary affect. Reserved data remain sealed throughout the pilot. FACED material identities are catalogue titles until physical footage is authenticated.

### 2. Confirm the information-contract finding rather than claim a novel decoder

**Question:** under available elicitation category and acquisition/context metadata, what prediction gain is independently supported from correctly paired EEG for this fixed retrospective task?

The current development finding motivates this question; it does not answer it independently. Qualify the existing spectral recipe and one small neural reference, with the documented learning/convergence limitation resolved or explicitly retained. Fix an EEG-plus-context predictor, its same-alpha/scaler/context-only comparator and the source-category prior. Freeze prediction export before outcome decoding. Preserve all acquisition failures in each denominator, and state which contrasts measure whole-recipe utility versus adding EEG features.

A possible publishable result is a confirmed, task-bounded increment with heterogeneity and pairing support, or a sufficiently precise bound on the tested recipe's practical increment. Neither direction is novel solely because it uses confidence intervals. Equivalence needs an independently justified useful-effect margin and adequate precision; intervals containing zero do not supply it. Person/title resampling must respect the fixed material roster and crossed observations. Overlapping development rotations are not independent confirmations.

**Gates:** qualified backend, source transformations and target join; no model/comparator reselection after scoring; justified endpoint/margin; fixed missing-input policy; honest limited-title scope. Pairing degradation is a predictive dependency diagnostic and cannot prove a cerebral emotional mechanism. A change to category-unavailable deployment or participant label calibration is another information contract and requires a separate explicit proposal.

### 3. Replicate the fixed context-versus-EEG finding on another suitable public corpus

**Question:** does a fixed native-target protocol show the same contextual advantage or EEG increment under independently acquired participants, materials and hardware?

This is the strongest potential external-validity improvement, but it is a qualification dependency rather than permission to download and fit every dataset. Choose one suitable corpus, preserve its native individual report scale, build corpus-specific source-only contextual controls, authenticate waveform/annotation/timing and reserve people/materials before outcomes. Differences in rating scales and available metadata require explicit estimands; one pooled cross-corpus MAE is inappropriate. A second corpus is not evidence of transfer unless a model is actually transferred under a declared information and scaling contract.

The contribution would be reproducibility or boundary conditions of this defined prediction question. Generic cross-dataset evaluation is established, including in Pandilova and mdJPT. Additional participants with overlapping clips improve acquisition replication without certifying unseen-content replication. More windows cannot create more independent films.

## Recommended immediate order

1. Preserve the reviewed paper and its receipts; do not revise its claims from proposed experiments.
2. Complete an identity-only, source-only feasibility check for hypothesis 1 and the existing independent-confirmation backend. Freeze a bounded coverage comparison before any permitted new feature/outcome arithmetic. If the representation cannot be matched or timing cannot be authenticated, use the existing qualified recipe rather than silently redefine inputs.
3. Present the source/development evidence, exact candidate claim, strongest comparator and precision limits to the author. Agree one question before its adoption. A stronger venue is a reason for stronger controls, not for selecting a favorable endpoint.
4. Freeze and execute independent confirmation once the procedure and gates are qualified. Report it regardless of sign; source fitting and prediction export precede outcome decoding. Another large encoder search should require new positive evidence.
5. Qualify one backup public corpus in parallel using metadata only. DREAMER/AMIGOS access and DENS/EmoEEG-MC joins are dependencies; they are not currently ready for supervised replication. No external access requests or follow-ups are sent without authorization.

This can address the reuse weakness and make a sharper empirical contribution possible. It cannot guarantee that EEG improves prediction or that a better venue accepts the paper. If the independently supported finding remains small or imprecise, retain the exploratory manuscript and report the scope honestly rather than relabeling development evidence as confirmation.
