# FACED independent benchmark: claim and precision decision

13 October 2026, Asia/Karachi. **Assessment complete; confirmation remains inactive.** Existing papers, archives, the approved research question, participant/material assignments and all prior activation gates are preserved. This decision uses public source results and metadata only. It authorizes no protected signal or rating access, new fitting, model search or manuscript revision.

## Decision

**A limited independent performance estimate is scientifically defensible. An EEG-superiority confirmation is not supported by the present development results, and the precision of the complete eight-family benchmark is not certified.** Independently measured results could strengthen the exploratory paper's credibility; they would not, by themselves, resolve limited novelty or establish a strong-conference contribution.

Proceed next with qualification of the already fitted source states, inference/export path and source/manufactured-data backend. Keep confirmation inactive until a concrete package establishes the implementation, resource scope and prediction locks, and the limited estimation purpose and unresolved precision assumptions are explicitly accepted. This is preparation within the existing retrospective-valence/context question, not adoption of a new research question. No previous failed continuation gate is cleared by this assessment.

## Exact candidate claim

The proposed question is: **for previously unused FACED participants on the fixed reserved catalogue, what mean absolute-error change does the source-selected distributed spectral-plus-context Ridge produce relative to its matched contextual Ridge?**

The frozen candidate is distributed joint Ridge at common alpha 100. The target is item-10 retrospective whole-clip valence on its original 0–7 scale. The primary reserved arm contains 33 participants, nine clips in eight catalogue title families, and 297 clip outcomes. Clips 7 and 8 share a family. Compute each method's absolute clip errors first, average within each participant/family, weight the eight families equally within each participant, then average the 33 participant gains. Positive gain means matched-context loss minus distributed-joint loss is positive.

The practical source-category median and the common-alpha late joint model must both remain reported. Matching context consumes the shared EEG-availability routing flag; it is not an acquisition-free predictor. Category medians are the practical context-only reference. Both EEG representations require all three regional acquisitions under unchanged quality rules. Their contrast concerns representation coverage, not acquisition latency or burden. Shared fallback retains every outcome and gives zero paired EEG/coverage gains on unavailable trials.

A primary interval entirely above zero would support this particular recipe relative to this particular matching control on this catalogue. Practical benefit additionally requires the category-prior comparison. An interval entirely below zero would support worse mean performance by this recipe within that scope. An interval crossing zero would leave superiority uncertain. None establishes equivalence, absent EEG information, emotion-specific neural encoding, an adequately optimized deep-learning baseline, arbitrary-film generalization or a peak-end mechanism.

## What the completed source experiment establishes

The matched source evaluation contains 70 participants and 840 distinct held participant/clip pairs, drawn from 12 titles in six categories. Source-only selection nominated distributed joint at alpha 100, but repeated earlier development makes that nomination adaptive. It is not independent confirmation.

| Method at frozen common alpha | Source MAE |
| --- | ---: |
| Practical category median | 1.30397 |
| Matching contextual Ridge | 1.36650 |
| Late spectral-plus-context Ridge | 1.46462 |
| Distributed spectral-plus-context Ridge | 1.44554 |

Distributed coverage improves over late by **0.01907** MAE, while distributed EEG is worse than matched context by **0.07904** and category medians by **0.14157**. Every evaluated category has an adverse mean matched EEG gain. Matching context at alpha 100 is not its individually optimized source setting: its alpha-0.1 MAE is 1.34496. Preserve that qualification rather than implying the primary control is the best achievable contextual regressor. The category benchmark further prevents a favorable future comparison to the matched control from being mistaken for practical superiority.

Fear and Inspiration have no material-held source transfer observations. Their clips were training only. Source training rows and titles also differ from the final fixed source state and reserved catalogue. No current source summary establishes the sign or variance of the future effect.

## Precision assessment

The [planning protocol](FACED_Benchmark_Precision_Plan_2026-10-13.md), code, tests and operative v2 declaration were committed and pushed at `de865da42` before the analysis. The preserved initial v1 declaration was not executed. A global identity/role/context barrier qualified all 8,400 public source rows before the fixed panels' 3,360 numeric rows were interpreted. Seventeen manufactured tests passed. The [independent audit](FACED_Benchmark_Claim_Precision_Independent_Review_2026-10-13.md) reconstructed the gains, sample SDs, 36 hypothetical sample counts and 15 unsupported-category scenarios; maximum arithmetic discrepancy was 2.22e-16.

| Fixed contrast; positive favors distributed EEG | Source mean gain | SD of 70 participant gains | Source-SD planning halfwidth for 33 people |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Matched context minus distributed loss | −0.07904 | 0.17206 | 0.06101 |
| Category median minus distributed loss | −0.14157 | 0.29094 | 0.10316 |
| Late joint minus distributed loss | +0.01907 | 0.10846 | 0.03846 |

These halfwidths use `t(0.975,32) × source_SD / sqrt(33)`. They are planning proxies, not confidence intervals on the development means or a guarantee about the reserved sample. Source participant errors share fitted CV models, source outcomes have been repeatedly inspected, each source person is scored across two material branches, and those 448-row fits differ from the fixed 980-row state. Future titles, category weights and acquisition failures can change both means and variances. **The independent unit for the proposed primary interval is the participant, not each of the 297 clip outcomes.**

The two unsupported categories receive one quarter of the future family weight. Under the explicit, unqualified assumption that the other six families retain the source SD, assigning SD 2 to the unsupported-category mean gives illustrative halfwidths **0.18310** at zero correlation and **0.22305** at correlation 1. Neither that SD nor correlation is observed. Smaller grid widths can arise purely from assumed negative-correlation cancellation. The full predeclared sensitivity table and bounded-loss guard are retained in the [precision assessment](FACED_Benchmark_Precision_Assessment_2026-10-13.md). Do not select a favorable scenario as certified precision or extrapolate the future mean.

The planned participant-level 95% paired-t interval conditions on the exact films, frozen states and completed source selection. It assumes independent participant gains and a suitable approximate sampling distribution. It does not include retraining, adaptive-selection or arbitrary-film uncertainty, and convenience recruitment does not establish population representativeness. Family and crossed-resampling summaries are descriptive sensitivity analyses; they cannot replace the primary interval.

The old 0.15 engineering continuation screen remains unmet. The 0.05/0.10/0.15 planning resolutions are not clinical, perceptual or equivalence margins. This exercise neither guarantees a narrow interval nor demonstrates that a wider estimate would be scientifically worthless: its worth depends on reporting the restricted estimate honestly, regardless of sign.

## Novelty and remaining scientific limitations

The [closest-method assessment](FACED_Benchmark_Closest_Method_Assessment_2026-10-13.md) documents established work on FACED stimulus confounds, individual-report targets, crossed person/video evaluation, contextual priors and retrospective EEG regression. A reproducible evaluation instance could be useful, but the components themselves supply no architectural novelty or first-use claim.

HEART's [author university record](https://recerca.udg.edu/ca/publications/heart-hybrid-embedding-for-affective-and-riemannian-trajectories/) lists VAD regression and categorical classification and names FACED among its datasets. Its inaccessible full methods leave the exact FACED target and evaluation branch unresolved. This is potential close overlap, not proof of an identical individual-FACED-valence regression experiment. Neither a first-FACED-regression claim nor a claim that HEART already performed this exact benchmark is qualified.

The principal limitations remain a small fixed catalogue, adaptive reused development, unqualified transfer for two categories, unauthenticated physical footage independence and playback alignment, incomplete category-annotation ancestry, and unresolved neural convergence/stronger regression baselines. Condition conclusions on the supplied catalogue/context metadata. Catalogue title separation does not certify independent footage, prior exposure or entirely independent upstream annotation. A Ridge-only confirmation cannot settle the effectiveness of optimized EEG or deep learning generally. No source missing-EEG trial occurs in the actual held panel; inference fallback therefore still needs manufactured qualification.
