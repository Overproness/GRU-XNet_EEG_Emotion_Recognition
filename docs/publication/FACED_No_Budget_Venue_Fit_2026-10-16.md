# Budget-independent venue fit for the current FACED paper

Checked 16 October 2026. This assessment reads the approved 15 October exploratory manuscript and current official publisher policies. It ignores publication cost as requested. It is a qualitative editorial-fit assessment, not an acceptance-probability estimate. No manuscript, reserved data or experiment was changed; nothing was submitted or sent externally.

The current paper is a transparent, single-corpus exploratory evaluation with context controls, adverse follow-up EEG results and learning-capacity diagnostics. It does not establish a general EEG null, an optimized decoder comparison or independent replication. A venue that explicitly welcomes valid negative findings is a better editorial match than one primarily seeking a new architecture or major methodological advance. Such policies do not waive scientific-quality requirements.

## Recommended options

| Option | Current-paper fit and rationale | Remaining obstacle |
|---|---|---|
| **PLOS ONE** — best balance of a full research article and validity-based editorial criteria | Its official publication criteria explicitly consider negative/null findings and original engineering research. They emphasize technical rigor, appropriate controls/replication and conclusions supported by data. This accommodates a narrowly interpreted exploratory evaluation more naturally than an architecture novelty claim. | Reused development outcomes and the single later neural cell still invite reviewer criticism; make the operational question, adaptive history, comparator limitations and reproducibility explicit. Public-dataset reuse needs a justified scientific question, not just another model application. |
| **BMC Research Notes** — closest explicit format for a deliberately bounded research note | Its current scope includes initial observations, valid negative results and methodological observations across engineering/science. Its Research note criteria explicitly include additional controls, replication and null findings. This closely matches a modest, carefully delimited observation. | Requires note-specific structure: **200-word Objective/Results abstract, 2,000-word combined introduction/main text/limitations, and at most three main tables/figures**. Supporting material may hold additional details. The current paper has a 209-word abstract and three main displays plus five supplementary displays, so its display allocation already fits; abstract structure/length, note headings and declarations need adaptation. Retain complete evidence in the supplement. |
| **Journal of Medical and Biological Engineering (JMBE)** — focused biomedical-signals option | Biomedical signals are explicitly in scope. Its reviewer criteria ask whether the manuscript is scientifically sound, coherent, clear and nonduplicative. The current draft already approximates its 3,000-word/eight-display limits. | Its scope is not an explicit invitation for limited negative notes. The contribution must explain what EEG benchmark practice learns from this case, and editors still screen suitability before review. Existing formatting is convenience, not acceptance evidence. |
| **Scientific Reports** — plausible broader full-length alternative | Its current editorial process emphasizes technical quality, robust methods/analysis/statistics/ethics and data-supported interpretation, leaving the community to judge impact. Its official editorial-board guidance explicitly accommodates negative findings and scientifically justified replications. | The same unresolved comparison/generalization issues apply. Do not infer that the Nature Portfolio label makes it a Nature flagship or that impact-independent screening guarantees review/acceptance. |

My suggested choice is **PLOS ONE if retaining the full research-article treatment is the priority**, or **BMC Research Notes if the priority is the most directly matched modest observation format**. JMBE remains a defensible focused alternative. This ordering reflects fit, not measured venue acceptance rates. No defensible manuscript-specific acceptance percentage is available from these sources.

Before a BMC submission, qualify its written-consent requirement for this secondary public-data analysis. Its Research note instructions require written informed consent for human-subject research. FACED's [original article](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-023-02650-w) reports informed consent and approval THU201906; that statement alone does not authenticate the consent's written form or settle this secondary study's declaration. This is a documentation/policy check, not evidence of noncompliance or an automatic reason to exclude the journal. Do not invent a new NUST exemption or assert written consent without support.

The current source-based main-body check gives 1,727 alphanumeric tokens after excluding citations, float text, declarations and bibliography. This suggests the 2,000-word note limit is feasible without discarding findings; it is not a certified final submission-system count. The earlier BMC-format package is preserved under `paper/submission_note_2026-10-12/`, but its empirical presentation predates the completed later source diagnostics and should not be submitted unchanged.

The present results do not make top machine-learning or leading affective-computing venues realistic high-probability targets. Stronger method/comparator qualification and independent participant/material evidence would strengthen applications to more selective field venues, but those are future scientific work; the reserved cohort is not activated by this recommendation.
