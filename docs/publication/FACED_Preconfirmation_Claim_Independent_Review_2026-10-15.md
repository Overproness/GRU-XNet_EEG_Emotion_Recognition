## Recommendation

**Finish and audit the bounded neural diagnostic before deciding whether to spend the reserved cohort. Do not activate confirmation because the implementation has passed its checks.**

A four-recipe Ridge/category comparison can produce a worthwhile independent estimate for a modest applied or replication-oriented paper, if its authors value an adverse or inconclusive result enough to publish it. It has low expected value as a way to obtain a substantially stronger venue by itself. The source-selected spectral recipe currently trails both contextual references; independently estimating that recipe's performance does not resolve the effectiveness of adequately optimized neural prediction. This is a judgment about the present evidence and scope, not a prediction of the reserved result or a venue acceptance guarantee.

If the source neural diagnostic supplies a reproducible lead, do not adopt it from this one inspected cell. Propose bounded source replication and a concrete candidate-set amendment before considering reserved access. If optimization remains unresolved, describe that limitation plainly. If the diagnostic supplies no lead, the decision is between publishing a narrowly scoped, sign-agnostic benchmark and preserving the reservation while qualifying a more consequential scientific comparison. Another architecture search does not follow automatically.

## Defensible claim

The proposal can test the mean change in absolute prediction error produced by **these frozen spectral-plus-context and matched contextual Ridge recipes**, for original individual retrospective valence, under the declared acquisition policy and fixed reserved FACED catalogue. It can separately compare distributed against late coverage and the joint predictor against the acquisition-free category prior. Independently measured adverse performance would be an informative failure of that recipe; it would not establish that EEG lacks emotion information.

One qualification should be more prominent in the proposal: the matching contextual Ridge at common alpha100 is not its best observed source setting. The preserved decision reports context MAE1.34496 at alpha0.1, versus1.36650 at alpha100. Common regularization is useful for a controlled representation contrast, but cannot justify calling the comparator the best contextual predictor. The practical category-median contrast should therefore be central to a practical-utility claim, even though its inferential status remains secondary in the frozen design. Do not silently alter the primary comparison after reserved outcomes are known.

The participant interval conditions on the specified films, model states and source selection. Thirty-three participants do not become297 independent observations. Fear and Inspiration, which lack material-held source support, receive one quarter of primary family weight; the existing variance scenarios correctly leave future precision uncertified. Nothing here supports equivalence, an absence-of-information conclusion, arbitrary-film generalization, a temporal psychological mechanism or a first-FACED-regression claim.

## Changes that would materially strengthen the science

- **A stable predictive increment over credible contextual references:** a capacity-qualified reference, source-only selection, and bounded crossed participant/material replication before any amended confirmation. Outperforming one common-alpha control while trailing the practical prior is insufficient for a useful-decoder claim.
- **Independent empirical breadth:** a qualified public-data replication with compatible individual targets, authenticated timing and genuinely distinct stimuli, or better-qualified material independence within the current catalogue. More people watching the same small catalogue cannot substitute for material breadth. Dataset qualification must precede fitting.
- **A scientifically interpretable limitation:** demonstrate the scope and stability of a failure across adequately learned, bounded reference recipes, rather than claiming that one unsuccessful spectral decoder disproves useful EEG prediction. Neural tiny-sample capacity and permutation controls diagnose implementation/learning behavior; they do not establish generalization.
- **Target and acquisition interpretation:** disclose the retrospective whole-clip target versus sampled EEG intervals, unqualified category-annotation ancestry, timing/footage scope, and acquisition-failure policy. Resolve metadata or stimulus provenance where feasible without opening reserved outcomes. Model size cannot repair these scope limitations.

These improvements remain within the accepted individual-retrospective-valence/context question when they qualify the target, comparator or generalization evidence. Changing the target, adopting another dataset as the paper's main subject, or making a broader mechanism/absence-of-information question the central contribution would require a separate evidence-backed proposal and the user's approval.

## Necessary engineering versus scientific contribution

State replay, complete prediction locks, durable restart, label-blind failure routing, immutable resource budgets and negative tests are necessary safeguards. They make the eventual estimate reviewable and protect its interpretation. Passing them neither creates architectural novelty nor supplies an empirical discovery. Preserve this distinction in the submission: explain the safeguards concisely, then let independently measured findings and their limited scope carry the scientific claim.
