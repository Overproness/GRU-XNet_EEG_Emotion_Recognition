## Claim that the existing evidence can support

The completed spectral study supports an exploratory comparison of four specific recipes on reused source data. At common alpha 100, source-category median MAE was 1.30397, matched context 1.36650, late joint 1.46462 and distributed joint 1.44554. Distributed coverage improved over late by 0.01907 but was worse than context by 0.07904 and category medians by 0.14157. The [preserved source assessment](FACED_Benchmark_Precision_Assessment_2026-10-13.md) records the adaptive selection and six-category coverage limits. No independent superiority, equivalence or absence-of-EEG-information finding exists. The matching context at alpha 100 is not its best source setting: alpha 0.1 gave MAE 1.34496. The practical category contrast must therefore remain central to any utility interpretation.

The proposed independent question is: **For these reserved FACED participants and catalogue families, how does the frozen source-selected distributed spectral EEG-plus-context recipe change individual retrospective-valence prediction error relative to the matched contextual recipe?** The practical category-median comparator and equal-dimensional late-coverage control remain mandatory secondary comparisons. The empirical contribution would be the resulting independently reserved estimate and inspectable evidence, rather than an invented architecture, first-use claim or new general evaluation principle.

The [closest-method follow-up](FACED_HEART_Target_Qualification_2026-10-15.md) leaves HEART's exact individual FACED supervision and material reservation unresolved. Prior work already establishes participant separation, contextual confounds and temporal EEG representations. Our operational task can be specified precisely while priority over previous work remains unqualified. Do not compare categorical accuracies with this scalar MAE.

## What the stronger-reference diagnostic can change

The new EEGNet experiment uses an authenticated classifier backbone with disclosed local scalar/residual/preprocessing adaptations. It is a learning-capacity diagnostic on one availability-selected source cell, not the strongest decoder in the field. Both real/permuted tiny controls, both initializations and every fixed checkpoint must remain reportable. The ensemble after 360 epochs is fixed; held-score checkpoint selection is prohibited.

- If state/metric audits fail, hold interpretation and preserve the failure. A failed runtime or serializer supplies no evidence about EEG utility.
- If source learning is inadequate or still improving under the fixed schedule, report that optimization remains unresolved. The Ridge benchmark cannot then stand for adequately optimized neural prediction.
- If learning is adequate but held source gains are adverse or unstable, report this specific reference outcome without generalizing to all deep learning or EEG.
- A promising source increment motivates a separate bounded replication/candidate proposal. One reused source cell cannot select a new confirmation candidate or establish a reproducible lead on its own.

No outcome in this diagnostic silently modifies the four existing Ridge/category candidates. Generic pretrained models remain a separate provenance, exposure and continuous-target preprocessing qualification problem. Another encoder grid does not follow automatically.

## Exact proposed independent scope

Retain 33 participants, nine unfamiliar clips from eight catalogue families (297 primary outcomes) and fourteen familiar clips (462 secondary outcomes). All 759 trials and all four methods yield 3,036 target-free predictions before any reserved item-10 conversion. Preserve real roles/arms; do not reclassify original participants as synthetic fixtures. The primary gain is contextual absolute error minus distributed-joint absolute error, averaged within participant/family, then equally across eight families and 33 people. Positive gain favors the EEG recipe.

Shared three-region acquisition rules retain every trial. On unavailable EEG, the matching context and both joint recipes use the same source-category fallback; category medians are always the practical acquisition-free comparator. The matching context branch therefore consumes an EEG-availability flag and is not an independent no-acquisition control. Unknown/nonfinite/technical/resource errors stop the attempt rather than excluding trials or changing thresholds.

Participant t intervals condition on these fixed films and fitted states, with explicit participant-sampling assumptions. They omit arbitrary-film, retraining and adaptive-selection uncertainty. Fear and Inspiration lack material-held source estimates yet comprise one quarter of future family weight. Source-derived precision proxies do not certify the future interval. Catalogue identity is not verified footage independence, prior-viewing history or exact playback alignment. These limitations cannot be removed by making the model larger.
