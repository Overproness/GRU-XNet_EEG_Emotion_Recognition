## Scientific positioning for the revised manuscript

The defensible contribution is a reproducible, explicitly exploratory empirical case study of **incremental EEG prediction beyond available study metadata for individual retrospective FACED valence**, under new-participant familiar/unfamiliar catalogue-title development panels. It contributes this combination of target, information conditions, acquisition-aware denominator and diagnostic evidence, rather than introducing context control, crossed splitting, residual learning or EEGNet.

State the available evidence in the abstract and title: category predictions remain competitive with the tested EEG models, and consistent incremental advantage has not been established. The revised paper should report the matched context-versus-EEG contrasts, uncertainty, all acquisition failures and the limits of reused development panels. Statistical intervals that include zero neither prove equivalence nor demonstrate that EEG contains no affective information. Descriptive participant/title sums of squares do not estimate physiological signal, annotation reliability or irreducible noise. Pairing sensitivity is diagnostic and cannot identify a causal emotional mechanism.

The manuscript's catalogue-title separation does not authenticate unseen physical footage or arbitrary-media generalization. Acquisition group, presentation ordinal and duration are legitimate supplied-study metadata in this operational evaluation, not universal deployment information. Metadata availability must be stated beside the context comparator. Outcome-aware development exploration and remaining neural convergence limitations belong in the main paper, not only in audit appendices.

This positioning makes the current evidence clearer and harder to misinterpret. It does not remove the modest-originality concern or establish acceptance at JMBE. A stronger venue case would require a distinct independently supported finding, qualified stronger baselines and/or a second suitable public dataset. Independent confirmation remains a separate scientific stage; its inactive protocol, unfinished backend qualification and sealed outcomes must not be presented as completed results.
