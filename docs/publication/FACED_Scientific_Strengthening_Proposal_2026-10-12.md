# Scientific strengthening of the FACED study

12 October 2026. **Proposal only: no new fit, rating/waveform access, confirmation opening or research-question change.** The user clarified that the priority is actual scientific content and better prospects at good venues, rather than submission administration. The current exploratory manuscript and its sealed review package remain unchanged.

## Main weakness

The project has a careful, reproducible development evaluation, but not yet a distinctive independently supported finding. In the completed comparison, joint spectral Ridge has mean MAE 1.2156 versus metadata Ridge 1.2577: a reduction of 0.0422 original rating units, approximately 3.35%. Rotation gains are -0.0062, +0.0332, +0.0995 and each descriptive crossed-resampling interval includes zero. The covariance comparison's gain shrinks against the stronger metadata comparator. EEGNet's residual readout does not consistently improve on stronger EEG-free controls, and five of six selected panels continue appreciable source learning near the training cap.

These results establish neither a robust EEG advantage nor a precise bound showing negligible EEG information. They concern reused development outcomes. The existing engineering continuation margin 0.15 is not a justified psychometric or publication threshold. Author-reviewed exploratory reporting remains possible, but better formatting or another encoder alone cannot create the stronger scientific contribution.

Evidence is already public in the [complete result table](../../paper/current_progress_2026-10-12/results_table.json), [neural findings](GRU-XNet_FACED_EEGNet_Diagnostic_Findings_2026-10-11.md) and [scope/precision analysis](GRU-XNet_FACED_Scope_Precision_Plan_2026-10-11.md).

## Recommended sequence

1. **Development-only target/context/pairing diagnosis.** Use the existing permitted development corpus and public summaries to determine what the target variation and context advantage represent. Predeclare a small set of pairing/context controls and any whole-trial coverage control before running them. Preserve the current results as exploratory history.
2. **One bounded baseline qualification.** Address optimization/convergence for one neural recipe under the same target/material contract; qualify any single stronger representation before comparison. Set a fit/time budget and stopping rule in advance. Lack of improving held-person/title evidence stops further encoder expansion; falling training loss alone does not continue the search.
3. **Choose one scientific claim from those findings.** A positive paper requires reproducible incremental EEG benefit and evidence that useful person/trial pairing matters. A rigorous limited-result paper requires sufficiently strong baselines, adequate precision and a justified upper bound for this defined task. Nonsignificance alone is insufficient. A mechanistic shortcut finding would need controls that support its specific explanation.
4. **Present the claim and fixed confirmation proposal for approval.** The old programme has no qualifying positive-method lead and keeps confirmation on hold. A limited-result confirmation would be a revised decision, not an automatic bypass of that gate. Preserve the 33 participants/eight families until the method/comparator/analysis and any research-question adoption are approved.
5. **Confirm and report regardless of direction.** Use the untouched roster once and retain inconclusive or adverse outcomes. Reassess venue fit from the resulting contribution and evidence; no outcome guarantees selection by a good venue.

This sequence prioritizes scientific substance. It does not discard the existing paper, promise a favorable confirmation, activate a new data reservation, silently redefine the target, or start more fitting. It respects public-dataset-only work and the user's approval boundary for adopting a different research question.
