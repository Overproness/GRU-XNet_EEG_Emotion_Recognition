## Choice and authentication

Use **one longer residual EEGNet-8,2 schedule**, rather than expanding an encoder grid. EEGNet has an accessible author implementation, a compact task-independent convolutional backbone, no external pretrained membership dependency and a previously qualified local scalar adaptation. The original work evaluates classification across BCI paradigms, not FACED continuous valence; the proposed model is explicitly a **local regression adaptation**, not a reproduction of a published emotion score. [Original paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.08024v4), [pinned author code](https://github.com/vlawhern/arl-eegmodels/blob/4a512e503198db2010848813ead9afbf8cd54c97/EEGModels.py).

The local author audit pins commit `4a512e503198db2010848813ead9afbf8cd54c97` and EEGModels.py SHA256 `604c7399a2a0e6c90cd8b4cbbc0f78550a3fb5c64b23f311d9c7db44dac94b02`. Its cross-framework manufactured checks authenticate evaluation, dropout-zero training, BatchNorm updates, constraints and flattening order, with maximum forward difference 1.79e-7. Optimizer trajectories, random dropout draws and published scores were not reproduced. The scalar adapter has 1,841 trainable parameters, averages seven window outputs before one trial-level loss and preserves post-update max-norm constraints. The newer generic `gruxnet/eegnet.py` is not the author-checked model used here; use `eegnet_control.py` and `eegnet_regression.py`.

The authors use configurable classifier inputs and Adam. Our 30 channels, 128 Hz, seven four-second windows, scalar head, residual offsets, source-only channel normalization, MSE and AdamW are documented local choices. FACED's independently qualified selective physical-unit decoder, notch/filter/CAR/resampling, bounded artifact interpolation and exact late-window replay supply preprocessing; they are not presented as an EEGNet-author preprocessing recipe. No trial z-score, EOG inclusion, new channel order, augmentation, ICA or tuned quality threshold is introduced.

CBraMod's [author release](https://github.com/wjq-learning/CBraMod) and local pinned weights are available, with documented 200 Hz/microvolt amplitude adaptation and FACED classification evaluation. Its TUEG pretraining is author-documented but actual checkpoint membership/overlap is not independently authenticated. Our earlier CBraMod studies used DEAP binary valence and assigned coarse SEED-IV labels, not this continuous FACED target. Transferring their encoder/head choices would introduce a new input/pretraining/regression qualification programme. Selecting it from earlier validation scores would not supply independent evidence. The existing log-Euclidean covariance diagnostic is also established rather than an authenticated reproduction of one author regression pipeline. EEGNet is chosen for provenance, question compatibility and a specific unresolved optimization limitation, not because it won old validation panels.
