## AI assistance and reproducibility

Springer Nature requires disclosed AI assistance, verifiable outputs and author accountability; AI-assisted figures need caption disclosure. [Current manuscript policy](https://www.springernature.com/gp/policies/editorial-policies/ai-manuscript-preparation).

PLOS requires disclosure of tools, affected research/content and output validation, in Methods, Acknowledgements or relevant figure legends. Its FAQ explicitly requires disclosure for analysis-script/software development. [Current AI policy](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/ethical-publishing-practice). This project used assistance for substantial drafting, analysis-code development, literature qualification and figure-code preparation; a language-polishing-only declaration would misdescribe the work. Human authors must personally review the interpretation, evidence and final text before submission. Automated numerical checks do not substitute for that review.

BMC requires a data/materials availability statement and permits stated access conditions where sharing is restricted. [Research-note data instructions](https://link.springer.com/journal/13104/submission-guidelines/research-note). PLOS requires the minimal supporting dataset and recognises third-party data restrictions when the source, access procedure, restrictions and permission information are supplied. [PLOS data policy](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/data-availability). The package should distinguish original FACED access through its authors' repository from the public anonymous predictions, manifests, numerical results and replay code. A permanent archival identifier should be obtained only for a final approved release; none is claimed yet. Do not promise redistribution of FACED recordings or protected reserve outcomes.
