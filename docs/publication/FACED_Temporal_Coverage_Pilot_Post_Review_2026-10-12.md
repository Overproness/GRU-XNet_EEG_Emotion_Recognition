## Historical QC agreement and serialization

The new private quality files use LF line endings; the prior source-QC files used CRLF. Their direct file hashes differ despite identical numerical content. For each authenticated new late-QC dictionary, the reviewer reconstructed `json.dumps(indent=2)` plus a final newline with historical CRLF encoding. All **eight reconstructed byte strings reproduce the exact prior quality-file SHA-256** from the frozen source lineage.

This establishes full dictionary agreement without opening any additional old private QC file or assuming that a small subset of metrics agrees. It is a representation difference, not a change to quality rules, thresholds or inputs. The public hashes remain their original recorded bytes.

The audit history preserves two stopped parser attempts: an unresolved relative `Path` initially pointed the private probe inside the repository and failed before any private read; a subsequent ledger prototype assumed numeric header column values where the public header stores strings. Explicit absolute-root and integer conversions corrected those assumptions. No producer setting, threshold, identity, interval or raw access was changed, and no incomplete audit receipt was published.

## Scientific disposition

The pilot supports proceeding to a **separately declared complete-source preparation and matched baseline proposal** if that remains the selected next step. It does not itself authorize either. The intended future comparison would hold the inference information and feature dimensionality fixed while comparing the historical late spectral vector with an equal average of early/middle/late vectors. The current worker computes only per-region QC and hashes; no pooled predictor or performance gain has been evaluated.

All twenty-four selected intervals being available does not qualify all **2,940** proposed source ranges. The eight trials were deterministically selected to cover categories and acquisition groups, not to estimate artifact prevalence. Regions can overlap and include only bounded portions of a whole clip. Seven people and eight clips provide no new independent participant/material outcome evidence, learning-convergence result, novelty finding or general guarantee of artifact freedom.

No rating, target, fitting, validation or protected access occurred in the recorded pilot. Those boundaries remain unchanged. The [pre-access review](FACED_Temporal_Coverage_Pilot_Independent_Review_2026-10-12.md), saved exploratory papers and inactive confirmation proposal remain intact. No manuscript claim should be upgraded to independently confirmed performance on the basis of this QC result.
