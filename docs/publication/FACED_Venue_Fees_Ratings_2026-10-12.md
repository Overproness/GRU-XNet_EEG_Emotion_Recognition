BMC's Pakistan-residence tier: 25%, conditionally USD447.50 before tax. [Official pilot](https://www.springernature.com/gp/open-science/policies/journal-policies/apc-pilot).

The [complete ICORE2026 export](https://portal.core.edu.au/conf-ranks/?search=&by=all&source=ICORE2026&do=Export) has 987 rows. Exact IJCNN and FIT entries were checked, and no EMBC, EMBEC or Pakistan ICET match was found. ICORE selects computing venues; it does not comprehensively rate biomedical-engineering meetings. A proceedings publisher, IEEE technical sponsorship or indexing entry is not a tier.

SCImago primary access returned 403. Secondary sites mix SJR2025Q3 and JCRQ2 claims; those are different systems and were not authenticated. No current HEC category was verified. The report therefore uses the publisher's current impact factor rather than guessing a quartile.

My practical assessment is that EMBC provides strong topical specialist fit, IJCNN has the clearest formal computing rank, EMBEC is a credible specialist alternative, FIT/ICET offer more regional exposure, and BMC Research Notes matches the current exploratory manuscript format. These judgments neither establish acceptance probabilities nor repair the scientific limitations documented in the [research-strengthening proposal](FACED_Scientific_Strengthening_Proposal_2026-10-12.md).
