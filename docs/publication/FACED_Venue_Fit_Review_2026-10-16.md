## Practical recommendation

There is no need to wait for a conference. The ordinary journal submission routes below can be prepared independently of a conference call. With a budget of free first and at most PKR 10,000, a journal without a mandatory author charge is preferable to paying for international conference registration and travel.

The current manuscript is a transparent, single-corpus exploratory evaluation of incremental EEG utility, with context/pairing controls and learning-capacity checks. It is not a new classifier or state-of-the-art result. Its main editorial risk is the combination of limited novelty, reused development outcomes, one later neural panel and incomplete comparator/independent replication evidence. Free publication does not remove those scientific requirements. None of the following recommendations predicts acceptance.
