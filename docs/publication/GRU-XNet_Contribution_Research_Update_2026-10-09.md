# Contribution constraints and CBraMod readout review

Checked online on 9 October 2026 while the matched conditioning/dropout grid was still running. This is a targeted primary-source supplement, not an exhaustive review or an adopted research question. Full-grid interpretation waits for completed fitting and independent analysis. The manuscript remains unchanged.

## Additional directly relevant prior work

**Stimulus identity versus emotion already has a close empirical precedent.** Gerster and colleagues' *Stimulus identity rather than emotion drives EEG classification on the FACED dataset* was posted on 16 June 2026. Its primary abstract describes LinearSVC/CLISA controls, assigned versus individual labels, and reducing stimulus diversity. It reports stimulus-identity confounding and offers evaluation recommendations. The abstract was accessible through search; direct full-text routes failed here. It is a preprint, and its numerical methods/results have not been reproduced locally. A generic discovery that stimulus-driven accuracy can exaggerate emotion recognition would overlap it. Our inference is about novelty overlap, not a conclusion that the same mechanism explains our datasets. [Primary abstract](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.06.12.731889v1).

**Source-only versus oracle checkpoint selection is also an explicit existing direction.** The authors' repository for *In Search of Lost Generalization: An Honest Benchmark for Cross-Subject EEG Emotion Recognition* describes auditing target-test-label checkpoint selection on SEED/SEED-IV and comparing nine DA/DG implementations with differential-entropy baselines. It calls the manuscript under review at IEEE Transactions on Affective Computing. No accepted-paper status, full-paper verification or local numerical reproduction is established by this check. Its stated protocol uses pre-extracted DE inputs, participant LOSO and segment-level scores; it does not establish the participant-and-material separation used here. It nevertheless constrains a generic checkpoint-leakage contribution. [Author repository](https://github.com/blaxe05/honest-cross-subject-eeg).

Kong and colleagues' 2 October 2026 preprint already compares familiar-video/time priors, EEG–fNIRS and physiological residual contributions. Its task is continuous regression on familiar content; it explicitly excludes unseen-video transfer. A prior-versus-EEG result alone would overlap it, while different targets or splits still need a substantive new question rather than a new table. [Primary manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.03618v1).

LibEER already provides a standardized seventeen-model/six-dataset benchmark. EEGain provides standardized preprocessing, splits and metrics across six corpora. A smaller generic benchmarking package or corrected split pipeline therefore needs a demonstrable additional contribution. [LibEER primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.09767v3), [EEGain primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18175).

EMOD aligns continuous/discrete emotion annotations in valence–arousal space and uses eight pretraining datasets. Generic label harmonization or flexible joint EEG training also overlaps existing work. Our inference is that a pooled model needs a more specific hypothesis and matched evidence; this does not establish superiority or equivalence to EMOD. [AAAI primary record](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/38796).

## What the CBraMod diagnosis can and cannot settle

The CBraMod paper describes flattened representations followed by multilayer heads for its downstream fine-tuning. The pinned author code also lists an average-pooling linear option, which motivates our deliberately controlled 200-dimensional linear readout. Our tests preserve that readout while varying normalization/dropout. They do not reproduce every published fine-tuning adapter or the published FACED/SEED-V scores. The paper's reported emotion datasets are different from our DEAP/SEED-IV tasks. [Primary CBraMod methods](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.07236v3#A4.SS4), [pinned author model directory](https://github.com/wjq-learning/CBraMod/tree/b9e961003214326972c567eff390e75b0287e32a/models).

Any proposal to test a richer existing readout should authenticate its exact tensor path first, retain an equal-budget pooled control, distinguish dataset/sequence-length adaptations from exact reproduction, and preserve all outcomes. Source-only feature conditioning, optimization, or a multilayer head is not itself a new method.

A new [outcome-free CPU shape audit](../../results/development/cbramod_readout_audit_2026-10-09/verification.json) executes the unchanged pinned head/wrapper files with a synthetic identity backbone. Six standalone average/one-layer/two-layer heads accept their documented FACED/SEED-V token shapes. All three FACED wrapper paths match their standalone heads; all three SEED-V wrapper paths fail after the wrapper flattens the four-dimensional tokens before a head expecting four dimensions. The source files and audit script are hash recorded. This is a narrow wrapper-path finding, not a real-encoder execution, published-score reproduction, or proof that the paper used that faulty version. The present controlled readout implements its own explicit pooling and is unaffected. Keep the author sources unchanged; any future adapted tensor path must be declared and compared directly with the standalone author operators before task fitting.

## Decision constraints

A robust source-panel effect could justify bounded independent-initialization repetition under the same declared training schedule. A training-only gain without consistent validation benefit does not justify a large encoder expansion. These reused panels cannot deliver independent population confirmation, even after additional seeds.

If no robust validation lead emerges, reassess baseline/readout and native-target suitability before investing in additional encoders. A candidate conference contribution still needs a precise hypothesis, a distinction from the work above, controlled evidence across appropriate cohorts, and clear failure criteria. No such distinct contribution is established by this literature update alone. Present the completed grid findings and a concrete proposal to the author before any actual paper pivot. A changed main question requires explicit author approval and an immediate fresh manuscript archive.
