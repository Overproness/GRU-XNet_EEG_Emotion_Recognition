## Dataset and target choice

| Dataset | Role recommended now | Why / unresolved limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **FACED** | Primary candidate: individual retrospective item-10 valence, continuous 0-7; keep the existing non-neutral elicitation population | Qualified local raw/marker/rating joins and explicit ADC calibration; 33 participants and eight source-title families remain reserved. Current evidence is exploratory, with physical footage independence/timing and published derivative provenance unresolved |
| DEAP | Supporting development/sensitivity corpus | Individual valence is coherent, but first-party waveform identity/calibration remains unresolved and all local people/materials have already been evaluated. Another split cannot create untouched confirmation |
| SEED-IV | Separate assigned-category auxiliary task, only if the eventual question needs it | Clip-assigned neutral/sad/fear/happy is not a direct measure of each participant's experienced valence. Native four-class supervision has already been explored |
| GAMEEMO | Bounded game-condition/participant study, outside the proposed primary unfamiliar-film task | Four interactive conditions; the eligible cohort has no positive G3 trial and only one negative G4 trial. Broad both-class unseen-condition folds lack support |
| EmoEEG-MC | Conditional external acquisition/context candidate | Joins, numbered rating semantics and execution endpoints remain blocked on first-party documentation. Shared collection provenance means new people do not automatically imply independent new content |

This retains an already declared target; it changes no labels, thresholds, exclusions or role assignments. FACED's individual ratings concern experience over a whole clip, while the EEG interval is late viewing. The target therefore supports **retrospective trial-level valence prediction**, without establishing moment-to-moment or real-time emotion tracking. Its original methods describe the continuous individual reports. [Original FACED paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-023-02650-w).

The existing eligibility excludes clips 13-16 (the shared neutral family) and globally version-ambiguous clip 22 for every participant. Do not change these after inspecting outcomes. This is a restricted non-neutral elicitation population, not a representative all-valence sample. Retain the original continuous scale; do not try arousal or another rating item merely because valence did not produce a lead. Other items require a separately justified question and declaration.

Confirmation capacity is **33 people x nine clips = 297 potential unfamiliar-film trials**, representing **eight exact-title families**, plus 462 familiar-film trials after a hypothetical locked 90-person/14-film refit. These are metadata counts, not inspected confirmation quality or demonstrated power. The 297 trials and seven windows per trial are not 297 or 2,079 independent stimulus replications. Family-equal weighting must first average the two fear clips belonging to one family. More participants cannot replace missing material diversity.

The [earlier feasibility review](GRU-XNet_Data_Target_Feasibility_2026-10-10.md) supplies the original-corpus target/support audit. A continuous FACED target avoids forcing incompatible binary semantics into one pool; it does not create novelty by itself. No available evidence justifies a main target change to assigned categories, a favorable binary threshold, or a new encoder family.

## Contribution decision and reviewable alternative

**Original joint architecture paper: hold.** A GRU/transformer substitution and pooled accuracy do not support a strong-conference contribution. Retaining this route would require matched full joint and leave-one-dataset-out evidence, source-only choices, native target semantics and an effective intervention with a clear difference from closest methods. Those expensive experiments are conditional on choosing this question; they are not automatically queued.

**Negative-transfer paper: hold.** Some feature-level pooling penalties exist, but the neural target-only comparisons are uncertain and separate-head gains depend on budget. A mitigation paper needs a credible effect against target-only with matched target exposure, then a mechanism/control and independent confirmation. The current FACED single-corpus study does not test cross-dataset negative transfer.

**Evaluation-focused individual-valence paper: the most coherent candidate to assess, with adoption held.** The exact proposed question is:

> For new participants, does EEG improve prediction of individual retrospective valence beyond source-learned elicitation/context information, and does that improvement survive transfer to different source-film families?

The candidate contribution would have to be a new, reproducible finding about **incremental EEG usefulness under the specified information and shifts**, supported by strong matched controls and untouched confirmation. A positive result needs a substantive generalization effect or an effective, explained intervention. A negative result needs sufficiently narrow uncertainty to bound a prespecified useful increment for explicitly tested model families. A package, family split, quality fallback or collection of failed fits is supporting infrastructure, not the central contribution.

The [candidate specification](Contribution_Candidate_2026-10-11.md) makes the comparison, inference information, baselines, uncertainty and stopping criteria concrete. **I do not recommend adopting it yet:** the precise gap from the closest FACED full methods is unresolved, current panels are reused, a strong adapted neural control has not been qualified on this FACED target, and eight held material families do not guarantee the precision needed for a useful null. Preserve the reserve rather than using it to discover whether a story exists.
