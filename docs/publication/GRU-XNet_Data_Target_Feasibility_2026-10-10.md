# Data, target and contribution feasibility decision

Reviewed 10 October 2026, Asia/Karachi. **Stop encoder-grid expansion. The available evidence does not support a conference submission or an adopted replacement question. Proceed with source and annotation qualification before further fitting.** This review completes the requested feasibility stage: a reproducible metadata audit, bounded primary-source checks, a contribution comparison and a concrete next-stage specification. It does not complete the paper.

No EEG waveform, new participant rating row or new model prediction was produced in this stage. The historical joint DEAP/SEED-IV/GAMEEMO question and manuscript remain unchanged. Any actual pivot still requires the author's explicit approval and a fresh archive of the then-current manuscript.

## What the available targets can support

The [metadata findings](../../results/development/data_target_review_2026-10-10/metadata_findings.json) are reconstructed from seven byte-bound, existing public prediction/split files. Probability columns are ignored. The [verification](../../results/development/data_target_review_2026-10-10/verification.json) covers 2,437 eligible original trials and 160 source-preparation invariance checks. The four focused boundary/inventory tests pass. These are metadata and software checks, not new performance evidence.

An additional [local annotation audit](../../results/development/data_target_review_2026-10-10/local_label_verification.json) checks 2,472 source annotation entries against all 2,437 eligible public metadata entries: both DEAP spreadsheet copies, SEED-IV's three README label lists, and the 112 previously extracted GAMEEMO SAM records with all 112 original PDF hashes. DEAP has zero binary-label differences; maximum spreadsheet/CSV rating difference is 4.44e-16 from floating-point serialization. This cross-check requires no waveform loading or new SAM mark extraction and makes no first-party authentication claim. Completed experiments need no label changes.

| Corpus | Available target and population | Suitable claim | Limitation for the proposed paper |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DEAP | Recovered individual valence; 32 people, 40 videos, 1,264 eligible trials: 556 negative/708 positive; 16 midpoint exclusions | Predicting a participant's reported positive/negative valence, with explicit thresholds and observation time | First-party waveform identity/calibration remains unresolved; all declared people/videos have already been evaluated |
| SEED-IV | Native clip-assigned neutral/sad/fear/happy; 15 people, 72 clips, 1,080 trials; 270 per class | Recognizing assigned elicitation categories on new people/content | Available labels do not independently measure each person's experienced valence; native four-class study is already development evidence |
| GAMEEMO | Individual SAM valence; 28 people, four interactive games, 93 eligible trials: 52 negative/41 positive; 19 midpoint exclusions | Participant-reported valence during these game conditions | Four conditions, scarce minority labels and interactive trajectories limit unseen-condition claims; no fresh local participant cohort remains |

For SEED-IV's corrected binary task, sadness and fear map to negative, happiness to positive, and 270 neutral trials are excluded: 810 retained trials. That is a declared coarse interpretation of assigned emotions. Pooling it with individual self-ratings does not establish that all three datasets measure the same target. Dataset-specific heads preserve the distinction but do not themselves provide novelty or solve distribution shift. The official SEED-IV documentation describes 72 clips, 15 participants, three sessions, 62 channels and its 200 Hz feature pipeline; its accessible text does not establish the calibrated unit of our local arrays. [First-party SEED-IV documentation](https://bcmi.sjtu.edu.cn/home/seed/seed-iv.html).

DEAP contains both binary labels on **all 40 videos**. Within the eligible cohort, 263 trials disagree with their video's cohort-majority label; this is a descriptive count computed using all observed labels, **not an admissible predictive baseline or an estimate of disagreement with official stimulus labels**. Another 369 eligible ratings lie within one unit of midpoint 5. Including the 16 excluded midpoint ratings gives 385/1,280 near-midpoint ratings. This motivates retaining the original rating scale and declaring thresholds in advance; it does not justify choosing an easier threshold after inspecting results. A binary target is not intrinsically invalid, and these counts do not establish that label noise explains weak EEG performance.

The native four-emotion SEED-IV target was **already tested** in the [120-run transformer/MLP phase](GRU-XNet_Transformer_Native_Label_Findings_2026-10-06.md). Native four-class transformer BA was 33.95% with absolute and 33.09% with relative features; native versus coarse objective contrasts on common evaluation tasks all had intervals spanning zero. The recent CBraMod readout did not test native four-class supervision, but native supervision is not an unexplored cure for the overall project. No further native-label encoder grid is justified by this review alone.

GAMEEMO's first-party record states that participants supplied SAM arousal/valence after four five-minute game conditions. Its latest displayed release is version 3, dated 9 July 2020; the earlier provenance catalog points to version 1. Version identity must be reconciled before claiming an original-release content match. [Author-hosted GAMEEMO release](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/b3pn4kwpmn/3).

## GAMEEMO needs a different evaluation design

The retained SAM labels have the following condition coverage. Midpoint exclusions are shown separately; they were not changed.

| Game | Negative | Positive | Excluded midpoint |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| G1 | 20 | 3 | 5 |
| G2 | 5 | 18 | 5 |
| G3 | 26 | 0 | 2 |
| G4 | 1 | 20 | 7 |

Of the twelve two-training-game/one-validation-game/one-test-game allocations, six have both classes in every role at whole-cohort level. In an identity-only alphabetical seven-fold participant example, only **six of 84 cells** have both classes in every role, and **no allocation works on all seven folds**. Every allocation and failure is retained; the grouping was not chosen for favorable coverage. With just one negative G4 participant, repartitioning cannot give every disjoint four-person G4 test fold a negative example.

This does not prohibit all unseen-game analysis. Loss and pooled predictions can still be reported with their class support, and participant-independent evaluation across the four observed conditions is a separate possible task. It does prohibit treating every game/fold BA as an equally supported two-class score or copying the forty-video protocol unchanged. G3 cannot estimate positive sensitivity, and four game keys do not supply four identical audiovisual trajectories or a rich sample of independent environments. More windows or augmentations do not add independently rated trials/conditions. GAMEEMO should be a separate secondary task unless the selected question specifically concerns interactive affect.

## Source integrity, physical units and a genuinely reserved population

The existing bundle-to-Kaggle checks remain useful: 304 size/CRC matches and six representative SHA-256 matches. They establish mirror consistency, not first-party waveform identity. DEAP spreadsheet recovery is already implemented; this stage requires **no further relabeling of completed experiments**. The 9 October bounded official-route recheck remains the latest DEAP access evidence; repeating the same unavailable routes adds no authentication. [Provenance record](GRU-XNet_Dataset_Provenance_2026-10-05.md), [latest official-route checks](../../results/development/deap_authentication_recheck_2026-10-09/route_checks.json).

This review retrieved the author-hosted GAMEEMO description but did not obtain a usable first-party file/checksum listing through the bounded public API routes. Some returned HTTP 200 with an empty list or an error object; others returned 404. None is recorded as a successful content comparison. New source records, including failures, are in [source checks](../../results/development/data_target_review_2026-10-10/source_checks.json); their [byte verification](../../results/development/data_target_review_2026-10-10/source_verification.json) retains 27 response checks for 28 attempted retrievals, with full third-party text kept privately.

The CBraMod adaptation follows author-loader numerical microvolt/100 scaling, but independent calibration of local DEAP/SEED-IV arrays is unresolved. Plausible numerical RMS, matching shapes, resampling, z-scoring and healthy source fitting cannot certify acquisition units, channel references, filtering or actual pretrained-checkpoint membership. A future baseline must trace the release/version, decoder, physical dimension/gain, reference, channel order and filtering to source metadata, and test conversion before fitting. A scale-invariant reference is useful as a diagnostic; it cannot authenticate the waveform.

Coverage is now demonstrated directly from already evaluated prediction identities: **all 32 DEAP people/40 videos, 15 SEED-IV people/72 native clips, and 28 GAMEEMO people/four games have been evaluated**. No declared participant/material key in these cohorts is untouched. This refers to dataset-qualified identities; it does not certify biological identity across different corpora. Another initialization, rotation or newly named holdout is development sensitivity, not fresh confirmation.

The older DEAP matched-arm population uses training-participant labels from both arms, including labels on material excluded from the unexposed arm. This was declared, and the existing comparisons remain conditional on that matched population. A stronger sealed-material claim needs a different preparation boundary. The new reference constructor chooses permitted subjects/materials first and retains all their eligible source rows without alternate-arm matching. All **80 prior geometries** retain both classes in all three roles; source sizes are 374–384 trials. Poisoning excluded labels or deleting excluded rows leaves source selection unchanged in **160 checks**. This shows a feasible future preparation rule; it does not replace any historical fit, equalize exposure arms, or make reused cohorts independent.

## External-cohort options checked before acquisition

No new participant rating rows or EEG waveforms were downloaded. Publisher text and public repository/schema metadata were checked; exact retrieved response hashes and revisions are recorded. Metadata access is not full waveform authentication or a verified sample-size guarantee.

| Candidate | Why it is relevant | Current feasibility finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EmoEEG-MC | Author-hosted video and guided-imagery elicitation, individual ratings, 64 channels; a different acquisition cohort | Preferred **qualification candidate**. Paper describes 60 people; the pinned repository lists 103 raw EEG EDF paths across **59** people, with sub-01 behavioral data but no raw EEG path. Several recordings are split across runs. Event annotations need decoding from EDF |
| FACED | 123 people, 28 clips, 32 channels; both assigned categories and individual ratings | Useful secondary option. The public BIDS conversion exposes a rating-column schema; original-release/converted-byte lineage and calibration must still be checked. Close FACED confounding work already constrains novelty |
| DREAMER | Author-described individual valence/arousal/dominance from 23 people | The author Zenodo page currently labels files restricted and requires an access request. It is not an immediately available first-party download here |
| AMIGOS | Individual/group viewing and self-ratings from a larger cohort | Official page describes 40 participants and sixteen short/four long videos; signed request and credentials are required. Download/units/material overlap have not been verified |

EmoEEG-MC's paper describes 21 videos and 21 imagery trials per complete participant and a continuous 0–7 valence scale. It already evaluates within/cross-context decoding. Its video materials include material supplied from FACED, so **FACED and EmoEEG-MC cannot automatically be called independent unseen-content datasets**. Build a stimulus-family identity map before splitting; independently acquired people do not guarantee new content. [Original EmoEEG-MC paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-025-05349-2), [pinned author-hosted dataset documentation](https://github.com/OpenNeuroDatasets/ds005540/blob/83b5b7f28dea9964a5389509548219a27b755cc4/README.md).

The EmoEEG-MC repository is pinned to `83b5b7f28dea9964a5389509548219a27b755cc4`. It documents missing trials for six participants. Its tree contains no separate events.tsv/channels.tsv files; the README describes per-person trigger mappings. One public EEG sidecar declares 1,200 Hz, 64 channels and software filtering 0.5–47 Hz, while the paper/README preprocessing describes 0.1–47 Hz and downsampling to 200 Hz. This is a documentation/representation distinction to resolve, not proof of a defective signal. Header calibration, run stitching and raw-versus-derivative preprocessing must be established before promising a cohort size or preprocessing reproduction.

FACED's original paper describes individual ratings and raw BDFs, with preprocessed 30-second/250 Hz trials. [Original FACED paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-023-02650-w). Curator code at `4c37c73ece79e68702de2d8afa6b9274811a7cc8` documents a unit-header encoding problem in 33 original BDFs and a header-only conversion repair; an uncorrected decoder could yield a factor of one million error. That is a **curator-documented risk, not an independent inspection of those raw files here**. Do not use a generic numerical scale or the upstream recording-unit column as a substitute for checking the actual BDF calibration. [Pinned conversion code](https://github.com/nemarDatasets/nm000112/blob/4c37c73ece79e68702de2d8afa6b9274811a7cc8/code/faced.py).

The current DREAMER access finding comes from the retrieved author record, not an assumed open mirror. [Author Zenodo record](https://zenodo.org/records/546113). AMIGOS's acquisition requirements and study description are stated on its [official dataset page](https://eecs.qmul.ac.uk/mmv/datasets/amigos/). No request form, email, credential operation or access agreement was submitted.

## Closest-work check changes the contribution assessment

An important additional precedent was missing from the earlier review: **mdJPT, NeurIPS 2025 Main Conference**, studies joint emotional-EEG pretraining and cross-dataset generalization. Its official proceedings identify covariance alignment and a hybrid channel/spatiotemporal encoder. The full primary manuscript includes DEAP, SEED-IV and FACED, an imagery-transfer experiment on EmoEEG-MC, and discusses individual emotional experience as an unresolved limitation. Its DEAP median-split target and acquisition/preprocessing differ from our controls; its published scores are not matched local baselines. **Joint training, a transformer/Mamba substitution or imagery transfer alone is therefore insufficient as a new contribution.** This is a novelty-overlap inference; no mdJPT code execution or numerical reproduction was performed. [Official accepted-paper record](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/f1b8d443042f376aa3654b6c68de6297-Abstract-Conference.html), [primary methods and imagery appendix](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.22197v1).

The 2025 *Affective annotation of videos from EEG-based crowdsourcing* paper also studies video annotation and leave-one-video-out settings. A generic unfamiliar-video or context-prior comparison needs to distinguish itself from that question. [Primary published paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10044-025-01476-z). The existing [9 October literature update](GRU-XNet_Contribution_Research_Update_2026-10-09.md) already covers the FACED stimulus-confounding preprint, LibEER/EEGain, EMOD and source-only-selection work. No claim is made that a targeted search proves an unoccupied research gap.

## Concrete continuation decision

**Go:** qualify an author-hosted corpus with individual ratings and a reservable acquisition cohort, starting with EmoEEG-MC metadata/header lineage. **No-go:** another encoder grid, a retrospective confirmatory label, treating GAMEEMO as a forty-video task, or a paper pivot based on this review alone. No fitting is launched.

The next bounded stage should deliver a source/target qualification packet, in this order:

1. Pin the author release and file-object hashes, reconcile the 60-described/59-raw-available distinction, and distinguish raw recordings from published derivatives. Inspect a bounded header/trigger example and the annotation schema before decoding a waveform or reading individual labels. Establish physical dimension/gain, channel reference/order, time origin, run stitching and preprocessing; record any unresolved issue instead of guessing a scale.
2. Map video/imagery IDs and source-content families using stimulus metadata, including FACED overlap. Specify participant and material reservation using identities only, before loading individual outcomes. Keep a qualifying development subset and a separate confirmation cohort; do not inspect reserved ratings during source preparation or tuning. Record baseline/derivative ICA/filtering context so a held-out trial is not accidentally used to fit a source transform.
3. On the declared development subset only, join EEG, trial identity and individual 0–7 ratings; check missingness, both classes and within-material rating variation. Retain the continuous ratings. Decide in advance whether the target is continuous valence, binary self-report or assigned elicitation category; these are separate tasks. Do not tune thresholds from confirmation labels or discard inconvenient conditions after scoring.
4. Present a concrete contribution proposal only if the qualified data and closest-work comparison support it. One **question to assess, not an adopted claim**, is whether a representation trained on elicitation-assigned labels transfers information about individual self-reported valence beyond a source-learned elicitation/content prior, across both new participants and new elicitation material. mdJPT's stated individual-experience limitation motivates examining this distinction; it does not establish that our proposed experiment is novel or feasible. Required evidence would include healthy source-learning controls, authenticated spectral and small neural references, appropriate source-only selection, a matched prior comparison, and a genuinely reserved cohort.

Set explicit gates before fitting: complete identity/unit/annotation joins; an outcome-independent reserve; estimable target support; a documented distinction from the cited individual-label/confounding/harmonization work; and a fixed analysis/budget. If these fail, stop that direction. If they pass, submit the findings, exact question, experiment matrix and failure criteria to the author for approval **before adopting it**. The feasibility review cannot justify claiming negative transfer: that still requires matched target-only versus joint training on aligned tasks, which the readout grids did not test.

Small CPU metadata/header checks fit the current workstation. Any later approved neural diagnostic should retain spectral and random/scratch learning controls, use a measured memory pilot within the RTX 3050's 6 GB, and stay bounded. No full dataset download, GPU schedule or new baseline search is queued by this report. Dataset size, model parameter count or a deeper head does not replace a measured resource estimate.

The [readiness checklist](GRU-XNet_Publication_Readiness_2026-10-05.md) remains open for first-party recording/calibration, actual pretraining membership, a supported contribution, independent confirmation and final manuscript work. Full joint/leave-one-dataset-out and broader architecture/montage/augmentation comparisons are conditional on retaining a question that needs them. Historical 95.91%/confusion-matrix/augmentation claims still need reconciliation, five manuscript source figures remain missing, and no submission-ready PDF or venue selection is complete. The source and existing PDF remain archived and byte-identical.
