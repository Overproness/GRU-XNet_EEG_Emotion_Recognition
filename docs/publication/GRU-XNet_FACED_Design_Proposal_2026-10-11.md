# FACED target, material split and preprocessing proposal

11 October 2026, Asia/Karachi. **A defensible exploratory experiment is feasible: predict each participant's continuous valence rating on new people and held source-film families, and test whether EEG improves on an informed contextual predictor.** This is a proposal for assessing the direction, not an adopted replacement paper question or demonstrated conference contribution. No original rating values or EEG samples were opened, and no model was fitted.

The [recommended machine-readable proposal](../../results/development/faced_design_proposal_2026-10-11/recommended_proposal.json), [evidence index](../../results/development/faced_design_proposal_2026-10-11/README.md) and [execution proposal](FACED_Exploration_Proposal_2026-10-11.md) make the choices reviewable. The manuscript and existing participant reservations remain unchanged. All new material assignments are marked **proposed**, and they authorize no outcome access.

## Proposed target and its scope

Use item 10, the individual **continuous valence rating on its original 0–7 scale**, with MAE as the primary loss/metric. There is no binary threshold or exclusion based on an individual's rating. These are retrospective whole-clip reports, so one trial has one target; they do not supply a continuous second-by-second ground truth.

The primary elicitation population comprises 23 catalogue clips from 22 minimum exact-title source-film families. Exclude clips 13–16 together because they constitute the one neutral source family, and define the claim explicitly for **non-neutral elicitation conditions**. This does not exclude people who individually report neutral, weak or contrary valence. Exclude clip 22 globally because its documented beginning differs across participant groups and actual content alignment remains unauthenticated. Neither exclusion is chosen from performance or numerical rating distributions. The older full catalogue and all excluded outcomes remain preserved/sealed.

| Candidate | Metadata feasibility | Interpretation and tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nine assigned emotions on all clips | All nine classes in source/validation/confirmation film-disjoint sets is impossible | Neutral has one film family and fear two; windows cannot create additional films. |
| Assigned positive/negative valence, excluding neutral clips | Coarse two-class coverage is feasible across material roles | Recognizes the assigned elicitation condition, not each person's subjective valence. Clip-22 alignment still requires a policy. |
| Continuous personal valence on all 28 clips | Regression is structurally feasible | A neutral film cannot be represented independently in all roles; full-neutral-condition generalization would be unsupported. Version alignment remains unresolved. |
| **Recommended continuous valence on 23 clips** | **8 source / 6 validation / 8 confirmation film families** | Preserves individual ratings, avoids the clip-version ambiguity, and supports a precisely limited non-neutral elicitation claim. |

Continuous ratings are recommended for target fidelity and structural feasibility, not because they are known to improve accuracy. Numerical rating variance, missingness, valid vector geometry and usable EEG quality still require checks under a subsequent access declaration. A metadata-feasible design can still fail empirically.

## Concrete proposed split

Keep the previously frozen **70 development-source, 20 development-validation and 33 confirmation people**. Source-title family grouping keeps clips 7 and 8 together. The source-title hashes already reconcile the original workbook with the published supplement; they are a minimum grouping, not authenticated absence of shared footage, franchises or stimulus versions.

For each of eight non-neutral assigned emotion categories, rank film-family hashes using a fixed, public SHA-256 salt. Assign one family to source and one to confirmation; assign the remaining family to validation where a third exists. This gives all eight categories in source and confirmation, but only six in validation: fear and inspiration each have only two eligible families. **Validation cannot directly assess unseen fear/inspiration films.** The regression estimand retains its 0–7 range; no eight-class validation score is assumed.

| Material role, primary development rotation | Families | Catalogue clips | Planned observations |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| Development source | 8 | 3, 4, 9, 11, 17, 20, 23, 27 | 560 training trials from 70 source people |
| Development validation | 6 | 2, 6, 12, 18, 25, 28 | 120 unseen-material validation trials from 20 validation people |
| Confirmation | 8 | 1, 5, **7 and 8**, 10, 19, 21, 24, 26 | 297 unseen-material trials from 33 confirmation people |

The two fear clips contribute one confirmation film-family unit. Source/confirmation each have four positive and four negative *elicitation* families; validation has three of each. This says nothing yet about individual self-rating balance or variance.

Two further metadata-selected source/validation rotations are fixed in the proposal. There are 64 eligible development permutations; the second selected rotation reverses all six switchable categories, and the third changes three. Confirmation film/participant identities remain identical throughout. Fear and inspiration source families cannot rotate into validation while preserving source coverage and the fixed confirmation set. Reused development panels are sensitivity checks, not independent confirmation.

Initial scalers, features, priors and models use only source people × source films. Primary model selection uses validation people × validation films. Familiar-material validation on the same 20 people supplies a separately identified diagnostic. Source people's validation-film outcomes are excluded from initial preparation; all people's confirmation-film outcomes remain sealed. The identity gate never accepts a rating payload, and excluded-row removal leaves the selected source population unchanged.

After development choices are locked, refit on 90 development people × 14 development films: **1,260 trials**. Final confirmation consists of 297 unseen-film and 462 familiar-film trials, giving 759 original trials across the 33 protected people. Confirmation film labels from development people remain sealed; they cannot construct an unseen-film prior or inform source matching. No role is replaced after opening outcomes.

## Proposed preprocessing

The source-authored methods identify CPz as acquisition reference and AFz as ground. The author validation pipeline uses the last 30 seconds, 250 Hz, 0.05–47 Hz filtering, interpolation, ICA and a common average reference. Our minimal proposal differs and must be described as such; it does not reproduce that cleaned derivative or its published scores. The primary method text is available through the pinned BioC copy even where the publisher/PMC browser route fails. [Original FACED paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-023-02650-w).

1. Bind each selected recording/header to the completed raw qualification receipts before decoding. Convert signed ADC counts using the header's physical/digital limits into microvolts. Interpret `?V` only for the 33 independently qualified recordings; preserve the original bytes and do not silently accept arbitrary units.
2. Use the fixed 30 common scalp channels. Rename T3→T7, T4→T8, T5→P7 and T6→P8 explicitly; omit the A1/A2-labelled and HEOR/HEOL-labelled slots in both groups. Auxiliary physical roles are not inferred solely from these names. Source documentation and electrode nomenclature support this mapping. The installed MNE generic alias table maps T5/T6 differently, so automatic alias selection is unsuitable here. [Source-author electrode explanation](https://robertoostenveld.nl/electrode/).
3. Select exactly 32 seconds ending at or before the original video-end marker, using `floor(end_seconds × native_rate)` and a 32-second integer sample span. Retain the central 28 seconds, approximately end−30 to end−2. All **2,829 included original metadata intervals** accommodate this without borrowing another trial's samples. The minimum included marker span is 34.106 seconds. End-marker timing is not measured frame/audio onset latency.
4. Subtract the sample-wise average of these 30 channels. Apply `scipy.signal.butter(N=4, Wn=[0.5, 45], btype='bandpass', output='sos', fs=native_rate)`, forward/backward with odd edge extension, independently inside each selected trial segment. Here N=4 specifies the Butterworth prototype; the realized bandpass has eight poles per pass. Its two-pass magnitude response is squared, with approximately −6 dB at the critical frequencies. This is offline processing with guard periods, not a causal decoder. No global ICA, interpolation or statistics estimated from other films/people enter this minimal recipe. [SciPy filter-order definition](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.signal.butter.html), [filtering considerations](https://mne.tools/stable/auto_tutorials/preprocessing/25_background_filtering.html).
5. Resample to 128 Hz with polyphase anti-alias filtering, Kaiser beta 5 and linear boundary extension. The 28-second core produces seven non-overlapping four-second windows, each 30 × 512. Source-only scalers are fitted later on permitted training trials. [SciPy polyphase resampling](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.signal.resample_poly.html).
6. Preserve one trial-level prediction and weight after aggregating its seven windows. Never score seven copies of the same rating as independent trials. Numerical validity failures must be logged by identity, with a predetermined context-only fallback and coverage reporting for evaluated trials; silently dropping difficult confirmation trials is inadmissible.

The initial early-viewing candidate remains preserved. The source-method supplement replaces its observation choice with late-viewing before any sample/outcome access; all identity assignments are unchanged. [Bound supplement](../../results/development/faced_design_proposal_2026-10-11/preprocessing_plan.json).

The synthetic prototype passes physical endpoint/unit checks, common-reference invariance, channel-order alignment, signal-scale/resampling checks, high-frequency alias suppression and trial-boundary rejection. These tests validate implementation properties, not actual artifact removal. Omitting ICA leaves ocular/muscle contamination possible; predictive success cannot automatically be attributed to neural emotion signals. Current runtime: Python 3.11.13, NumPy 2.4.4, SciPy 1.17.1. No installed package was changed.

## What the experiment would test

The candidate question is: **Does EEG improve prediction of a new person's individual whole-clip valence beyond provided elicitation/material and acquisition context, including on held source-film families?** This does not yet supply a new method or prove a literature gap.

Use source-only global medians, coarse-elicitation medians, fine-elicitation medians and regularized metadata models as contextual controls. Permitted metadata are assigned elicitation category, catalogue duration, presentation ordinal and acquisition group; these are explicitly provided context for an individual-rating target, not the individual's target label. A source-learned video-ID median is legitimate only on familiar videos. On unseen films it must fall back to source-learned metadata/global estimates, never evaluated-film consensus. Acquisition-group reporting is mandatory: the legacy/1,000-Hz subgroup has only 3 source, 1 validation and 2 confirmation people.

Compare context-only, EEG-only and EEG-plus-context with identical trial identities. Start with regularized log-band-power regression; only after source learning/quality checks use a scratch EEGNet-based regression control. A regression adapter requires new implementation verification; the existing author-checked classification port is an architectural starting point, not an already authenticated regression model. No external checkpoint is required, so unknown external pretraining membership is avoided by design. Cross-corpus pretrained controls would require their own compatibility/overlap audit.

For a residual EEG head, construct context predictions for source training rows with participant-excluded cross-fitting; do not feed a consensus containing a row's own target as a training prior. Select checkpoints/regularization on the unseen-material development panel. Do not choose transforms or head families from confirmation outcomes.

The primary endpoint is paired improvement in participant- and film-family-equal MAE on the eight held families, averaging clips within a family first. Report ordinary trial-pooled MAE and MSE secondarily. The proposed continuation threshold is **0.15 units on the 0–7 rating scale**, with a positive lower uncertainty bound. This is an engineering requirement chosen before outcomes, not a validated psychometric minimum important difference.

Two fixed-model pairing diagnostics retain recipient targets/context: exchange EEG among people viewing the same clip within acquisition group; and exchange EEG among different film families within a person and assigned coarse valence. Fix donor rules/seeds before outcome access. These can distinguish useful pairing from shared-context or stable-person shortcuts, but cannot establish causality or eliminate all sensory/artifact explanations. A within-person, same-emotion, different-film control is not supported by the one held family per emotion. Predeclare multiplicity handling if interpreting several contrasts as statistical evidence.

Eight confirmation families limit uncertainty. Hypothetical crossed-effect calculations give approximate 80%-power detectable gains of 0.116, 0.205, 0.347 or 0.578 across four assumed variance settings; these are **not measured variance or empirical power**. Window count does not increase the number of independently held films. A broad confidence interval is inconclusive; it cannot establish that EEG is useless. If the upper interval excludes the 0.15 continuation threshold, a negligible-gain conclusion is better supported for this finite task. Preserve leave-one-family-out and acquisition-group sensitivity summaries.

## Relation to existing work and continuation budget

[Gerster et al., 16 June 2026 preprint](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.06.12.731889v1) already discusses FACED stimulus identity, individual versus assigned labels and temporal splitting. The primary abstract is available; full methods/code remain unavailable through the checked routes. Therefore the candidate cannot claim novelty from those themes, and a detailed method comparison remains necessary before a paper pivot.

[Kong et al., 2 October 2026 manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.03618v1) evaluates video/time priors and small residual physiological gains for familiar videos, explicitly excluding unseen-video transfer. Our trial-level, individual-rating and held-film design differs, but the general contextual-prior idea is already present. Its author manuscript names ACM MM 2026; independent proceedings acceptance is not newly verified here.

[mdJPT, NeurIPS 2025](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/f1b8d443042f376aa3654b6c68de6297-Abstract-Conference.html) already studies joint emotional-EEG pretraining and transfer, including FACED. Its [methods/limitations](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.22197v1) acknowledge individual experience as a limitation. A scratch control, improved split or residual head alone is not a new conference method. These novelty judgements are our inferences from primary sources, not evidence that no narrower contribution exists.

Proceed in small gates:

- **Next technical stage, proposed:** eight trial-local source EEG checks, two source films in four source people (001, 044, 085, 098), spanning all three acquisition groups. The selected clips are 11 and 27, both in the source set. All ratings stay sealed. Verify decoder calibration, boundary handling, signal availability and basic quality; do not launch training from synthetic-test success alone. [Pilot candidate](../../results/development/faced_design_proposal_2026-10-11/source_quality_pilot_candidate.json).
- **Then:** a separate declaration for the exact target item, material identities and source/validation access, with a selective decoder that skips protected participants, films and other rating items before numeric interpretation. Reconcile failures without changing confirmation roles or seeking easier targets.
- **Classical phase:** source-only contextual controls and spectral regression over three declared development rotations. Check learning capacity before interpreting weak transfer; retain negative/zero results.
- **Conditional compact neural phase:** at most 12 base-rotation EEGNet regression fits (two readouts × three learning rates × two initializations), then at most eight selected-setting repetition fits across the other two rotations. Cap at 120 epochs per fit and 12 GPU-hours total on the 6-GB RTX 3050, after a measured memory/time pilot. Stop or document budget truncation explicitly; no transformer/foundation-encoder grid is queued.
- **Paper decision:** show actual source/validation findings and the resolved closest-method comparison to the author before adopting a new question. Archive the then-current manuscript immediately before an approved change. Open final confirmation only after choices and analysis are separately locked.

Fourteen new synthetic/boundary tests pass, the metadata proposal replays, and all 2,829 late intervals pass the metadata geometry check. These complete the requested **proposal preparation**, not the EEG quality pilot, supervised experiment programme, confirmatory evidence or manuscript revision.
