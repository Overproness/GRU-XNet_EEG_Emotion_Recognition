# Material generalization: focused prior-work update

Checked on 6 October 2026 while the predeclared within-session experiment was running. This is a focused protocol audit, not a systematic review or an approved paper pivot. No experimental outcome from the current batch was used to choose its splits, models or contrasts.

## The gap we can investigate

Generalization to a new person does not automatically imply generalization to new stimulus materials. Holding out both has substantial prior work. The current experiment adds matched training sizes, identical test trials and an independent material-disjoint validation set **within each session** to separate this concern from the preceding session-transfer comparison. Those controls make the evidence easier to interpret; their existence alone does not establish a sufficiently novel paper contribution.

## Findings from the primary texts

**EMBC precedent.** Hu and colleagues separate source people/materials from target people/materials. On SEED-IV, they use session separation; film sets and recording sessions therefore change together. Their KPCA arm explicitly fits on concatenated training and test inputs, unlike our source-only scaling. DEAP valence uses a threshold that retains midpoint ratings; our corrected binary target excludes those ratings. These differences prevent a direct numerical leaderboard comparison. [Author paper](https://www.paperhost.org/proceedings/embs/EMBC21/files/1565.pdf).

**Stimulus-aware self-supervision.** GSCL pairs groups of different participants exposed to the same one-second video segment, applies learned shuffling/splitting, and weights contrastive negatives by signal distance. Its inspected split description pools participant data before a 70:15:15 partition, without documenting the participant and original-video grouping required here. This does not establish that the method fails on new stimuli; that would require a matched reproduction. Pretraining/test-population access also needs an explicit implementation audit before using it as an inductive baseline. [Primary full text, sections 1.1, 2.2 and 3.4](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12948548/), [publication record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41760201/).

**Data and label quality.** The data-centric SEED-VII study states that augmentation is confined to training folds and that a trial is represented by one differential-entropy vector. It explicitly acknowledges that EEG responses to the same clip may appear across training and test participants, and calls for clip-disjoint, subject-independent evaluation. Its participant emotion-intensity scores serve as label-reliability information. The current SEED-IV categorical targets instead follow stimulus-assigned classes; they should not be described as individual self-reported valence. [Primary article, evaluation discussion](https://www.mdpi.com/2306-5354/12/11/1264), [author institutional record](https://digital.sandiego.edu/engineering_facpub/46/).

**Transformer precedent and split interpretation.** BRAD-Net constrains attention to predefined brain regions and combines temporal and spectral-spatial pathways. Its subject-dependent split operates on windows. Non-overlap and DataLoader shuffling do not themselves demonstrate that original trials are separated: other windows from one recording can remain on both sides. This is an interpretation of the stated protocol, not a reanalysis proving the size of any resulting bias. The paper also reports LOSO separately; those results should not be conflated with the headline within-person scores. [Primary article, sections 2.2, 3.2 and 4.2](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnins.2026.1810609/full).

**Access in adaptation.** The sample-hybridization study describes multi-source domain adaptation, including unlabeled target data in its cross-subject setup. Its cross-session experiment concerns a subject recorded in different sessions. That evaluates a different deployment setting from excluding target people and fitting on source data alone. Our frozen REVE adapter uses only each observation's normalization statistics and a source-fitted classifier, while complete checkpoint pretraining exclusion remains independently uncertified. [Primary adaptation paper](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/human-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnhum.2024.1464431/full), [our REVE audit and limitations](GRU-XNet_Session_Pretraining_Findings_2026-10-06.md).

The [official SEED-IV design](https://bcmi.sjtu.edu.cn/home/seed/seed-iv.html) provides 72 films, 15 participants and three sessions with 24 trials each. We use session plus original trial position as a material key, supported by that design. Original stimulus files and video hashes are unavailable; material identity is a design-supported proxy, not independently authenticated media identity.

The EMBC 2025 abstract describes transfer between video-induced SEED emotion and an olfactory corpus for positive/neutral/negative targets. It uses both adaptation and generalization approaches, including a transformer DANN. **Abstract-level evidence only:** target-input access, inner validation, participant overlap and original-stimulus grouping were not audited without the full methods. Do not treat its reported accuracy as a protocol-compatible comparator. [Primary abstract and publication record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41335954/).

**Pinned alignment-code audit.** Sera's proposed mechanism combines neural-source separation with coarse-to-fine representation alignment. [Primary ACM paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/3746027.3754775). Five author files were downloaded at commit `ab9eb97b01007cd87c7ba8171f32f463f0474bf9`, hashed and read; no author code was executed. The active LOSO path concatenates source segments and selects inner validation segments by class, rather than excluding validation people/materials. [Pinned split code](https://github.com/JZH98/Sera-code/blob/ab9eb97b01007cd87c7ba8171f32f463f0474bf9/cross_validation.py#L202).

The DA loop consumes test inputs without their class labels for alignment, which is legitimate additional target-covariate access when declared. Separately, it computes labeled test accuracy each epoch and uses that value to save the best checkpoint. This **is test-based selection in the inspected release**, even though validation accuracy is also calculated. A fair independent evaluation must replace it with validation-only selection. This source finding does not establish the exact provenance or bias magnitude of every score in the published paper. [Pinned trainer, adaptation and selection](https://github.com/JZH98/Sera-code/blob/ab9eb97b01007cd87c7ba8171f32f463f0474bf9/trainer.py#L404).

Preprocessing normalizes each participant using all forty trials, including held-out-person observations, before segmenting. That is participant-population calibration, unlike our stateless per-observation REVE adapter. Stored DEAP ratings are used directly with midpoint assigned negative; our corrected mirror target excludes midpoints. [Pinned preprocessing](https://github.com/JZH98/Sera-code/blob/ab9eb97b01007cd87c7ba8171f32f463f0474bf9/prepare_data_DEAP.py#L79). The [source manifest](../../results/development/material_prior_source_audit_2026-10-06/manifest.json) records exact commit, file sizes, SHA-256 and Git blob hashes. These released settings are protocol evidence, not a reproduced Sera baseline.

**Efficient pretraining precedent.** EEG-DLite uses a self-supervised compressor, outlier removal and diversity-based selection to retain a small informative fraction of a large pretraining corpus. The AAAI proceedings identify it as a 2026 conference paper; the inspected full preprint is v1 from December 2025. Its reported reduction to 5% of 2,500 hours concerns large-scale pretraining, not classifier training on this three-corpus bundle. The paper's hardware/runtime results cannot be transferred directly to our laptop. We have not run its distillation or pretraining code. [Primary AAAI proceedings](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/38830), [full author preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.12210v1).

## Decision boundary

A metadata-only [cross-corpus feasibility audit](../../results/development/material_population_audit/feasibility.json) uses the existing corrected binary cohort; it fits no model. DEAP has 1,264 retained observations across 32 people and 40 video identifiers. The loader joins `Participant_id + Experiment_id`, checks dominance/liking against stored data, and uses that video order rather than presentation `Trial`. Labels remain individual ratings: a video does not have one shared binary class. SEED-IV's binary subset has 810 observations and 54 nonneutral session/video keys; the current three-class experiment includes all 72 keys and 1,080 observations.

GAMEEMO has 93 retained observations from 28 people and four game conditions. Negative/positive counts are G1 20/3, G2 5/18, G3 26/0 and G4 1/20. Only six of twelve condition-disjoint two-train/one-validation/one-test assignments contain both classes in **every role at cohort level**. This does not guarantee class coverage in participant-disjoint folds; holding out G3 alone supplies no positive test examples. A condition holdout must therefore report pooled out-of-fold class coverage and cannot silently average one-class fold BA as though it measured both classes. The full [condition counts](../../results/development/material_population_audit/material_label_counts.csv) are retained. Game IDs identify interactive conditions, not identical audiovisual trajectories; a game-condition study is not an exact repeat of the SEED-IV shared-film experiment. [Original GAMEEMO dataset article](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1746809420301075).

Report the whole matched experiment, including unsuccessful models and both uncertainty analyses. A substantial within-session effect would justify investigating robustness mechanisms and replication across corpora. A small or unstable effect would weaken a stimulus-generalization paper direction. Neither outcome licenses adopting a changed research question without the author's approval.

A strong paper still needs a narrow demonstrated gap relative to these studies, a useful method or substantial validated evaluation contribution, matched baselines and evidence beyond repeatedly inspected development folds. Transformers and existing foundation-model probes are controls so far. See the [predeclared matched protocol](Within_Session_Material_Control_Protocol_2026-10-06.md) and [readiness checklist](GRU-XNet_Publication_Readiness_2026-10-05.md). Archive the then-current manuscript immediately before any approved change.
