## Two weaknesses, two different remedies

**Development reuse:** new seeds, windows, models or splits of the already examined participants do not produce independent evidence. FACED still has 33 reserved people and eight reserved catalogue-title families/nine clips. Predictions from a fully frozen source-trained procedure can be committed before those ratings are opened. This can supply a genuine one-time held-out evaluation, conditional on the stated population, information and material roster. It does not undo the earlier adaptive development; that stage remains explicitly reported.

**Limited novelty:** independence does not manufacture originality. The useful prospective contribution must be an independently supported finding or a distinct validated method. The latest [primary-source review](FACED_Improvement_Prior_Work_2026-10-12.md) finds more overlap: recent work already studies participant confounding on DEAP/DREAMER and temporal/peak-end EEG prediction of retrospective affect. Transformers, residual fusion, temporal pooling, subject/stimulus splits and negative findings are established. We will not brand these controls as new algorithms.

The realistic scientific lead is a well-defined result about **incremental EEG utility under available context**, including whether temporal coverage explains the current weak performance, where the result holds and what acquisition failures do to practical prediction. It may be positive, negative or uncertain. A modest, precisely reported held-out result can strengthen the present case study; a broad method or arbitrary-film claim needs more independent materials and preferably a qualified second corpus. No experiment guarantees a better venue or a favorable result.

## Stage 1: one bounded timing-coverage diagnostic

There is a concrete adequacy gap: the outcome rates the whole clip, but the model observes only its late 28 seconds. Marker durations in permitted development metadata range from about 34 to 130 seconds. This mismatch is a plausible explanation to test, not an established cause.

The [prepared source-only preflight](../../results/development/faced_temporal_coverage_preflight_2026-10-12/README.md) computes early/middle/late native ranges for all 980 source trials and exactly reproduces every prior late range. The [bounded pilot protocol](FACED_Temporal_Coverage_Pilot_Protocol_2026-10-12.md) authorizes only eight source trials and twenty-four segments for outcome-blind signal/QC qualification. It keeps the same 32-second operator, channel order and thresholds; no model or outcome is used. Source-quality failures and technical failures are preserved rather than repaired through retrospective scope changes.

If the pilot passes, the next separately declared candidate is late spectral features versus the equally weighted mean of three regions' spectral vectors, each 150-dimensional. Equal dimensionality, fixed contextual information, common source rows and source-only scaling make this a focused coverage comparison. It is distributed sampling, not exhaustive whole-video measurement; overlapped regions and greater preprocessing cost must be reported.

The feasibility review proposes ten source-only person/material cells: five fourteen-person held folds crossed with two material assignments. Each fits 56 source people × eight titles and holds fourteen people × six other titles, while keeping all categories in training. Fear and Inspiration remain training-only singleton categories and cannot be qualified for material transfer. A possible bounded three-alpha/two-representation-plus-matched-context design totals ninety CV Ridge fits. **This supervised stage is proposed, not launched or activated here.** Its exact alpha set, selection, budgets and failure policy must be frozen before source targets are joined. It cannot be reported as independent evidence.

Resolve remaining neural convergence with one matched, source-selected training schedule only if it is necessary for the final claim. Continuing source MSE improvement is a diagnosis, not justification for an unrestricted encoder grid. Neural results remain reported if no further neural study is justified; the conclusion then concerns tested recipes rather than adequately optimized neural affect decoding.

## Stage 2: fix a defensible confirmation purpose

The [independent confirmation audit](FACED_Improvement_Confirmation_Audit_2026-10-12.md) rechecks all current paper/preparation/evidence bindings and gives concrete backend/state/export gates. The old superior-whole-recipe contract stays inactive: its 0.15-unit continuation screen is unmet, precision simulations are hypothetical and the final backend is not implemented. The current manuscript approval does not silently activate it.

Before spending the reservation, prepare a **new versioned proposal** distinguishing two purposes. A matched context-only versus joint spectral comparison can directly test adding spectral features within that fixed recipe, with category medians as the practical benchmark. A fixed-catalogue participant estimand asks about new people under the exact reserved films; a general-film-population estimand requires much stronger material sampling than eight deliberately chosen families. Any change in primary contrast or uncertainty procedure is fixed before access, never chosen from a favorable result. If this amounts to adopting another research question, present the findings and obtain the author's decision first.

The proposed protected rectangle has 297 clip outcomes but only 264 person/title cells. Clips 7/8 share a family and must receive one family weight after clip-level absolute errors. More clips, windows or seeds are not more independent families. Source state/scaler/feature ordering, original-file extraction, exact export serialization and missing-EEG fallback must pass a source/manufactured-data preflight. Complete predictions for every declared method and identity are hash-locked and committed before protected ratings are decoded.

An interval crossing zero is inconclusive about superiority; it cannot establish no useful EEG information or equivalence. A practical negligible-gain claim requires a justified margin and adequate precision, fixed without protected outcomes. The old engineering screen is not such a psychometric margin. The available holdout can strengthen credibility while remaining too imprecise for a broad negative claim.

## Stage 3: one independent public-corpus replication

The strongest external-validity addition is a suitable second corpus, reserved before numerical outcomes. It must provide qualified individual retrospective ratings, participant/material identities and timing/calibration, with a compatible information contract. Retain native scales and report corpus-specific gains rather than pooling incomparable MAEs.

Current choices have real dependencies. Local DEAP people/materials have already been evaluated and first-party waveform authentication remains incomplete, so resplitting them is not fresh confirmation. DREAMER's author-hosted files require research-access approval; AMIGOS has a request/access gate. DENS and EmoEEG-MC execution joins/endpoints are unresolved, and some materials overlap FACED. No new author message, access request or recording download is part of the present work. Qualification can continue when original documentation/access becomes available; no mislabeled corpus is fitted to rescue a novelty claim.
