# Contribution and public-dataset decision after the EEGNet diagnostic

11 October 2026, Asia/Karachi. **Stop the current model-search programme. Retain FACED's continuous individual valence as the best qualified local target, but do not adopt a new paper question or open confirmation from these results.** The author has now specified **public datasets only**; collecting new EEG is outside the proposed route.

The next useful action is a bounded, metadata-first review of a separate public self-report corpus, DENS, while retaining the existing EmoEEG-MC author-response dependency. Neither is selected for fitting or claimed to solve the contribution problem. The [decision options](Contribution_Options_After_EEGNet_2026-10-11.md) and [decision evidence](../../results/development/post_diagnostic_decision_2026-10-11/README.md) make these limits explicit.

## What the new evidence changes

The earlier missing small-neural implementation dependency is resolved: an authenticated scalar EEGNet runner completed twenty fixed fits and sixty checkpoints, with independently checked predictions, source-window/QC replay and public-only numerical replay. A strong predictive neural reference is still unqualified. Independent checkpoint-to-waveform forward replay was outside the saved-state geometry/hash/norm checks.

Five of the six EEGNet readout/rotation panels have continuing-source-learning flags at the fixed 120-epoch limit. Closed-form capacity controls can interpolate both real and shuffled source labels. Neither observation establishes absent biological information, invalid self-reports, a universal model ranking or optimal iterative training. The completed programme supplies no qualifying lead for further encoder expansion; it does not prove a universal null.

The same twenty FACED validation participants and overlapping material panels have been reused. Their rotations are consistency checks, not independent replications. The protected roster has 33 people, nine clips and eight catalogue-title families: 297 potential unfamiliar trials but 264 participant/family cells. Windows do not increase independent stimulus diversity. Existing illustrative precision projections omit final selection/refitting uncertainty and do not justify opening that reserve to find a story.

## Which target and data should remain in scope

**Keep FACED item-10 individual retrospective valence, continuous 0–7, under the existing eligibility and quality rules.** Do not select arousal, new thresholds, neutral inclusion or a category-unavailable track because the present result is weak. Those changes ask different questions and need a prior scientific rationale. Assigned elicitation labels and individual experienced-valence reports remain separate targets.

FACED's qualified scope is offline late EEG, seven nonoverlapping four-second windows from marker end−30 to end−2, predicting a whole-clip report. Its context branch assumes assigned category, acquisition group, catalogue duration and presentation ordinal are supplied at prediction time. Neutral clips 13–16 and globally version-ambiguous clip 22 remain excluded. Transfer separates catalogue source titles, without authenticated physical-footage independence or playback latency. This scope work is complete within those limits; repeating it is not the next priority.

## Why a conference contribution is still missing

The primary-source update confirms substantial overlap. Gerster's FACED work and released code already address stimulus identity and nested cross-video controls. Kong studies context-versus-physiology residuals and explicitly recommends crossed participant/video evaluation. Wang studies shared versus individual affect and unsuccessful feature/model searches. mdJPT already covers multi-dataset emotion pretraining and cross-dataset/imagery transfer. EEG-FM-Compass is now a published foundation-model benchmark. A new split, transformer substitution, wrapper correction or collection of failed fits is insufficient by itself. [FACED preprint](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.06.12.731889v1), [video-time priors](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.03618v1), [shared/individual affect](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02796), [mdJPT proceedings](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/f1b8d443042f376aa3654b6c68de6297-Abstract-Conference.html), [EEG-FM-Compass publisher record](https://academic.oup.com/nsr/advance-article/doi/10.1093/nsr/nwag466/8750569).

The operational candidate remains: **does EEG add prediction of individual retrospective valence beyond permitted elicitation/context information for new participants, and does that increment survive catalogue-title-family transfer?** The review does not establish that this exact question is conclusively answered, or that it constitutes a publishable gap. A strong contribution needs a reproducible, substantive effect/intervention or an adequately precise bounded negative finding about explicitly tested recipes. Current evidence provides neither.

Pretraining membership also matters. MGCRL explicitly describes FACED pretraining; an inspected mdJPT saved configuration includes FACED participants/materials, without an authenticated link to each released checkpoint. A public reserve is not automatically pretraining-unseen. REVE, by contrast, documents removal of downstream-task recordings from pretraining; do not describe it as known FACED contamination. Its exclusion claim still needs checkpoint-specific authentication if used. No new checkpoint is selected or loaded by this review. [MGCRL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04139v2), [pinned mdJPT configuration](https://github.com/ncclab-sustech/mdJPT_nips2025/blob/bb84e89df5d05928ef3aa8645cbc339b59ed59de/log/pretrain/norelu_DATASET_loo_SEEDV.yaml), [REVE primary methods](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.21585v1).

## Concrete next action and stopping rule

Prepare one bounded **DENS metadata/header qualification**: pin the current original release; reconcile EEG/behaviour inventory; identify annotation fields and rating scale without reading rating values; establish material mapping, trial endpoints, channel/reference/physical calibration and release terms. Reserve participant/material roles using identities before outcome access. A prospective SET/MAT reader must selectively skip EEG.data and behavioural values; a generic file loader is not automatically metadata-only. Do not download the full corpus, open protected values or fit a model to answer these metadata questions. If the schema cannot be qualified, record the hold rather than guessing a join.

Only after a public corpus passes these gates should a new exploratory proposal specify its scientific hypothesis, closest-method distinction, information available at inference, source-only selection, bounded learning controls and a justified independent endpoint. Publicly released recordings alone do not supply a research contribution. Existing acquisition-quality policies and declared outcomes remain intact; another identical audit-to-grid cycle on the same panels is not queued.

If no qualified public corpus and distinct feasible hypothesis emerge, preserve this work as a reproducible exploratory project and consider a different research direction with the author. Do not manufacture a conference claim from effort, failed significance or favorable secondary scores. Any actual question change still requires the author's explicit approval and a fresh manuscript archive immediately before adoption.

The original paper remains unchanged and is not submission-ready. Historical metric/augmentation reconciliation, five missing source figures, manuscript rewrite, independent scientific confirmation and final venue/PDF work remain open in the [readiness checklist](GRU-XNet_Publication_Readiness_2026-10-05.md).
