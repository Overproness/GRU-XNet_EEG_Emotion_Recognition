## What can make the paper stronger

The [all-three-target investigation](GRU-XNet_Multitarget_Transfer_Findings_2026-10-05.md) extends the first SEED-IV phase to DEAP/GAMEEMO: all 165 neural runs, 285 selected checkpoints and 42,225 probabilities are checked, with 160 linear candidate refits and 5,954 probabilities reproduced. The suite has 32 tests. This completes the matched feature-level across-target diagnostic, while full architecture, repeated-grouping and unseen-dataset evaluations remain outstanding. Its findings do not justify a general negative-transfer or separate-head method claim. No research-question change follows automatically.

A stronger paper needs a specific research question, a contribution distinguishable from the closest work, and evidence that survives matched controls. High accuracy under an easier split would not repair the novelty or generalization problems. A negative finding can be useful if the study isolates causes and establishes a new, reproducible result across methods/datasets; the current coupled development runs do not yet do that.

Two exploratory tracks are justified before proposing a pivot:

1. **Resolve the learning/generalization failure with matched controls.** Hold model, montage, labels, split, optimizer, and budget fixed while changing normalization; inspect per-participant probability offsets using validation only. Run native-label, native-montage controls to determine whether forced binary pooling is concealing usable dataset-specific structure. These experiments diagnose possibilities, not a newly adopted paper question.
2. **Assess a focused contribution rather than adding another recurrent layer.** Possible directions include reliable transfer under electrode/subject shifts, preserving dataset-native label semantics, or a controlled integrity/leakage study. Montage alignment overlaps UBRRL and pretrained heterogeneous EEG models; coarse/fine label alignment overlaps CIHL; a generic unified benchmark overlaps LibEER and EEGain. Each direction needs a narrower demonstrable gap before it is worth adopting.

Recent LibEER already benchmarks seventeen deep models across six datasets, so a small generic benchmarking pipeline is not by itself a convincing novelty claim. [Primary LibEER paper, revised July 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.09767v3).

For each candidate, report the exact hypothesis, closest prior method, what is new, the controlled experiments, expected resource cost, failure criteria, and measured evidence. Show the author the comparison **before** changing the paper question. If neither the original question nor an alternative has a supported contribution, report that outcome rather than manufacture a positive result.

This is a modest exploratory temporal-model signal. All eight native-minus-coarse objective intervals include zero, and the binary logistic mean exceeds the neural means. No native-label mitigation of pooling harm has been tested: these runs use SEED-IV alone.

All 1,080 regenerated full-trial features and all 810 overlapping retained waveforms agree exactly. Input duration is fixed to avoid direct sequence-length variation; filtering still uses full-trial offline context. A duration-only classifier measures label-duration association without establishing that older models used that shortcut. Holding participants out does not also hold stimulus material out.

The [new primary-source supplement](GRU-XNet_Transformer_Research_Update_2026-10-06.md) adds EEG-Conformer, One Model for All, PESD, an EMBC 2021 material-disjoint study, and a July 2026 evaluation preprint. ## Update: participant/session and frozen pretraining, 6 October 2026

The [new findings](GRU-XNet_Session_Pretraining_Findings_2026-10-06.md) complete 90 source-session neural fits, 30 selected classical controls and 40 frozen REVE/random encoder probes. All 34,560 test probability rows replay. The 160 REVE candidate heads independently refit; selected coefficients and sampled embeddings match exactly. The current suite has 43 passing tests. Hardware feasibility is established for frozen REVE on the author's RTX3050 6GB: about 318 MiB peak CUDA tensor allocation, excluding context/driver memory. Downloaded models/code and embeddings remain local.

Source training and validation see only one SEED-IV session, with participants disjoint across train/validation/test. Models are selected once and evaluated on the same held-out people in all three sessions. Transformer three-class BA falls from 45.64% familiar to 39.90% unseen sessions; its MLP point advantage disappears. The crossed participant/material interval for this drop is [-9.51,-2.21] percentage points, exploratory and unadjusted. The familiar-session transformer-versus-MLP interval includes zero when material uncertainty is included. Sessions change films and recording conditions together; this does not causally isolate video identity.

Frozen pretrained REVE gives 39.14% unseen-session three-class BA versus 33.33% for its frozen random architecture, difference +5.80 pp with crossed interval [+0.62,+11.08]. This shows a useful existing representation under the declared adapter, not a new method or a resolution of session shift. Only one random encoder initialization is used. The adapter narrows bandwidth, normalizes per observation and directly patches 37 seconds within each 40-second observation. The public pretraining-source review is limited: the full checkpoint corpus is not independently authenticated as target-disjoint.

The crossed material analysis was explicitly added after initial session outcomes, before pretrained classifier outcomes. Its 22 paired contrasts resample both people and published-design clip-position keys while holding the three sessions, folds and fitted models fixed. Video hashes are unavailable. An independent verifier resolved tiny softmax/logsumexp rounding amplified in one conditional-binary log loss without changing data, selection, definitions or tolerance; the diagnostic is retained.

These tasks are complete as development controls. **Full repeated grouping/LOSO and LODO, the matched full GRU-XNet architecture ablation suite, first-party DEAP recording authentication, historical training provenance, final novelty assessment and a revised compilable manuscript remain incomplete.** No negative-transfer or new transformer/pretraining contribution is adopted. The next discriminating test should separate people and clips within the same recording session, with matched training sizes and material rotations; a proposed mitigation then needs evidence across corpora and protocol-compatible prior baselines. Obtain explicit author approval and a fresh manuscript archive before adopting any changed research question. The paper is still not ready for submission.

## Update: matched within-session material controls, 6 October 2026

The previously recommended [within-session study](GRU-XNet_Within_Session_Material_Findings_2026-10-06.md) is now complete. It fits 540 neural models and 360 selected classical heads on matched session/participant/material rotations. All selected checkpoints and 21,600 test rows replay; all 1,440 classical candidates independently refit, with exact selected coefficients. The current suite has **47 passing tests**. Both arms train on 108 trials and share the same twelve validation and 24 test trials per fit. Three material rotations and five participant rotations cover every original trial once per model/arm/seed.

Transformer three-class BA is 43.35% for shared versus 39.92% for unseen materials, difference -3.44 pp with crossed participant/material interval [-7.88,+0.56]. All twelve material-exposure intervals include zero when material variability is included. This does not prove equivalence or no material effect. The unseen-material transformer-minus-MLP interval is [-4.26,+2.41] pp; pretrained-minus-random REVE is +6.36 pp with interval [-0.13,+12.90]. No clear new-model advantage or strong material-generalization contribution is established. The earlier session-transfer result cannot be attributed to recording-day shift alone: training/validation size and material access also change between phases.

The [focused primary-source audit](GRU-XNet_Material_Generalization_Research_Update_2026-10-06.md) adds 2026 stimulus-aware SSL, 2025/2026 conference alignment and efficient-pretraining work, plus exact code/target-access qualifications. The released Sera code at a pinned commit uses test inputs for adaptation and labeled test accuracy for checkpoint selection; this is an inspected implementation finding, not an assertion about every published score's provenance. A fair reproduction must use independent validation.

The verified metadata feasibility audit fits no model. DEAP has forty video identifiers with individual rating labels. GAMEEMO has only four interactive game conditions; G3's 26 retained ratings are all negative, and G4 has one negative. Only six of twelve two-train/one-validation/one-test condition assignments contain both classes in every role at cohort level. Participant-disjoint folds require further coverage checks. The three-class SEED-IV study therefore cannot simply be duplicated across both other corpora.

**Do not adopt a paper pivot from these findings.** Repeat independent material/participant groupings and a compatible DEAP binary control before judging a broader robustness direction. Those new experiments have not been run.
