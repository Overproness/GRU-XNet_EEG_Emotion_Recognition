## Compatible DEAP control

Use 1,264 retained trials from all 32 people and all 40 Experiment_id video keys. Spreadsheet valence ratings below five are negative, above five positive and exactly five excluded. Different people can give opposite labels to the same video; do not assign a single emotional class to a DEAP video. Existing first-party signal authentication remains outstanding. Source hashes bind the previously audited Kaggle mirror and corrected spreadsheet labels.

Use the same two participant/material seed pairs. Divide 32 permuted participants into eight groups of four. In fold i, group i is tested, group i+1 validated and the other 24 people trained. Independently permute 40 sorted videos into five blocks of eight. For material rotation r, test block r, validate block r+1, share training block r+2 between arms, and add block r in the exposed arm or block r+3 in the unexposed arm. Block arithmetic is modulo five. Both arms have the same validation and test trials; no validation video enters either training arm. Each retained original trial is tested once per grouping across eight participant folds and five material rotations.

Missing midpoint ratings and individual class preferences can produce different training counts. For every training participant/binary-class pair, retain the smaller available arm count in both arms. Rank eligible training trials by a deterministic SHA-256 digest of grouping, rotation, participant fold, participant ID, label and trial ID; no features, validation or test scores influence retention. Training participant/class counts therefore match exactly. All training people must remain represented, every train/validation/test set must contain both classes, and every test video must remain represented in exposed training after matching. Stop on infeasibility and report it; do not search for a successful seed. Save complete counts before fitting.

DEAP inputs use the same first ten non-overlapping four-second waveform windows and the same 14 electrodes, four Welch bands and 56 absolute log-power features as the SEED-IV neural controls. Check all 32 raw-file hashes, the spreadsheet hash, all 1,264 waveform hashes and window order before creating the new cache. Hash the resulting array/table. This is offline full-trial zero-phase filtering followed by a 40-second crop; it is not causal 40-second acquisition. DEAP baseline samples were already excluded. Train-only scalers fit the selected training observations for each condition.

Use the same MLP and transformer backbones with actual two-class output heads, **18,992 and 19,042 parameters**, respectively. Also fit bandpower logistic and a training-label-only video-prior diagnostic: Laplace-one smoothing of each video's positive fraction, with the global training fraction for unseen videos. The video prior uses no EEG and has no hyperparameter search; it tests what source labels associated with video identity alone can achieve. DEAP frozen-REVE extraction is outside this phase. A duration-only classifier is omitted because all retained DEAP trials have the same duration.

## Uncertainty and limits

Use 10,000 fixed paired participant-only and crossed participant/material percentile draws, seed 20261006. SEED resamples 15 people and six clips within each of twelve session/native-emotion strata. DEAP resamples 32 people and 40 videos without video-emotion stratification because emotional class differs across people. Weighted class numerators and denominators use actual observed person/video cells; excluded midpoint cells remain absent. For combined estimates, average correctness across groupings within each observed cell. Repeated groupings do not increase participant or material sample sizes. Report every declared contrast per grouping and combined; intervals are conditional on fitted models/partitions and exploratory without multiplicity correction.

Independently replay every selected neural checkpoint, source-only scaler, training/validation/test metric, paired sampling stream, initial state and available validation history. Refit all linear candidates and check selected coefficients and probability rows. Check exact once-per-trial coverage, source/configuration hashes and deterministic full analysis recomputation. Derived evidence is exported; raw signals, embeddings, weights and checkpoints remain local.

The comparison changes training video content, order and difficulty along with exposure; it does not isolate a causal identity mechanism. SEED and DEAP have different label semantics, people, recordings and feature distributions, so their scores are not a pooled or unseen-corpus transfer result. This phase establishes no new model or mitigation method. Show complete findings before proposing a changed paper question, require author approval before adopting it, and archive the then-current manuscript immediately before that approved change.
