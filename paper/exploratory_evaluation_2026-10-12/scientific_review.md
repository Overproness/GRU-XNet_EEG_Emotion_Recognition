# Independent scientific and numerical review

Reviewed 12 October 2026, Asia/Karachi. The author has approved the exploratory FACED evaluation framing. This review covers the revised `manuscript.tex`, `supplementary_methods.tex`, references, seven generated tables, public evidence and the separately frozen retrospective matched-context sensitivity. It does not fit models or access original recordings, original rating archives, private feature/model arrays or protected outcomes.

**Assessment:** the revised paper supports a bounded exploratory empirical case study. Its scientific statements agree with the completed evidence and preserve adverse results, development adaptation and unresolved baseline limits. No scientific blocker to presenting this version for author review was identified. This assessment does not establish publisher acceptance or independently confirmed EEG utility.

## Scientific scope and interpretation

| Item | Reviewed disposition |
| --- | --- |
| Question and target | Individual retrospective item-10 valence on 0–7, with no threshold or substitution by assigned category. The late offline EEG interval does not establish momentary or full-video affect prediction. |
| Information available at inference | Category, acquisition group, catalogue duration and presentation order are supplied study metadata. Category prediction uses source participants' ratings only; no evaluated-person rating or held-title rating consensus supplies a predictor. This is a conditional information contract, not a category-free deployment claim. |
| Population and grouping | Seventy source, twenty reused validation and thirty-three reserved people; fourteen development catalogue-title families, eight reserved families. Each rotation has 560 source, 120 unfamiliar-title and 160 familiar-title trials. Source/validation participants are distinct; the three rotations are dependent sensitivity panels. |
| Material scope | Unfamiliar means a catalogue title absent from the source panel. Footage independence, semantic independence and physical playback timing are unauthenticated. Fear and Inspiration have no held-title development examples. Neutral and unresolved-version exclusions restrict the population by metadata. |
| Category-support qualification | The first crossed source-title folds also remove the held categories because each source panel has one film per category. The revised five-person-fold selection retains categories and compares five candidates: three median priors and two Ridge families. It qualifies familiar-source-title prediction for held source people; it does not independently estimate unseen-title generalization. Category and source-film identity remain confounded. |
| Practical comparator | Source-selected category median wins each rotation. Its contrasts with the fixed EEG anchors measure whole-recipe utility: the recipes also differ in contextual representation and predictor form. Source-only comparator selection does not erase earlier validation-based EEG selection or programme-level adaptation. |
| Matched predictive contrast | The separately declared sensitivity fixes alpha 100 and reuses original context-only and joint spectral predictions on the same source/validation identities. Available outputs stay unchanged. Both branches receive the old joint predictor's exact fallback on unavailable EEG. This tests adding spectral features within the fixed regression recipe; it is retrospective and does not identify cerebral or emotion-specific information. |
| Acquisition failures | All fifteen unavailable validation outcomes, including fourteen from one participant, remain scored in every rotation. The matched contrast has exactly zero gain on these failed inputs. Its historical full-context fallback is distinct from the separately inactive prospective category-fallback contract. |
| Pairing controls | Recipient target/context stay fixed. Donors remain in the same validation arm and satisfy availability and title/acquisition or person/coarse-valence constraints. Non-singleton derangements exclude self-donors; singleton/unavailable recipients remain unchanged. Repeat absolute losses are averaged before scoring. These are diagnostic dependencies, not exchangeable permutation tests or causal emotional counterfactuals. |
| Descriptive target variation | Participant/title/additive-residual squared-deviation components are correctly reported. One rating per cell leaves interaction, response style, measurement error and reliability unidentified. These fractions do not supply a neural signal estimate or a prediction floor. |
| Uncertainty and dependence | Rotation-specific 4,000-draw crossed intervals are descriptive and omit adaptive-selection uncertainty. Means do not pool independent replications. An interval including zero cannot establish equivalence, noninferiority or absence of useful EEG information. |
| Neural limitations | Five of six selected panels retain the fixed continuing-source-learning flag. The paper now says convergence remains unresolved rather than claiming proven incomplete learning. It does not rank optimized EEGNet or modern EEG representations. |
| Replay boundary | The source checkpoint check comprises 24 fixture/seed waveform forwards and 48 offset-convention comparisons across six states. This is partial source qualification. Validation neural swaps use cached corrections; neither a complete validation forward replay nor independent scientific confirmation is claimed. |
| Closest work | FACED stimulus-confound, context-prior and shared/individual-response work are acknowledged. Operational target, person/title, metadata and acquisition differences are distinguished from novelty. June Gerster manuscript/August code correspondence is unqualified; no author benchmark has been numerically reproduced. Recent preprints remain identified as preprints. |

The favorable spectral unfamiliar-panel mean gain is small and varies in direction. All six category-comparator unfamiliar intervals and all twenty-four pairing intervals include zero. The familiar EEGNet rotation-2 adverse gain of −0.1852, with descriptive interval [−0.3498, −0.0143], remains reported. This is neither a general negative-transfer result nor evidence of a biological information null.

## Remaining publication limits

No further fitting or original-data access is required to write an honest exploratory paper from these completed results. The category-support lesson, complete denominators and paired controls are useful setting-specific evaluation evidence. They are not a new architecture, a new general validation method or a confirmed general result about EEG emotion recognition.

A materially stronger venue case would need a distinct independently supported finding and adequate baseline/material coverage. More initialization runs or EEG windows on the same films do not supply independent materials. Better authenticated public-data support and/or a separately qualified fixed confirmation could strengthen generalization, but the protected panel stays sealed and its backend/source-state qualification is unfinished. This manuscript approval does not activate it.

Current remaining submission items include author-verified declarations and corresponding-author details, venue-specific reference/preprint policy and final format checks. The source-article ethics procedure must not be presented as an independently obtained institutional exemption for this secondary analysis. The budget-compatible venue route is separate from evidence strength; free publication does not imply easier acceptance.
