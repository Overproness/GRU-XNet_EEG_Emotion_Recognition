# Cover-letter draft for author review

15 October 2026. **Not sent.** Provisional target: Journal of Medical and Biological Engineering, subscription route. Final author consent, declarations, venue and instructions remain to be verified.

Dear Editors,

We submit for consideration our manuscript, “Context Controls and EEG Pairing in Retrospective Valence Prediction: An Exploratory FACED Study,” by Muhammad Muntazar and Muhammad Wasif Shakeel.

The study asks whether tested EEG recipes improve individual retrospective FACED valence prediction beyond supplied elicitation metadata. It combines a category-support audit, acquisition-aware contextual comparisons, constrained EEG-pairing diagnostics, and later source-only coverage and learning-capacity checks. Earlier new-person/title-held validation rotations, a ten-cell spectral source comparison and a separate longer-training EEGNet source cell are reported as distinct adaptive evaluations.

The results support a limited evaluation case study. Earlier spectral gains are small and inconsistent; subsequent source comparisons provide no positive EEG increment over the declared contextual controls. EEGNet fits real and shuffled small-set targets, while continuing source improvement leaves convergence unresolved. We retain adverse results and distinguish these observations from equivalence, absence of affective EEG information, a novel architecture or independent confirmation.

We believe careful baseline information matching and transparent development limitations may interest readers evaluating biomedical signal predictors. Anonymous predictions, code and audit records are public. Original recordings, rating archives and private model arrays are not redistributed. All 33 reserved participants remain sealed. Relevant prior work, including HEART and its unresolved exact target correspondence, is explicitly acknowledged.

The authors will verify author consent, originality/concurrent-submission status, funding, conflicts, contributions, institutional requirements and AI-assistance disclosures before sending. This draft does not certify those facts. We seek the subscription publication route, subject to final verification of its terms.
