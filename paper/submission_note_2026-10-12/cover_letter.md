# Cover-letter draft for BMC Research Notes

12 October 2026. **Draft for author review; not sent.** This describes the intended submission and must be updated to match the final title, declarations, corresponding author and chosen venue.

Dear Editors,

We would like to submit our research note, "EEG beyond elicitation context in retrospective valence prediction: an exploratory FACED study," for consideration in BMC Research Notes.

The study asks what EEG adds to prediction of an individual's retrospective valence rating when elicitation metadata are available to a comparator. Using FACED, we compare metadata controls with spectral, covariance and compact EEGNet regressors under simultaneous participant and catalogue-title separation. A fixed acquisition-failure policy retains all validation outcomes through source-fitted fallback predictions. The supplement reports all fifteen declared conditions and exact preprocessing, selection and learning diagnostics; anonymous development predictions and numerical replay code are public.

The main finding is comparator dependence: context Ridge is a strong baseline, a small average spectral EEG advantage is inconsistent across panels, and the tested EEGNet residual schedules do not consistently improve on stronger EEG-free controls. Continuing source learning and limited materials constrain interpretation. We do not claim a new architecture, a universal absence of affective EEG information or independent confirmation. The development panels reuse participants and overlap materials, and model selection uses development outcomes; the manuscript states these limitations directly. Reserved outcomes remain unopened.

We consider the note potentially suitable for the journal's scope covering additional controls and useful inconclusive findings. Its value is a complete, reproducible empirical comparison with explicit target, inference-information, coverage and selection boundaries. No DENS results or original GRU-XNet accuracy claims are included.
