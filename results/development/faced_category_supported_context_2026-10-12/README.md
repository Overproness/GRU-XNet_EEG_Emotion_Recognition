# Category-supported contextual baseline qualification

Frozen before execution in Git `585af67f2`. This separate adaptive development stage used 156 context Ridge fits and 18 source-prior constructions, 21,840 source-CV rows and 5,880 ordinary validation predictions. No EEG fit, private EEG array, original rating file or raw recording was accessed. Both manuscripts, the first-stage packet and protected confirmation remain preserved.

The five source-person folds retain all eight source films/categories in each training fold. They select for held-person prediction on familiar source films; they do not estimate unseen-title generalization. Category medians win source-only comparator selection in all three rotations. All 1,680 copied EEG anchor rows preserve the preceding prediction, target, availability and fallback field text exactly.

On unfamiliar-film validation, the descriptive mean category-median MAE is 1.2602, spectral joint 1.2156 and EEGNet residual 1.2550. Gains average 0.0446 and 0.0052. All six unfamiliar gain intervals include zero. Familiar neural rotation 2 is worse than category median under its descriptive interval; this adverse result is retained. Rotations reuse people/materials and are not independent replications.

`verification.json` checks frozen identities, hashes and counts. `independent_numerical_audit.json` separately reproduces public source selections, priors, exact anchor reuse and 12 paired crossed intervals. That audit does not refit Ridge or interpret private states. All results remain exploratory; no research question, manuscript or confirmation activation changed.
