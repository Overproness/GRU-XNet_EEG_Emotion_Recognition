Some replication paths tune on reported held-fold outcomes; separately, nested SVM variants are present. These observations describe inspected implementation paths. They do not establish which exact settings generated every published number, and no numerical result is disputed or reproduced here.

Our independent **catalogue-title metadata** reconstruction finds that clip folds separate video IDs but share a title family: The Shining (clips 7/8) crosses two of the three folds; Blue (neutral clips 13/14/15) crosses all three. This is evidence about catalogue-title separation, not authenticated footage identity. The [JSON audit](closest_method_audit.json) records the exact train/test IDs and overlap.

The candidate's operational differences are continuous retrospective valence, new participants together with title-held families, explicit available-metadata context comparisons, source-only transformations, full-denominator acquisition fallback, and untouched confirmation. **Cross-video testing, nested tuning and stimulus-confound diagnosis themselves are already covered by this release.** The operational differences are grounds for a diagnostic, not a demonstrated novel contribution. No paper pivot or classification-to-regression claim of published-score reproduction is justified. Full-paper/code version matching and eventual submission-level literature positioning remain open.
