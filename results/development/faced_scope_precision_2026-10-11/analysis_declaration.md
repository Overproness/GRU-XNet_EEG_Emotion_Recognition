## Precision calculations fixed in advance

For balanced I by J paired-gain matrices, use the additive working model d_ij = mean + participant_i + film_j + residual_ij. Compute the ordinary two-way ANOVA mean squares and untruncated method-of-moments components: participant variance = (MS_participant - MS_residual)/J; film variance = (MS_film - MS_residual)/I; residual variance = MS_residual. Preserve negative estimates. Truncate negative components to zero only for the illustrative planning scenarios, labeling that choice.

Project to 33 participants and eight films using SE = sqrt(v_participant/I + v_film/J + v_residual/(I*J)). Also show 20/33/60/120/1000 participants at eight films; eight/16/32/64 films at 33 participants; and the infinite-participant film floor. Use all-variance inflation factors 1, 2 and 4. Never interpret these projections as validated power or coverage for the protected films: variance estimates come from six reused development films, the additive/exchangeability assumptions are unverified, and model-training/selection uncertainty is omitted.

For orientation only, show a known-variance normal two-sided 5%/80% detectable-effect approximation (z_.975 + z_.8)*SE. Fixed sensitivity margins are 0.05, 0.10, 0.15 and 0.25 on the 0-7 rating scale. The 0.15 engineering margin was used previously; it is not a validated perceptual/clinical threshold. Do not estimate a minimum participant count if the film-variance floor alone exceeds the required SE.

Separately project conditional fixed-roster precision using the observed sample SD of the 20 participant means, divided by sqrt(33), with t_32 for an illustrative 95% half-width. This answers participant generalization over the specific eight-film roster, not generalization over a population of films. No numeric projection determines a paper pivot, opens protected outcomes or licenses a broad absence claim.
