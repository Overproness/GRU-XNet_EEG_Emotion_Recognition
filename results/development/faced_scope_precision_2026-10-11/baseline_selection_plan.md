## Scope and information contract

Predict item-10 retrospective individual whole-clip valence, on 0–7, from the existing marker-defined late interval, in FACED's non-neutral elicitation population. Use all observed rating values. Exclude clips 13–16 and version-ambiguous clip 22 globally. Preserve the 70 source, 20 development-validation and 33 protected participant roles, the three existing development rotations and eight protected catalogue title families. Do not describe this as instantaneous or real-time emotion, coverage of neutral elicitation, or authenticated independent footage.

No-EEG information at inference is the assigned elicitation category, acquisition group, nominal catalogue duration and presentation ordinal. These must actually be available in the conditional benchmark. Category is supplied study metadata, not a test person's self-report. Familiar-video medians use source people only; they have no valid direct lookup for unfamiliar films. Do not use outcomes from source people watching reserved films. Do not add video encoders, unsupervised target-pool normalization, protected-label calibration, new rating dimensions or category-unavailable tracks during this phase.

Use the already-qualified 30-channel alias map, fixed native-rate preprocessing, 128 Hz resampling, seven 4-second windows and trial-level averaging. The input is the last marker-defined 32 seconds; the analyzed core runs from marker-end minus 30 to minus two seconds. Filtering is offline. Marker containment is verified; physical onset latency and footage correspondence are not. Keep the existing acquisition-failure policy: source failures stop a run; inference failures retain the outcome and use the source-context fallback (EEG-only uses a source global median). Keep the full denominator, coverage and acquisition-group tables.

## Bounded development diagnostic, if later activated

This is a fixed qualification budget, not an encoder search. Two neural readouts (EEG-only and source-context residual) × learning rates {0.0003, 0.001, 0.003} × initialization seeds {20261101, 20261102} give **12 fits on rotation 0**. Train each for 120 epochs with AdamW, weight decay 0.01, dropout 0.5, batch eight original trials and no augmentation. Record checkpoints at epochs 30, 60 and 120; all candidates finish the fixed schedule unless numerical failure or the resource cap stops the stage. Apply the qualified spatial/head constraints after each update. No learning-rate scheduler or additional warm starts. Use deterministic seeded trial shuffling and record any CUDA nondeterminism.

Estimate channel mean/SD from training-role waveforms only, with an explicit floor of 1e-6 µV on SD. For EEG-only, subtract the training-role global median and divide the target by seven. For the residual branch, use five participant-fold source-context out-of-fold predictions to form training residual targets, divide by seven, and add the full training-role context prediction at inference. Every residual-producing context fit excludes the residual recipient's participant. Its alpha is the previously recorded rotation-specific context alpha; fit its scaler/prior within each inner training fold. No input, target or learned normalization may be estimated from the validation or confirmation waveforms. Clip final predictions to 0–7 only after window averaging and adding the offset. Score the identical original trials, including fallbacks.

Choose one learning rate/epoch pair **separately for each readout** on rotation 0's 20 × six unfamiliar-family development panel, minimizing participant/family-equal MAE of the two-seed averaged prediction. Ties within 1e-8 choose the earlier epoch, then smaller learning rate. Keep both seeds as a fixed ensemble; do not choose the better seed. Evaluate familiar materials, source-fit learning curves and source-only participant-fold behavior descriptively. The same two chosen schedules transfer unchanged to rotations 1 and 2: two readouts × two seeds × two rotations = **eight further fits**, for **20 neural diagnostic fits total**. Those rotations reuse people/films and are consistency checks, not fresh confirmation.

Do not count epochs/windows as independent trials or a falling training curve as evidence of emotion information. At each saved checkpoint measure source MSE in evaluation mode on complete original trials, before final prediction clipping. Flag a readout as optimization-inconclusive if any loss is nonfinite, epoch-120 source MSE is greater than 0.8 times the zero-residual/median starting-control MSE, epoch-120 source MSE is less than 0.9 times epoch-60 source MSE (budget-limited convergence), the two seeds' unfamiliar-panel MAEs differ by more than 0.15, or the larger epoch-120 source MSE exceeds twice the smaller (denominator floored at 1e-8). These are fixed engineering diagnostics, not validated emotion thresholds. Retain and report failures; do not add schedules to repair a weak validation result within this declaration. An exact runner must implement and verify these thresholds before activation.

Resource ceiling for the diagnostic is six GPU-hours and 20 fits, with a measured dry-run memory ceiling of 5.5 GB. These are caps, not wall-time predictions. If memory exceeds the ceiling, stop to revise and version the batch policy before fitting; if time expires, mark the incomplete panel and choose no candidate. Do not use a partial panel to select a winner. Existing cached anchors are not retrained during this stage.

## Selection and confirmation plan

Development evidence is already adaptive. A promising lead requires finite/qualified learning, a positive gain against the strongest context comparator in each of the three rotations and a mean gain of at least 0.15. This is an engineering screen, not a significance test or a psychometric threshold. Report leave-family and acquisition sensitivity; no outcome-based exclusions or subgroup claims. The current anchors do not pass. If no qualified readout passes, stop expansion and retain the decision hold; do not open protected outcomes to rescue it.

If there is a qualified lead and the question is approved, use the existing 90 development people × 14 development families as the final source pool **after selection**. This planned final refit does not replace any original participant role or convert development scores into confirmation. Freeze five salted participant folds of 18 and seven material folds of two title families. Assign folds by lexicographic SHA-256 of `FACED_final_source_selection_v1_2026-10-11|participant-or-family-ID`, then distribute round-robin. Each of the 35 crossed cells trains on 72 people × 12 families and validates on 18 × two. Never use the other quadrants' labels for that fit. Explicitly retain missing-category source support and global fallback; the Fear/Inspiration singleton development families make some source category cells unsupported.

Select one no-EEG recipe from three source priors plus the eight metadata regularization candidates by MAE of the 1,260 unique held-source participant/family predictions. Ties within 1e-8 prefer global, coarse-valence, category, Ridge, then kernel; within a model prefer larger alpha. This entails **280 regression/kernel fits and 105 prior constructions**. Fold-level transformations and RBF bandwidth are fitted on that fold's source only. These source panels are used for selection; their winning scores are not unbiased external performance estimates.

Keep the neural learning-rate/epoch/ensemble recipe fixed from development. A conditional source qualification may then require 35 cells × two seeds = **70 neural fits**, with source-context residual targets cross-fitted within each cell's 72-person training role. The selected global context recipe was selected on development panels; this diagnostic remains adaptive even though recipient labels are absent from each individual fit. No retuning the neural schedule from these panels. Source qualification plus the final two-seed refit has a separate 24 GPU-hour cap and 72-fit cap; numerical/resource failure leaves confirmation sealed. These budgets are prospective and **not launched**. A source qualification cannot prove transfer to protected families.

Lock exactly one joint recipe and one strongest no-EEG recipe, all preprocessing, seeds, stopping rules and inference metadata before generating confirmation predictions. Archive source models, configuration, source-only scalers/prior, hashes, failed-run ledger and the final hypothesis/analysis specification. No new model may be chosen based on any protected EEG/outcome performance. Check source quality and inference input geometry before accessing targets; if prediction generation or a required source gate fails, stop rather than change handling from the protected outcomes.

Generate and hash predictions for all nominal 33 × nine unfamiliar-film trials (297) and 33 × 14 familiar-film trials (462) **before joining any protected rating**. Keep every trial, including unavailable-EEG fallback. Clips 7 and 8 count as two trials in coverage but one equally weighted family in error/gain. Primary gain is the mean over 33 participant means, each averaging eight family means after within-family clip averaging. Familiar-arm gain, MSE, coverage, per-family errors and acquisition results are secondary.

## Precision and claims

The primary confirmatory estimand would be performance for new participants **on this fixed eight-family roster**, conditional on the declared metadata and acquisition population. For a single locked context/joint contrast, report a two-sided 95% Student-t interval over 33 participant means (32 degrees of freedom), with an explicit approximate independent-participant assumption. Report participant bootstrap sensitivity with 10,000 draws and seed 20261131. Do not choose an interval method by its sign. Unavailable EEG stays in the outcome denominator; a failed branch is not a reason to delete a participant.

A positive lower bound supports incremental prediction for this roster and these pipelines. An upper bound below 0.15 supports a **roster-conditional upper bound at the engineering margin**, not absence of neural emotion or a clinical equivalence claim. An interval crossing zero with upper bound at least 0.15 is inconclusive. Retain fixed sensitivity margins 0.05/0.10/0.15/0.25. One prespecified primary contrast needs no multi-model winner search at confirmation. Any additional inferential contrast requires a declared multiplicity treatment before access; otherwise label it descriptive.

Participant × family resampling, 10,000 draws with seed 20261132, and leave-family sensitivity are descriptive material-transfer checks. With eight fixed, deliberately balanced categories and nonrandomly sampled film families, do not present their intervals as calibrated inference over arbitrary films. Exact title separation cannot prove physical or semantic independence. Known-variance normal planning estimates in this packet omit training/selection uncertainty and depend on unstable six-film components, some negative. The fixed-roster projection uses the observed development six-film participant-mean SD as a proxy, not observed eight-film precision. Even the six-film validation panels omit Fear/Inspiration present in confirmation. No projected detectable gain is promised power for the protected roster.

Two fixed pairing checks may follow the primary prediction freeze: 1,000 participant exchanges within film × acquisition strata (seed 20261133), and 1,000 within-participant exchanges across distinct families within assigned coarse valence (seed 20261134). Exchange only available EEG; retain recipient fallback status and metadata; report strata with no eligible donor and actual exchange coverage. Fine-category cross-family exchanges are structurally unsupported in the protected roster, so do not promise that control. These checks are descriptive changes in prediction error, not causal emotion tests or automatically valid randomization p-values. They must never influence model selection or redefine the primary population.
