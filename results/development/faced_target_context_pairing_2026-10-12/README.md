# Development target, context and EEG pairing diagnosis

Frozen before execution in Git `416cd6170`; completed packet published in `cc44f5094`. Both manuscripts, the historical report and the 33-person/eight-title-family confirmation reservation are preserved. No research question was adopted.

The packet contains 612 Ridge fits, 63 source-prior constructions, 21,840 source-CV rows, 5,880 ordinary validation predictions, 53,760 donor assignments and 107,520 swap predictions. Models and EEG arrays remain private. Anonymous predictions and participant IDs were authorized for publication.

`checkpoint_replay.json` qualifies 24 source fixture/seed forwards and 48 offset checks, not validation forward inference. The validation neural swaps transform authenticated cached raw corrections while retaining recipient context. Source fixtures replay original intervals, calibration, artifact handling and exact QC; cached-source authentication does not amount to a fresh all-980-source waveform replay.

`independent_numerical_audit.json` and `acquisition_summaries.json` add public-only numerical replay and secondary acquisition descriptions. The audit independently verifies all donor seeds, neural correction swaps, selections, priors, target decomposition and 24 crossed loss intervals. It does not refit Ridge, read private spectral arrays/states or recompute switched spectral predictions from original inputs.

The first source-selection contract held out films whose categories consequently also disappeared from training. Its coarse-valence comparator is not a qualified strongest category-informed baseline. The separately frozen category-supported follow-up preserves these outputs and addresses that selection mismatch.

All outcomes are reused development evidence, and the three rotations are dependent. All 24 pairing intervals include zero. No causal, equivalence or independent-confirmation claim is made.
